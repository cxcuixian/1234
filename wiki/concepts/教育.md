# Education

## Definition

Education is a major institutional instrument that supports national development and individual intellectual growth. It includes bilingual approaches that combine English-language and international integration with the preservation of the mother tongue and traditional culture. It also involves sustained investment of time and effort in learning during the era of artificial intelligence.

## Key Characteristics

- Functions as an institution supporting national development.
- Connects international integration and English-language learning with cultural preservation.
- Preserves the mother tongue and traditional culture through bilingual education.
- Encourages continued investment in learning despite advances in artificial intelligence.
- Supports individual intellectual growth and lifelong learning.

## Applications

- Designing bilingual education systems that balance international participation with cultural continuity.
- Developing national education policies and institutions.
- Encouraging lifelong learning and intellectual development in an AI-enabled society.
- Supporting language preservation alongside global communication.
- Improving individual and collective capacity through sustained learning.
