# 收割散户

## Definition

收割散户 describes the extraction of money from retail investors who are less informed, less powerful, or more vulnerable to market narratives. The concept refers to situations in which large funds, market operators, or institutions benefit while ordinary investors purchase inflated or low-quality assets. It is presented as a consequence of profit-centered business models and speculative market behavior.

## Key Characteristics

- Involves an information, power, or resource imbalance between institutional participants and retail investors.
- Often depends on persuasive market narratives, speculation, emotional trading, or herd behavior.
- May transfer wealth from ordinary investors to large funds, market operators, or institutions.
- Is associated with inflated valuations, low-quality assets, and delayed selling decisions.
- Highlights the importance of independent understanding, timely selling, and avoiding emotional attachment to investments.
- Is closely related to business models that prioritize extracting profit from vulnerable market participants.

## Applications

- Analyzing how financial products, promotional strategies, or market narratives may disadvantage retail investors.
- Evaluating whether speculative assets are supported by fundamental value or primarily by investor enthusiasm.
- Developing investment practices based on independent research, a defined [[能力圈]], and disciplined exit decisions.
- Understanding the risks of [[长期思维]] when it becomes an excuse to retain deteriorating or overvalued assets.
- Examining how [[商业模式]] can benefit from retail participation even when individual investors experience losses.
- Identifying situations involving [[垃圾股]] and other low-quality assets that may attract retail speculation.
