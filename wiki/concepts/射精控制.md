# 射精控制

## Definition

射精控制 is a practical method for managing arousal and delaying ejaculation during sexual activity. It treats rapid ejaculation, especially during a first sexual experience, as a response that may be influenced by overstimulation, high sensitivity, sleep deprivation, or nervousness—not as a moral failure. The approach also emphasizes that intercourse is not the only form of sexual satisfaction and that ejaculation does not end responsibility toward a partner.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses slower pacing, pauses, and changes in activity to manage rising arousal.
- Recognizes overstimulation, sensitivity, sleep deprivation, and nervousness as possible contributors to rapid ejaculation.
- Avoids framing rapid ejaculation as a personal or moral failure.
- Encourages continued attention to a partner’s comfort and pleasure if ejaculation occurs.
- Treats sexual satisfaction as broader than penetration or ejaculation alone.
- Works alongside communication, consent, lubrication, and other forms of sexual activity.

## Applications

射精控制 can be applied during first sexual experiences and other situations in which arousal increases rapidly. Practical applications include slowing movement, pausing before ejaculation becomes imminent, switching to another activity, and continuing to support a partner after ejaculation. It can also be used as part of preparation involving [[睡眠和放松]], management of [[表现焦虑]], and attention to [[同意]] and [[润滑]].
