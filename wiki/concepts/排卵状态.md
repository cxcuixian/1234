# Ovulation Status

## Definition

Ovulation status refers to a woman’s reproductive timing in relation to ovulation. It is a factor that affects the possibility of pregnancy after sexual intercourse, including after a single unprotected encounter.

## Key Characteristics

- Pregnancy can occur after one sexual encounter.
- The likelihood of pregnancy varies according to reproductive timing and ovulation status.
- Ovulation status should not be used to assume that unprotected sex is harmless.
- The possibility of pregnancy supports treating first-time sex as potentially risky and using [[安全套]].

## Applications

- Assessing pregnancy risk after sexual intercourse.
- Explaining why a single unprotected encounter may result in pregnancy.
- Supporting safer-sex guidance, including condom use during first-time sex.
- Correcting the misconception that pregnancy is impossible or unlikely during a particular encounter without considering reproductive timing.
