# Personal Knowledge System

## Definition

A personal knowledge system is an internally organized body of understanding developed through thorough mastery of a subject. It emphasizes depth before breadth: one direction should be made fully functional and understood before beginning another. The concept distinguishes principled understanding from merely maintaining projects or imagining solutions without sufficient knowledge.

## Key Characteristics

- Built through complete and systematic mastery of a subject.
- Prioritizes depth before expanding into additional areas.
- Connects theoretical principles with practical execution.
- Uses tools such as [[ABAQUS]] as practical companions in the learning and construction process.
- Distinguishes genuine understanding from project maintenance, speculation, or solution design unsupported by adequate knowledge.
- Develops through sustained focus rather than opportunistic switching between directions.

## Applications

- Structuring a long-term learning plan around mastering one subject at a time.
- Using practical software or projects to test and consolidate theoretical understanding.
- Evaluating whether a proposed solution is grounded in sufficient knowledge of underlying principles.
- Building professional competence through deliberate, focused practice.
- Organizing knowledge so that principles, methods, and applications reinforce one another.
