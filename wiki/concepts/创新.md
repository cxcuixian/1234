# 创新

## Definition

创新 is the process of developing original ideas and selecting meaningful directions, especially when technological tools make implementation increasingly accessible. It emphasizes independent thinking, sound judgment, and the effective use of tools such as AI rather than technical execution alone.

## Key Characteristics

- Values original ideas and independent thinking.
- Focuses on selecting meaningful and high-impact directions.
- Becomes more important as implementation becomes less limiting.
- Benefits from a broad perspective and an understanding of fundamentals.
- Uses AI and other tools to transform ideas into practical results.
- Requires judgment about what is worth pursuing, not merely the ability to build.

## Applications

Innovation can guide product and organizational strategy, research direction, entrepreneurship, education, and technology development. In AI-assisted work, it helps individuals and teams identify valuable problems, evaluate possible solutions, and prioritize ideas that can produce meaningful outcomes.
