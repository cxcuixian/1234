# 资源稀缺

## Definition

资源稀缺 is the condition in which available resources are limited relative to competing uses and human demands. It is a foundational constraint that shapes economic behavior, market exchange, institutional arrangements, and social organization.

## Key Characteristics

- Resources are finite or constrained relative to demand.
- Scarcity creates competition among alternative uses.
- It requires choices, prioritization, and trade-offs.
- It influences prices, production decisions, distribution, and investment.
- It can arise from limited natural resources, production capacity, infrastructure, technology, or access.
- It provides an underlying rationale for exchange, specialization, institutions, and economic coordination.
- In energy and industrial contexts, scarcity can affect electricity costs, green-power deployment, and supply-chain resilience.

## Applications

- **Economic analysis:** Explaining opportunity costs, market exchange, resource allocation, and price formation.
- **Agriculture:** Guiding decisions about land, water, labor, and agricultural inputs.
- **Commerce and industry:** Shaping production capacity, supply chains, procurement, and investment.
- **Energy systems:** Analyzing the availability and cost of electricity, green power, and related infrastructure.
- **Technology manufacturing:** Evaluating constraints involving rare-earth materials, permanent magnets, and industrial inputs.
- **Institutional design:** Understanding why societies develop rules, property arrangements, markets, and legal systems to coordinate limited resources.
- **Public policy:** Supporting decisions about conservation, infrastructure, distribution, and competing social priorities.
