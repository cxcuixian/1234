# Vulva

## Definition

The vulva is the visible external genital region, distinguished from the internal vagina. It includes the mons pubis, labia majora, labia minora, clitoris, urethral opening, vaginal opening, and perineal area.

## Key Characteristics

- Comprises multiple distinct external anatomical structures rather than one undifferentiated area.
- Sensitivity, swelling, color, and moisture can change with arousal.
- Natural anatomical variation is expected.
- Accurate anatomical orientation supports gentler manual and oral stimulation.
- Understanding the structures helps avoid painful or misdirected contact.

## Applications

Knowledge of the vulva supports anatomical literacy, clearer sexual communication, accurate orientation during sexual activity, and safer, more comfortable manual and oral stimulation. It can also help learners distinguish the external vulva from the internal vagina and recognize normal variation in appearance and arousal-related changes.
