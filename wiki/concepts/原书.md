# Original Books

## Definition

Original books refers to reading an author’s original work rather than relying solely on summaries, interpretations, or derivative material. The practice helps align the reader’s understanding with the author’s actual ideas and supports direct evidence, independent understanding, and practical application.

## Key Characteristics

- Prioritizes an author’s original text over secondary interpretations.
- Reduces distortion introduced by summaries, commentary, or incomplete explanations.
- Supports independent comprehension and more accurate understanding.
- Is connected with the challenging process of establishing reliable learning workflows.
- Emphasizes direct engagement with evidence before forming conclusions.

## Applications

- Read primary works when studying an author’s theories, methods, or arguments.
- Compare summaries and interpretations against the original text to identify omissions or distortions.
- Use direct reading as part of a reliable workflow for improving comprehension.
- Apply the author’s ideas independently after understanding them from the original source.
- Combine original-text reading with [[Learning]], [[Cognition and Understanding]], and [[Establishing Workflows]].
