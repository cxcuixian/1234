# 群众体育

## Definition

群众体育 refers to the mass-participation dimension of sport. In the source’s framework, it explains how table tennis can achieve broad social diffusion through low costs, easy accessibility, and modest physical demands. It is positioned in contrast with [[举国体育]] and [[职业体育]] rather than defined as an entirely independent category.

## Key Characteristics

- Emphasizes broad public participation rather than elite or professional performance.
- Benefits from low participation and equipment costs.
- Is accessible in diverse social and physical settings.
- Requires relatively modest physical demands, supporting widespread participation.
- Contributes to the historical development and success of [[乒乓球]].

## Applications

群众体育 can be used to analyze why table tennis spreads widely across communities and social groups. It also provides a framework for comparing mass participation with the national-sports orientation of [[举国体育]] and the specialized performance structure of [[职业体育]].
