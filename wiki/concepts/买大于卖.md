# 买大于卖

## Definition

买大于卖 describes a market condition in which buying pressure exceeds selling pressure. In the source, it is presented as the direct mechanism behind a stock price increase. The concept offers a simple supply-and-demand framework for interpreting price movement and market momentum.

## Key Characteristics

- Indicates that demand for a stock is stronger than the available selling pressure.
- Provides a simple explanation for upward price movement.
- Should be interpreted together with trading volume, which can help indicate the strength of the movement.
- Does not imply that prices will rise indefinitely; investors should not expect perpetual rises or declines.
- Functions as a basic market interpretation rather than a guarantee of future performance.

## Applications

- Explaining why a stock price may increase during a given trading period.
- Interpreting price momentum alongside trading volume.
- Providing a basic supply-and-demand lens for discussing market behavior.
- Evaluating whether an apparent upward movement is supported by meaningful market participation.
