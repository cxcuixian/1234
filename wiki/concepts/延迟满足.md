# 延迟满足

## Definition

延迟满足 is the practice of postponing immediate rewards in favor of larger, more durable, or more valuable future benefits. It is a discipline that supports long-term decision-making by reducing impulsive consumption and limiting dependence on unstable income or speculative gains.

## Key Characteristics

- Prioritizes durable future benefits over immediate gratification.
- Requires resisting impulsive consumption and short-term temptations.
- Supports investment decisions and saving behavior.
- Preserves optionality by retaining resources for future choices.
- Complements [[长期思维]] and the avoidance of seeking quick success.
- Can reduce dependence on unstable income or speculative gains.

## Applications

- **Investment:** Favoring disciplined, long-term allocation over speculative attempts to achieve quick returns.
- **Saving:** Postponing discretionary spending to build financial reserves and future flexibility.
- **Consumption:** Resisting material consumption that provides immediate satisfaction but limited lasting value.
- **Personal planning:** Maintaining resources and options for future opportunities rather than exhausting them for short-term rewards.
