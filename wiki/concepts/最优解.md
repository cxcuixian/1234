# 最优解

## Definition

最优解 is the decision or solution that is best under the current conditions. It requires evaluating options according to the present situation rather than applying an outdated worldview to circumstances that have changed.

## Key Characteristics

- Context-sensitive: the best choice depends on current conditions, constraints, and objectives.
- Dynamic: the optimal solution may change as information, resources, or circumstances change.
- First-principles oriented: it emphasizes understanding the underlying nature of a problem before selecting a solution.
- Resistant to outdated assumptions: it avoids judging present decisions solely through earlier beliefs or frameworks.
- Practical: it focuses on finding an effective path to a desired outcome.

## Applications

- Re-evaluating strategies when market conditions, technologies, or organizational priorities change.
- Selecting solutions by analyzing current constraints rather than relying on established habits.
- Using AI to compare possible approaches and identify effective paths.
- Improving problem-solving by separating the present problem from assumptions inherited from the past.
- Applying [[大局观]] to balance local improvements with broader objectives.
