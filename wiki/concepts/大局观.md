# Broad Perspective

## Definition

Broad Perspective is the ability to understand the overall structure, context, and long-term direction of a subject without becoming overly absorbed in isolated details. It emphasizes studying a discipline’s history, past development, and future possibilities before pursuing detailed research.

## Key Characteristics

- Focuses on overall structure and long-term direction.
- Places details within their broader historical and conceptual context.
- Encourages independent thinking and innovation.
- Helps people evaluate and select useful tools, including AI.
- Prioritizes strategic understanding over excessive detail-oriented research.

## Applications

Broad Perspective can guide learning by encouraging learners to first understand a field’s origins, current state, and possible future. It can also support research planning, strategic decision-making, innovation, and the effective selection of tools such as AI. By maintaining an overall view, individuals can identify useful directions and avoid investing excessive effort in details that do not serve their goals.
