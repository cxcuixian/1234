# Pull-Up

## Definition

A pull-up is a vertical pulling exercise in which a person raises their body toward a bar through coordinated engagement of the back, shoulders, and arms, then lowers under control.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses a full range of motion, including controlled lowering.
- Emphasizes moving the shoulder blades away from the ears.
- Requires attention to where effort is produced and where fatigue is felt.
- May incorporate dead hangs and scapular pull-ups as related components.
- Uses energy expenditure and bodily feedback as cues for refining technique.

## Applications

Pull-ups are used in strength training, calisthenics, physical conditioning, and movement-skill practice. Practitioners can use bodily feedback to adjust shoulder position, muscular engagement, range of motion, and fatigue management.
