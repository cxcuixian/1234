# 处女膜

## Definition

处女膜 is anatomically variable tissue around the vaginal opening. Its appearance, presence, absence, or condition is not a reliable indicator of sexual history, and bleeding during first penetration cannot prove or disprove virginity.

## Key Characteristics

- Anatomically variable tissue located around the vaginal opening.
- Some people bleed during first penetration, while others bleed little or not at all.
- The presence, absence, or amount of blood cannot establish whether someone is a virgin.
- Hymenal appearance or bleeding should not be used to judge a partner or conduct sexual-status testing.
- Myths about the hymen are often reinforced by pornography-derived expectations and moralized ideas about first sex.
- Discussions of the hymen should account for individual variation, consent, comfort, pain, and sexual health.
- Hymenal remnants may appear as a ring-like fold or small pieces of mucosal tissue around the vaginal opening, with substantial variation among individuals.
## Applications

- Sexual-health education about normal anatomical variation and bleeding.
- Correcting misconceptions about [[阴道口]], [[第一次上床]], and virginity.
- Supporting nonjudgmental communication between sexual partners.
- Discouraging invasive or unreliable sexual-status testing.
- Reducing performance anxiety and moralized expectations connected with [[表现焦虑]] and [[场景性勃起困难]].
- Providing context for discussions of [[疼痛]], bleeding, [[前戏]], [[润滑]], and [[安全套]].
