# Practice Partners Imitating Opponents' Playing Styles

## Definition

Practice partners imitating opponents' playing styles is a training method in which practice partners reproduce an opponent's playing style to help athletes prepare for specific competitive matchups.

## Key Characteristics

- Simulates the playing style and tactical patterns of an expected opponent.
- Makes training conditions more representative of anticipated competition.
- Supports tactical adaptation and opponent-specific preparation.
- Can be incorporated into organized athlete development and competitive training programs.
- The available source does not specify implementation procedures or evaluation criteria.

## Applications

This method can be used in competitive table tennis to prepare athletes for known opponents or recurring playing styles. Coaches may use it to improve tactical readiness, expose athletes to unfamiliar patterns, and connect training activities with competition demands.
