# 逆向思考

## Definition

逆向思考 is a decision-making method that examines both desired outcomes and the conditions that could produce failure. In financial decision-making, it asks how much money can be made, what could cause losses, and what actions should follow a loss. This method complements independent thinking and helps reduce impulsive responses to market excitement.

## Key Characteristics

- Considers failure conditions alongside potential gains.
- Examines the consequences and appropriate responses to losses.
- Counters emotional or impulsive reactions to market enthusiasm.
- Recognizes that profitable activities may be boring, painful, and monotonous.
- Complements independent analysis and disciplined decision-making.

## Applications

- Evaluate investments by identifying causes of permanent loss before estimating potential returns.
- Define a response plan for adverse outcomes before committing capital.
- Resist market excitement by testing optimistic assumptions against possible failure scenarios.
- Maintain discipline when profitable activities are repetitive or emotionally unrewarding.
- Improve financial decisions by combining opportunity analysis with downside analysis.
