# Decisive Action on Opportunities

## Definition

Decisive Action on Opportunities is the recommended practice of acting promptly once a worthwhile opportunity has been identified. It combines preparation and patience with timely execution, emphasizing that hesitation should not prevent action after sufficient evaluation.

## Key Characteristics

- Follows preparation and assessment rather than replacing them.
- Encourages timely action when an opportunity is credible and worthwhile.
- Addresses the competitive costs of excessive hesitation.
- Differs from impulsive decisions made without adequate information.
- Helps reduce avoidable failure caused by bias or delayed response.

## Applications

- Acting promptly after evaluating a business, professional, or strategic opportunity.
- Converting preparation into execution when conditions become favorable.
- Establishing decision criteria that distinguish informed action from impulsive behavior.
- Reviewing potential biases before making a time-sensitive decision.
- Balancing patience during uncertainty with commitment once sufficient evidence is available.
