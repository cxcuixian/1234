# Scaling

## Definition

Scaling is the method of organizing activities so they can operate efficiently at a larger scale. It connects practical growth with rationalization, interface design, economies of scale, and productivity improvement.

## Key Characteristics

- Expands output or activity while maintaining operational efficiency.
- Relies on rationalization and standardized interfaces to coordinate larger systems.
- Seeks productivity gains through economies of scale.
- Treats scale as a practical route to higher output rather than only as an abstract economic theory.
- Can guide regional economic development and the creation of new industries.

## Applications

Scaling can be applied to industrial production, organizational processes, service delivery, and the development of new industries. It is also relevant to discussions of [[Hong Kong]]’s economic future, where larger-scale activity may support higher output and improved [[Productivity]].
