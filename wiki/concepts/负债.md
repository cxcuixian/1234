# Debt

## Definition

Debt is the condition of owing money or carrying financial obligations that must be repaid. In the source discussion, it is presented as a burden to avoid and is associated with simple living and restrained consumption. It is contrasted with the productive effects attributed to [[Compound Interest]] and [[Capital]].

## Key Characteristics

- Represents money owed or an obligation requiring repayment.
- Is framed normatively as a financial burden rather than a productive asset.
- Is associated with financial discipline, simple living, and [[Consumption Restraint]].
- Is discussed generally without distinguishing specific types of debt.
- Contrasts with capital and compound growth, which are described as forces that can work continuously for an individual.

## Applications

Debt serves as a consideration in personal financial planning, especially when evaluating borrowing, consumption habits, repayment obligations, and long-term financial discipline. The concept can also be used to discuss the risks of prioritizing immediate consumption over saving and productive investment.
