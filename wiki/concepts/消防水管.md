# 消防水管

## Definition

消防水管 is a metaphor for a high-volume information source that produces a large, potentially noisy flow of material. Unlike the narrower and cleaner [[科研水龙头]], it may provide information that is “muddy” and therefore requires filtering. The concept describes the tradeoff between information quantity and information quality.

## Key Characteristics

- Produces a large volume of information.
- May contain noise, ambiguity, redundancy, or low-quality material.
- Requires a reliable selection and filtering process.
- Can overwhelm a person who consumes information without sufficient judgment.
- Contrasts with the more focused and cleaner flow represented by [[科研水龙头]].
- Highlights the importance of [[认知和理解力]] when evaluating abundant information.

## Applications

- Evaluating high-volume information sources such as feeds, databases, search results, and large collections of research material.
- Warning against consuming more material than one can assess meaningfully.
- Designing information-filtering, curation, and prioritization processes.
- Comparing broad but noisy information access with narrower, higher-quality sources.
- Describing situations in which increased information quantity does not necessarily improve understanding because of [[混浊]].
