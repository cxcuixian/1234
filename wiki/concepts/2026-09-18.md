# 2026-09-18

## Definition

A collection of practical reflections connecting investing, table tennis, personal conduct, relationships, consumption, and self-discipline. It combines market heuristics with observations about sports institutions and direct advice on rationality, persistence, responsibility, and personal improvement.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats declining stocks as potential opportunities while emphasizing defensive protection and risk awareness.
- Examines institutional competition, trading volume, industrial policy, business-model changes, management quality, and the erosion of competitive moats.
- Analyzes table tennis through accessibility, national and professional sports systems, talent selection, tactics, rule changes, and opponent simulation through practice partners.
- Criticizes gambling, envy, broken promises, irrational reactions, long-distance relationships, wasteful spending, and abusive or unreliable behavior.
- Encourages direct experimentation, slower and more rational decision-making, depth over speed, lifelong learning, persistence, mutual achievement, and admitting ignorance or mistakes.

## Applications

- Evaluating investment opportunities while balancing downside protection and competitive-position analysis.
- Understanding how institutions, training systems, rules, and practice methods shape athletic performance.
- Developing more disciplined approaches to consumption, relationships, commitments, and personal conduct.
- Improving judgment by testing assumptions directly, slowing down reactions, and recognizing mistakes.
- Applying persistence and compound improvement to long-term learning and skill development.
