# 全面发展

## Definition

全面发展 (all-round development) is the idea that a person should cultivate capabilities across multiple dimensions rather than relying exclusively on a single strength. It provides a counterpoint to focusing only on areas in which one already performs well, while recognizing that limited time should be devoted to the most valuable work. The concept therefore involves a practical tension between specialization, effective allocation of effort, and broad personal development.

## Key Characteristics

- Encourages the development of capabilities across multiple dimensions.
- Challenges exclusive reliance on existing strengths.
- Requires balancing specialization with broader personal growth.
- Treats time and effort as limited resources that should be allocated according to value.
- Connects personal development with self-assessment and realistic recognition of one’s abilities.
- Can involve tension between productive busyness and genuinely valuable work.
- Is compatible with accepting that one is an ordinary person while still pursuing meaningful improvement.

## Applications

全面发展 can guide education, career planning, and personal development by encouraging people to strengthen complementary abilities alongside their primary expertise. It can also inform decisions about how to divide time between specialization and exploration, how to evaluate personal weaknesses, and how to distinguish valuable effort from activity undertaken merely to remain busy.

In team and organizational settings, the concept may support the development of people with broader capabilities, improving adaptability and reducing dependence on a single narrow skill. However, it does not imply that every capability deserves equal investment; priorities should still be determined by value, opportunity, and available time.
