# Question Quality

## Definition

Question Quality is a standard for evaluating interactions with AI and other advanced tools. It emphasizes that effective tool use depends not only on the tool’s capabilities, but also on asking questions that are reasonable, purposeful, and grounded in an understanding of the problem. It connects AI use with [[Problem-Oriented Learning]] and the systematic reduction of confusion.

## Key Characteristics

- Evaluates whether a question is reasonable, purposeful, and problem-grounded.
- Treats the quality of inquiry as distinct from the power of the tool.
- Helps prevent users from reinforcing existing misconceptions through poorly framed questions.
- Distinguishes polished outputs from genuine insight.
- Supports structured learning and systematic clarification of complex problems.

## Applications

- Designing prompts and questions for AI-assisted research, analysis, and decision-making.
- Evaluating whether an interaction with an advanced tool is likely to produce meaningful insight.
- Identifying when users are using sophisticated outputs to confirm pre-existing assumptions.
- Structuring problem-oriented learning activities.
- Reducing confusion by clarifying the problem before seeking solutions.
