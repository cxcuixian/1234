# 分工

## Definition

分工 is the organization of productive activity into specialized roles performed by different people or groups. It is a major feature of economic organization, connecting specialization and exchange with markets, social scale, and the formation of modern institutions.

## Key Characteristics

- Assigns different productive tasks to different people, groups, or institutions.
- Increases specialization, productivity, and efficiency through repeated practice and differentiated capabilities.
- Depends on exchange and coordination among specialized participants.
- Supports the development of markets and more complex forms of social organization.
- Relates economic activity to broader historical changes, including the development of agriculture and modern enterprise.
- Extends beyond operational task allocation to function as a structural feature of economic and social organization.

## Applications

- Organizing production across specialized occupations, firms, departments, and supply chains.
- Explaining the emergence and expansion of markets through specialization and exchange.
- Analyzing the historical development of agriculture, commerce, and modern enterprise.
- Understanding how economic specialization contributes to larger-scale social coordination and institution building.
- Comparing economic systems through their organization of productive roles and relationships.
