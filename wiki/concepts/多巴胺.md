# 多巴胺

## Definition

多巴胺 (Dopamine) is a neurotransmitter and neuromodulator involved in brain signaling, including systems associated with motivation, reward, movement, cognition, and the effects of various psychiatric medications.

## Key Characteristics

- Acts through several dopamine receptor types, including the D2 receptor.
- Is relevant to both therapeutic effects and adverse effects of some psychiatric medications.
- Dopamine-related mechanisms may be associated with movement-related adverse effects, including extrapyramidal symptoms.
- Its broader medication-related context may also include appetite and metabolic changes.
