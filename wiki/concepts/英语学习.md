# English Learning

## Definition

English Learning is a method of developing direct comprehension in English rather than translating every expression through another language. It emphasizes sustained exposure, daily practice, patience, and acceptance of temporary incomprehension as an accurate reflection of one’s current level.

## Key Characteristics

- Builds understanding directly in English instead of relying on constant translation.
- Emphasizes consistent daily exposure and practice.
- Treats temporary incomprehension as a normal part of the learning process.
- Relies on patience and sustained effort for gradual improvement.
- Reduces unnecessary cognitive intermediaries between English input and understanding.
- Aims to make English comprehension increasingly natural over time.

## Applications

This method can guide reading, listening, vocabulary acquisition, and everyday English interaction. Learners can apply it by maintaining regular exposure to comprehensible English, resisting unnecessary translation, and continuing to engage with material that is slightly beyond their current level.
