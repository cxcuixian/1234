# Neural Network

## Definition

A neural network is a computational model used to identify language patterns and investigate relationships among causes, effects, and human thoughts. It uses internal weights and feedback to adjust its parameters iteratively, enabling AI systems to organize information, infer structure, classify inputs, and support reasoning about human behavior.

## Key Characteristics

- Represents learned relationships through weighted parameters.
- Uses feedback and iterative adjustment to improve its outputs.
- Identifies patterns in language and other structured information.
- Supports classification, inference, and organization of information.
- Can be applied to reasoning about relationships among causes, effects, and human thoughts.
- Connects AI techniques with language learning and behavioral analysis.

## Applications

Neural networks are used in AI systems for language learning, pattern recognition, information organization, classification, and structural inference. They can also support analysis of causal relationships, interpretation of human behavior, and reasoning about how people form or express thoughts.
