# 2026-09-12

## Definition

A collection of observations and arguments concerning corporate trust, public institutions, investment, mathematics, artificial intelligence, productivity, and Hong Kong’s economic position. It combines criticism of organizational and technological practices with practical guidance on learning, investing, problem-solving, and system design.

## Key Characteristics

- Critiques companies and state-owned enterprises for weak credibility, excessive secrecy, obsolete technology, and poor institutional practices.
- Warns against careless use of foreign artificial intelligence tools and overestimating AI’s ability to solve difficult problems.
- Presents mathematics as a source of methods, ideas, and disciplined reasoning rather than merely a subject to memorize.
- Advocates deep, needs-driven learning and the simplification of complex problems.
- Emphasizes clear interfaces, scalable systems, maintainable code, and practical productivity improvements.
- Promotes patient and rational investing, endurance, limited trading, and ownership of durable businesses.
- Connects historical and political themes involving [[江西|Jiangxi]], [[香港|Hong Kong]], reform, corruption, migration, trade, and industrial development.
- Identifies artificial intelligence as a major potential productivity multiplier while maintaining that it does not replace human judgment.
- Uses [[华为|Huawei]], Apple, Nvidia, Google, Yangtze Power, and China Merchants Bank as examples in discussions of technology, business quality, and investment.

## Applications

- Evaluating the credibility, transparency, and technological maturity of companies and public institutions.
- Developing investment principles based on patience, rationality, business durability, and resistance to emotional decision-making.
- Designing learning strategies that focus on fundamental methods and deepen study when practical needs arise.
- Applying simplification and interface design to complex technical or organizational problems.
- Assessing AI-assisted productivity while preserving human review, judgment, and responsibility for maintainable results.
- Interpreting economic and institutional developments related to [[香港|Hong Kong]] and [[江西|Jiangxi]].
