# 超额利润

## Definition

超额利润 denotes profits above ordinary or competitive returns. In the source’s discussion of [[餐饮市场]], the term distinguishes the existence of demand and transactions from the presence of unusually high returns. Market volume alone is therefore not evidence of attractive profitability.

## Key Characteristics

- Exceeds ordinary, normal, or competitive returns.
- Is distinct from revenue, transaction volume, or market demand.
- May be absent even when a market has frequent transactions and substantial activity.
- Serves as an economic limitation on interpreting [[餐饮市场]] attractiveness.
- Requires analysis of competitive conditions, costs, risks, and sustainability rather than market size alone.

## Applications

- Evaluating whether activity in [[餐饮市场]] translates into unusually attractive returns.
- Distinguishing high transaction volume from genuine investment or business profitability.
- Assessing whether competitive pressure has reduced returns toward ordinary levels.
- Interpreting market opportunities alongside concepts such as [[保护费]], [[长期均线]], and [[买入机会]].
- Avoiding conclusions based solely on visible demand or market popularity.
