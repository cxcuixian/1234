# 温水煮青蛙

## Definition

温水煮青蛙 is a metaphor for gradual deterioration that may fail to trigger an immediate response. It describes how people can become accustomed to worsening conditions while continuing to assume that the overall situation remains safe. In the source, the metaphor is applied to the current market environment and is associated with skepticism about 宁德时代’s competitive moat.

## Key Characteristics

- Describes slow-moving deterioration rather than an abrupt crisis.
- Emphasizes delayed recognition and the absence of an immediate response.
- Highlights how adaptation to worsening conditions can reduce perceived risk.
- Connects market perception with uncertainty about the durability of competitive advantages.
- Often functions as a warning against assuming that a stable-looking environment is genuinely safe.

## Applications

- Analyzing market environments in which risks accumulate gradually.
- Evaluating whether a company’s competitive moat can withstand changing conditions.
- Describing investor or organizational complacency during prolonged deterioration.
- Explaining why delayed recognition can make corrective action more difficult.
- Framing discussions about the relationship between perceived stability and hidden risk.
