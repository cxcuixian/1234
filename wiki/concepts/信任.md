# Trust

## Definition

Trust is a relational foundation based on confidence in another person’s agency rather than possession, ownership, or control. It supports relationships grounded in autonomy, respect, and non-coercive requests.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats the other person as an autonomous agent.
- Distinguishes trust from possession and control.
- Supports respect and non-coercive communication.
- Favors guidance over attempts to command or own another person.
- Depends on confidence in the other person’s capacity to choose.

## Applications

Trust can guide interpersonal relationships, education, leadership, counseling, and conflict resolution. In these contexts, it encourages requests and guidance that preserve the other person’s autonomy instead of relying on coercion or control.
