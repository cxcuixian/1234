# Advantage Accumulation

## Definition

Advantage accumulation is the theory that repeated investment in correct actions and durable advantages can produce increasingly strong results over time. It connects persistence, continued input, reliable organizations, and compounding into a single model of cumulative improvement. Advantage accumulation treats long-term progress as a discipline rather than the result of one-time luck or cleverness.

## Key Characteristics

- Results strengthen through repeated, consistent investment.
- Durable advantages can compound across time.
- Persistence and continued input are essential to cumulative improvement.
- The theory applies to personal development, business quality, and investment judgment.
- Reliable systems and organizations provide a foundation for sustained accumulation.
- Long-term outcomes depend more on disciplined direction than on isolated short-term successes.

## Applications

- **Personal development:** Building skills through repeated practice, reflection, and deliberate improvement.
- **Business evaluation:** Assessing whether a company can preserve and expand durable competitive advantages.
- **Investment judgment:** Favoring high-quality businesses and long holding periods that allow compounding to operate.
- **Organizational improvement:** Establishing systems that convert repeated correct actions into increasingly reliable performance.
- **Strategic decision-making:** Selecting actions that create additional advantages instead of merely producing temporary gains.
