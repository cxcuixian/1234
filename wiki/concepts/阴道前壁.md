# Anterior Vaginal Wall

## Definition

The anterior vaginal wall is the vaginal wall oriented toward the lower abdomen. Some people may experience greater sensitivity in parts of this wall, but sensitivity and preference vary considerably.

## Key Characteristics

- It is oriented toward the lower abdomen.
- Sensitivity differs between individuals; it is not a guaranteed pleasure point.
- Some people may experience little sensation or may dislike stimulation there.
- Exploration should be gentle and responsive rather than forceful.
- A curved “come here” motion may be used only when the partner responds positively.
- Communication and ongoing consent are important when exploring it.

## Applications

The anterior vaginal wall may be explored during [[Foreplay]] or [[Finger Penetration]]. Exploration should be guided by [[Consent]], [[Verbal Confirmation]], lubrication, and the partner’s responses. It should not be treated as a guaranteed route to [[Orgasm, Ejaculation, and the G-Spot]].
