# Process Optimization

## Definition

Process optimization is the deliberate improvement of a workflow so that tasks are completed with less waste, delay, or manual effort. It is a practical approach to scaling productivity through more efficient processes, supporting tools, and automation.

## Key Characteristics

- Reduces waste, delays, and unnecessary manual effort.
- Improves operational efficiency through deliberate workflow redesign.
- May require upfront investment during the construction of supporting tools or systems.
- Can generate substantial output once the supporting system is complete.
- Supports the scaling of productivity through automation and repeatable processes.

## Applications

Process optimization can be applied to:

- Designing and refining automated workflows.
- Reducing repetitive manual tasks in project operations.
- Improving project-management systems and procedures.
- Scaling production and service delivery.
- Evaluating whether upfront investment in tools will produce long-term efficiency gains.
