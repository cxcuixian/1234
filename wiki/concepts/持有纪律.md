# Holding Discipline

## Definition

Holding discipline is the method of consistently buying and holding an investment while its underlying thesis remains valid, while retaining the objectivity to exit when the position becomes uncontrolled, poorly understood, or fundamentally unsound.

## Key Characteristics

- Commits to high-quality investments despite short-term disturbances.
- Distinguishes temporary volatility from a genuine deterioration of the investment thesis.
- Avoids treating every difficulty as a reason to sell.
- Requires continued understanding and monitoring of the position.
- Balances persistence with an objective willingness to exit bad investments.
- Recognizes that excessive attachment can be as dangerous as premature selling.

## Applications

- Maintaining a long-term position in [[中国巨石]] when the investment thesis remains valid.
- Managing hesitation when holding [[华能辽电]] or similar investments through uncertainty.
- Preventing [[散户]] investors from escaping every position in response to ordinary disturbances.
- Supporting disciplined use of [[卖出与风险退出]] when a position is no longer understood or controlled.
- Avoiding emotional commitment to [[垃圾股]] merely because they are already held.
- Combining commitment with [[系统化减少混乱性]] in portfolio decision-making.
