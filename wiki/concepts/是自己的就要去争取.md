# 是自己的就要去争取

## Definition

是自己的就要去争取 is a principle of actively pursuing legitimate interests, owed compensation, rewards, or other benefits rather than passively accepting their absence. It emphasizes decisive action when a person has a valid claim, particularly in situations involving acquisition, delayed compensation, or unequal consequences for past actions.

## Key Characteristics

- Encourages people to claim money, rewards, or benefits that are legitimately owed to them.
- Rejects passive acceptance of unfavorable or unequal outcomes.
- Connects personal responsibility with the willingness to act decisively.
- Reinforces effective incentives by ensuring that rightful contributions and outcomes are recognized.
- Applies even when changing circumstances require reassessing earlier decisions or arrangements.

## Applications

- Pursuing delayed compensation or other contractual benefits.
- Seeking a fair share of value created through an acquisition or transaction.
- Requesting rewards that were promised or earned.
- Evaluating whether unequal consequences reflect legitimate differences in responsibility or merely a failure to claim one’s rights.
- Making timely decisions when circumstances change and passive behavior would lead to avoidable losses.
