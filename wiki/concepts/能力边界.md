# 能力边界

## Definition

能力边界 describes the limits of what a person can genuinely understand, evaluate, or accomplish. It emphasizes recognizing the difference between merely knowing names and possessing substantive understanding, while encouraging focused learning and realistic judgments about uncertainty and risk.

## Key Characteristics

- Marks the boundary between genuine competence and superficial familiarity.
- Highlights the risk of confusing knowledge of terminology with actual understanding.
- Is closely related to [[concepts/知识原子化|知识原子化]] and the fragmentation of knowledge into isolated labels or facts.
- Encourages mastering one subject deeply rather than acquiring shallow familiarity with many subjects.
- Supports intellectual humility and more calibrated assessments of uncertainty and risk.

## Applications

- Evaluating whether a person has sufficient expertise to make a judgment or decision.
- Identifying situations where apparent knowledge consists only of recognizing names or terms.
- Setting realistic learning goals by prioritizing depth over excessive breadth.
- Assessing uncertainty and avoiding overconfident predictions beyond one’s competence.
- Managing risk by recognizing when a problem exceeds one’s understanding or practical ability.
