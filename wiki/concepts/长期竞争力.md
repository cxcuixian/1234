# 长期竞争力

## Definition

长期竞争力 (Long-term competitiveness) is the capacity of a company to sustain and strengthen its advantages, economic performance, and market position over an extended period. It is a central criterion for evaluating companies and investments, alongside pricing power, growth, patience, and valuation, rather than relying solely on a low market price.

## Key Characteristics

- Emphasizes durable competitive advantages over short-term market speculation.
- Evaluates whether a company can maintain its position and performance over time.
- Complements the assessment of [[定价权]], growth prospects, patience, and valuation.
- Distinguishes genuine long-term value from apparent cheapness.
- Encourages skepticism toward companies that spend heavily on technology without a sufficiently certain future market position.

## Applications

- Evaluating whether a company merits long-term investment.
- Comparing durable business advantages with temporary price discounts.
- Assessing the investment prospects of technology companies with uncertain future positions.
- Supporting investment decisions that prioritize business quality and durability over short-term speculation.
