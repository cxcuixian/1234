# 2026-09-20

## Definition

A collection of personal observations about decision-making, financial prudence, learning, judgment, and the causes of failure. The notes emphasize avoiding irreversible mistakes, improving reasoning systematically, and using patience, evidence, and interdisciplinary knowledge to increase the likelihood of finding a suitable path to success.

## Key Characteristics

- Critiques reckless, incompetent, and unsafe behavior, including dangerous driving, unnecessary borrowing, excessive spending, substance-related risks, conformity, envy, and pride.
- Advocates patience, lifelong learning, broad interdisciplinary study, and examination of problems from multiple perspectives.
- Promotes probability-based reasoning, accounting, objective facts, and careful judgment.
- Emphasizes avoiding debt and understanding balance sheets, incentives, and financial consequences.
- Treats integrity and personal conduct as standards relevant to financial success.
- Warns against cognitive and behavioral traps that can produce irreversible failure.
- Uses examples involving Microsoft, WPS, and named individuals to illustrate perceived problems with value, pricing, judgment, and conduct.

## Applications

- Evaluating financial decisions, borrowing, spending, and personal solvency.
- Improving decisions through probability, accounting, evidence, and perspective-taking.
- Developing a lifelong learning strategy across multiple disciplines.
- Identifying behavioral risks before they cause irreversible damage.
- Assessing products, pricing, incentives, and organizational conduct.
- Reflecting on personal standards of integrity and long-term success.
