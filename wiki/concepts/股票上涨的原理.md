# 股票上涨的原理

## Definition

股票上涨的原理 is the basic market mechanism in which a stock’s buying demand exceeds its available selling supply, creating upward pressure on its price. This explains a fundamental cause of price increases but is not, by itself, a complete predictive model.

## Key Characteristics

- Links price increases to the relationship between buying demand and selling supply.
- Emphasizes trading volume and liquidity as important conditions for reliable price movement.
- Does not independently predict the timing, magnitude, or duration of an increase.
- Should be combined with trend indicators and disciplined execution.
- Encourages avoiding stocks that lack sufficient trading volume.

## Applications

- Evaluating whether upward price movement is supported by sufficient market participation.
- Screening out stocks with weak liquidity or inadequate trading volume.
- Combining demand-and-supply analysis with [[趋势指标]] when assessing market direction.
- Supporting disciplined timing decisions, including situations related to [[低开抄底]].
- Interpreting [[买大于卖]] as the immediate demand–supply condition behind rising prices.
