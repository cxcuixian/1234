# Professional Sports

## Definition

Professional sports refers to the professional layer of the table tennis ecosystem. It represents the structured, competitive, and public-facing development of the sport alongside national sports and mass sports.

## Key Characteristics

- Organizes table tennis around professional competition.
- Supports talent selection and the development of elite athletes.
- Encourages tactical specialization and advanced competitive training.
- Contributes to athlete star-making and the public visibility of the sport.
- Connects broad participation with a structured competitive system.

## Applications

Professional sports is applied in the organization of elite competitions, the identification and development of high-potential athletes, specialized tactical training, professional career development, and the promotion of prominent athletes. It also helps explain how table tennis develops from a widely accessible activity into an organized competitive and public-facing field.
