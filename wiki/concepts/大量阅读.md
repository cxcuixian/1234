# Extensive Reading

## Definition

Extensive reading is a method of building knowledge and improving judgment through sustained engagement with a broad range of written material. It expands the perspectives and information available to a decision-maker and supports more informed strategic choices.

## Key Characteristics

- Emphasizes breadth and volume of reading across multiple subjects.
- Supports lifelong learning and the development of sound judgment.
- Broadens the range of perspectives available when evaluating problems.
- Complements attention to objective facts, probability, and competition.
- Can include technical, social, and scientific disciplines.

## Applications

Extensive reading can be used to build general knowledge, evaluate decisions from multiple perspectives, identify relevant facts, and reduce the risk of failure in complex or competitive environments. It is especially useful when combined with [[Lifelong Learning]], [[Considering Multiple Perspectives]], [[Objective Facts]], and analysis of [[Failure]].
