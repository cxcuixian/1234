# Human Grasp of Broad Strategic Direction

## Definition

Human grasp of broad strategic direction describes the continuing human responsibility to establish, evaluate, and adjust overarching goals and strategic orientation. Although [[AI]] may raise average performance levels and automate routine execution, it does not eliminate the need for human judgment, independent verification, and responsibility for direction.

## Key Characteristics

- Focuses on broad orientation and strategic judgment rather than routine execution alone.
- Requires humans to determine objectives, priorities, and acceptable trade-offs.
- Remains necessary because [[AI]] can produce false or misleading conclusions.
- Depends on independent checking and [[Independent Thinking]].
- Connects decision-making with responsibility for consequences.
- Complements the use of AI by defining where and how AI-generated outputs should be applied.

## Applications

- Setting organizational strategy when AI is used for analysis or operational support.
- Evaluating whether AI-generated recommendations align with long-term objectives.
- Checking important conclusions independently before making consequential decisions.
- Determining priorities in areas such as product development, investment, production, and customer service.
- Preserving human accountability when automated systems influence decisions.
- Combining broad human judgment with AI-assisted execution and analysis.
