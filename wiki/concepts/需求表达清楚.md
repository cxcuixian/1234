# Clear articulation of requirements

## Definition

Clear articulation of requirements is the practice of expressing needs, goals, constraints, and expected outcomes precisely before seeking assistance or building a solution. It serves as a bridge between a problem and its practical implementation.

## Key Characteristics

- Defines the desired outcome before selecting or developing a solution.
- Communicates needs, constraints, and context in a precise and understandable way.
- Reduces ambiguity, rework, and misaligned assistance.
- Supports effective problem solving and practical execution.
- Is especially valuable in AI-assisted work, software development, and entrepreneurship.

## Applications

- Writing precise prompts and task specifications for AI systems.
- Preparing software requirements, feature descriptions, and acceptance criteria.
- Communicating business needs to developers, consultants, or collaborators.
- Clarifying customer problems before designing products or services.
- Translating abstract goals into actionable workflows and deliverables.
