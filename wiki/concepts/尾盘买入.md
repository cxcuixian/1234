# 尾盘买入

## Definition

尾盘买入 is a trading method that involves purchasing a stock near the end of the trading session. It emphasizes waiting for additional market information and requires the stock to have stabilized above a long-term moving average before committing capital.

## Key Characteristics

- Takes place near the end of the trading session.
- Favors confirmation over impulsive entry.
- Uses stabilization above [[长期均线]] as a prerequisite.
- Reflects a patient and technically informed approach to identifying a [[买入机会]].
- Seeks to reduce uncertainty by waiting for more information before investing.

## Applications

尾盘买入 can be applied when a trader wants to defer entry until late-session price behavior provides greater confirmation. It may be considered after a stock has stabilized above a [[长期均线]], particularly when distinguishing a confirmed setup from a temporary move or a potential [[破线]] event. The method is part of a broader approach that values [[耐心]] and technical confirmation over immediate participation.
