# Todo法

## Definition

Todo法 is a simple productivity method for organizing tasks through straightforward numerical categorization. Its value lies in providing a practical structure with few moving parts, making it easier to understand and use consistently.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses numerical categorization to structure tasks.
- Emphasizes simplicity and ease of consistent adoption.
- Avoids unnecessary complexity in task organization.
- Illustrates how a method can appear simple while requiring considerable implementation discipline.
- Reflects the observation associated with [[芒格]] that simple methods may be complex to apply in practice.

## Applications

Todo法 can be used to organize personal tasks, prioritize work, and create a lightweight daily planning system. It may be especially useful when elaborate productivity systems create excessive maintenance or reduce consistent use.
