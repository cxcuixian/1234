# 赚钱

## Definition

赚钱 refers to earning money or pursuing financial gain. The concept treats financial success as important but subordinate to personal values, usefulness, loyalty, affection, moral character, and the quality of one’s team.

## Key Characteristics

- Represents the pursuit of income, wealth, or financial success.
- Is placed below being a person with loyalty, affection, and moral character.
- Is connected with discretion through the principle that wealth should not be publicly displayed.
- Is considered meaningful when supported by personal value, usefulness, and a stable team.
- Should not be treated as the sole measure of success.

## Applications

- Evaluating financial ambitions alongside character and interpersonal responsibilities.
- Pursuing income while maintaining discretion about personal wealth.
- Assessing business or career success through both financial outcomes and the value created for others.
- Building a stable team as a foundation for sustainable financial progress.
- Using [[反过来想]] to examine the risks of prioritizing money above personal values.
