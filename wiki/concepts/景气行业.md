# 景气行业

## Definition

景气行业 refers to an industry experiencing favorable operating conditions, market demand, and growth prospects. The concept emphasizes that industry-level conditions can substantially influence company performance: when an industry is weak, even an excellent company may not be able to fully avoid the downturn, while favorable industry conditions can create opportunities for companies operating within the sector.

## Key Characteristics

- Reflects favorable industry-level operating and market conditions.
- Is influenced by factors such as demand, competition, pricing, investment, and the broader economic cycle.
- Limits the extent to which company-specific excellence can offset a structurally weak industry.
- Creates opportunities for companies when sector conditions are supportive.
- Is related to sector cycles, competitive moats, and comparative company performance.

## Applications

- Evaluating whether an investment opportunity is supported by sector-level growth rather than company-specific strengths alone.
- Comparing companies such as [[宁德时代]] and [[欣旺达]] within the same or related industry context.
- Assessing how industry cycles may affect revenue growth, profitability, and valuation.
- Distinguishing the effects of a [[护城河]] from the effects of favorable or unfavorable industry conditions.
- Analyzing situations in which gradual deterioration resembles [[温水煮青蛙]].
