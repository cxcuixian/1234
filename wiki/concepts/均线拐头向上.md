# 均线拐头向上

## Definition

均线拐头向上 describes a moving average changing direction toward an upward trend. The source presents this directional change as a technical signal relevant to identifying a genuine [[买入机会]], while emphasizing that investors should wait for confirmation rather than rush into a position.

## Key Characteristics

- Indicates that a moving average has shifted from a declining or flat direction toward an upward direction.
- Functions as a technical confirmation signal rather than a standalone guarantee of future gains.
- Appears alongside the condition that a stock should stand firmly above a [[长期均线]].
- Supports patient entry decisions and discourages premature buying.
- May be evaluated together with signals such as [[破线]] and broader market conditions, including [[大跌]].

## Applications

- Assessing whether a potential [[买入机会]] has received sufficient technical confirmation.
- Supporting a cautious entry strategy after a stock’s moving average begins to turn upward.
- Combining moving-average direction with price position relative to a [[长期均线]].
- Helping investors practice [[耐心]] and avoid rushing into a position before technical conditions improve.
