# 物质绑架

## Definition

物质绑架 describes a condition in which material consumption and financial obligations constrain a person’s freedom of action. Escaping this condition is treated as a prerequisite for genuine freedom because reduced material dependence allows individuals to choose their preferred work and activities.

## Key Characteristics

- Material consumption or financial obligations limit freedom of choice.
- Dependence on income and possessions can determine which work a person must accept.
- Saving and reducing material needs are presented as ways to regain autonomy.
- The concept is closely connected to [[财富的本质是选择权]] and [[延迟满足]].
- It encourages [[长期思维]] by prioritizing future freedom over immediate consumption.

## Applications

- Evaluating whether lifestyle expenses require continued dependence on unwanted work.
- Setting savings goals to increase the ability to change careers or reduce working hours.
- Distinguishing essential needs from consumption that creates avoidable financial obligations.
- Planning for greater freedom by practicing delayed gratification and reducing recurring expenses.
