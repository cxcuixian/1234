# 举国体育

## Definition

举国体育 is an institutional lens for understanding the development of table tennis. It describes the state-supported layer of the sport’s development, alongside [[群众体育]] and [[职业体育]], and is associated with organized competition, talent selection, and the expansion of table tennis into a highly visible sport.

## Key Characteristics

- Emphasizes state-supported organization and development.
- Operates alongside mass-participation and professional forms of sport.
- Relies on organized competition and [[人才选拔机制]].
- Contributes to the transformation of [[乒乓球]] from a small-ball activity into a prominent sport.
- Functions as an analytical perspective rather than a formally defined institutional category in the source.

## Applications

- Analyzing the historical development of [[乒乓球]].
- Examining how state-supported systems contribute to athlete development and competitive performance.
- Comparing institutional layers of sport, including [[群众体育]] and [[职业体育]].
- Studying the relationship between organized competition, talent selection, and the visibility of a sport.
