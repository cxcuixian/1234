# Research Faucet

## Definition

Research faucet is a metaphor for a source that provides clean, focused, and reliable knowledge. Unlike a fire hose, which delivers a much larger but potentially muddy volume of information, a research faucet emphasizes a narrow, high-quality information channel that requires less filtering.

## Key Characteristics

- Delivers focused and reliable knowledge.
- Prioritizes information quality over information volume.
- Reduces the need to filter overwhelming or irrelevant material.
- Encourages identifying the most relevant people and evaluating their work or knowledge.
- Contrasts with broad, high-volume information streams.

## Applications

- Selecting expert sources for research and decision-making.
- Prioritizing high-quality information channels over unrestricted information volume.
- Building reading lists, expert networks, or monitoring systems.
- Evaluating knowledge through the work and expertise of relevant individuals.
- Improving information consumption when broad sources are difficult to filter.
