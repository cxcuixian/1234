# Opportunistic Shortcut-Seeking

## Definition

Opportunistic shortcut-seeking describes pursuing short-term advantage through shortcuts, opportunism, or deceptive tactics instead of developing durable competence. The concept suggests that such behavior may ultimately result in severe consequences.

## Key Characteristics

- Prioritizes immediate gains over sustainable capability.
- Relies on shortcuts, opportunism, or deceptive conduct.
- May involve self-deception about one’s own methods and prospects.
- Underestimates the possibility that others may use similar tactics.
- Can produce serious long-term costs despite short-term benefits.
- Provides a basis for skepticism toward the conduct of high-technology companies.

## Applications

- Evaluating individuals or organizations that seek rapid gains without building reliable expertise.
- Analyzing deceptive or opportunistic strategies in competitive environments.
- Assessing the long-term risks of shortcuts and self-deception.
- Critically examining the behavior and incentives of high-technology companies.
