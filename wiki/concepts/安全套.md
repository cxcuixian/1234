# 安全套

## Definition

Condom is a barrier-protection method used during sexual intercourse to reduce the risk of pregnancy and sexually transmitted infections. It is presented as the standard protective measure for first-time sex because a single encounter can result in either outcome.

Condom use is especially important when partners’ sexual-health histories are unknown, because a single encounter can result in pregnancy or infection. The source advises treating first-time sex as having potential risk rather than relying on assumptions about fertility or infection status.

## Key Characteristics

- Select a suitable size and check the condom’s orientation before application.
- Pinch the reservoir tip to expel air, and ensure the rolled rim faces outward while unrolling the condom.
- Roll the condom down over the erect penis and hold its base during withdrawal after ejaculation.
- Replace the condom if it was initially placed backward; do not turn it around and reuse it.
- Keep several condoms available because condoms may be reversed, damaged, or otherwise unusable.
- Do not combine latex condoms with oil-based lubricants.
- Do not rely on withdrawal or an assumed low-risk ovulation period as substitutes for barrier protection.
- Connects pregnancy prevention with infection prevention.
- Use a condom from the beginning of genital contact and do not take risks by having unprotected ejaculation inside the partner.
- A single sexual encounter can result in pregnancy or infection, particularly when the partners’ sexual-health histories are unknown.

## Applications

Condom is used during vaginal, anal, and oral sexual activity where barrier protection is appropriate. For first-time sex, it can be incorporated into preparation, foreplay, and intercourse by checking the package, selecting a suitable lubricant, applying it correctly, and replacing it when necessary.
