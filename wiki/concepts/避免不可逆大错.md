# 避免不可逆大错

## Definition

避免不可逆大错 is a risk-management principle that prioritizes preventing mistakes with consequences that cannot easily be repaired. It emphasizes protecting against catastrophic downside before pursuing success, and is situated within a broader strategy of avoiding harmful habits, reckless behavior, and other paths to failure.

## Key Characteristics

- Prioritizes avoiding catastrophic or irreversible losses.
- Emphasizes downside protection before the pursuit of gains.
- Complements [[概率]], [[终身学习]], and patience-oriented decision-making.
- Relates to debt avoidance, accountability, and [[损失厌恶]].
- Encourages identifying and avoiding behaviors that can permanently restrict future options.

## Applications

- Assess major decisions by considering whether a possible failure would be difficult or impossible to recover from.
- Avoid unnecessary financial exposure, including debt and commitments that can create lasting constraints.
- Establish safeguards against reckless behavior, harmful habits, and preventable high-impact mistakes.
- Use accountability and review processes to detect dangerous decisions before they become irreversible.
- Apply the principle to long-term learning, career planning, health, and personal finance by preserving future options.
