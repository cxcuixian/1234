# 美债收益率

## Definition

美债收益率 is the yield earned by holding U.S. government debt. It is treated in the source as a major financial reference point within a macroeconomic framework for asset allocation.

## Key Characteristics

- Represents the return associated with U.S. government debt.
- Serves as a major reference point for evaluating financial-market conditions.
- Is discussed in connection with the possibility of [[无风险套利]] and earning returns without active work over a year.
- Relates to [[美国通胀压力]], [[中国通缩]], and preferences for [[高股息的股票]].
- Can influence comparisons between relatively low-risk fixed-income returns and equity-income strategies.

## Applications

- Assessing macroeconomic conditions and asset-allocation choices.
- Comparing government-bond returns with high-dividend equity returns.
- Evaluating the attractiveness of risk-free or low-risk investment opportunities.
- Interpreting the interaction between U.S. inflation pressure, Chinese deflation, and financial-market positioning.
