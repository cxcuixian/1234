# 代谢综合症

## Definition

代谢综合症, or metabolic syndrome, is a clinically significant cluster of metabolic changes that can increase health risks. In the source context, it is associated with psychiatric medication and bodily effects such as increased appetite, slowed metabolism, and changes in blood glucose.

## Key Characteristics

- May involve interconnected metabolic changes affecting appetite, metabolism, and blood glucose.
- Can be a clinically important risk associated with some psychiatric medications.
- Highlights bodily effects of treatment beyond psychiatric symptoms.
- Requires awareness as part of understanding treatment, rather than simple memorization of medication names.
- Appears in the same medication-learning context as [[奥氮平]] and dopamine-related mechanisms.

## Applications

- Monitoring potential metabolic effects during psychiatric medication treatment.
- Supporting medication education and informed clinical discussions.
- Encouraging attention to appetite, metabolic rate, and blood glucose when evaluating treatment.
- Providing context for understanding medication-related risks alongside psychiatric benefits.
