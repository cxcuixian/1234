# 稳定最关键

## Definition

稳定最关键 is a care principle for people experiencing serious psychiatric symptoms. It prioritizes environmental consistency, predictable routines, and the avoidance of stimulating objects or interventions that could worsen psychological destabilization.

## Key Characteristics

- Prioritizes stability over forcefully changing a person’s circumstances.
- Advises avoiding stimulating objects and other potentially destabilizing influences.
- Recommends preserving the patient’s established routines.
- Treats environmental consistency as more important than imposing a new direction or destiny.
- Recognizes that people experiencing psychiatric symptoms may be especially vulnerable to destabilizing stimuli.
- Relates to the care approach of [[引导而非控制]].

## Applications

This principle can guide care planning for people experiencing serious psychiatric symptoms, including [[思琦]]. Applications include maintaining a familiar environment, minimizing unnecessary sensory or situational changes, avoiding coercive interventions where possible, and introducing changes gradually only when they are necessary and likely to support stability.
