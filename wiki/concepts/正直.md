# 正直

## Definition

正直 is a character standard centered on honesty, integrity, and trustworthy conduct. In the source context, it is presented as a quality associated with being worthy of [[巴菲特|Warren Buffett]]’s example and as a foundation for what a person can ultimately possess or achieve.

## Key Characteristics

- Functions as a moral criterion for evaluating conduct and long-term credibility.
- Treats personal integrity as foundational to achievement, possession, and reputation.
- Contrasts with wasteful, irresponsible, or self-destructive behavior.
- Emphasizes consistent character rather than short-term appearances.

## Applications

- Evaluating whether a person’s conduct is worthy of trust or emulation.
- Assessing long-term credibility in personal, professional, or financial relationships.
- Guiding decisions that prioritize integrity over wasteful or self-destructive behavior.
- Considering character as a prerequisite for sustainable achievement.
