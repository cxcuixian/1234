# 效率

## Definition

效率 is the principle of prioritizing effective resource use and system performance over minimizing immediate cost. A cheaper option is not necessarily better if it reduces scalability, productivity, or overall system performance.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats efficiency as a more important optimization target than short-term cost reduction.
- Recognizes that lower-cost choices can create performance or scalability constraints.
- Is supported by well-designed interfaces and the productive use of powerful AI systems.
- Benefits from economies of scale.
- Applies to both business operations and broader industrial development.

## Applications

- Selecting systems, tools, or architectures based on long-term performance rather than purchase price alone.
- Designing consistent interfaces that reduce coordination and integration costs.
- Using powerful AI systems to increase productivity and operational output.
- Scaling production or business processes to capture economies of scale.
- Evaluating industrial development strategies through their effects on system-wide productivity.
