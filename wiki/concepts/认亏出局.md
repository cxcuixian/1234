# 认亏出局

## Definition

认亏出局 is a risk-management method that accepts a loss and exits a position after a breakdown or adverse reversal, rather than maintaining unrealistic expectations of recovery. It emphasizes acknowledging market reality and abandoning fantasies about a rebound.

## Key Characteristics

- Accepts realized losses instead of denying or postponing them.
- Uses a breakdown or reversal as a signal to reassess and exit.
- Counters emotional attachment to a declining position.
- Reduces the risk of allowing prolonged losses to grow.
- Requires disciplined action despite the psychological discomfort of loss.

## Applications

- Exiting an investment after its price structure breaks down.
- Preventing continued holding based solely on hopes of recovery.
- Establishing rules for responding to adverse trend reversals.
- Supporting disciplined portfolio risk management.
- Limiting the effects of [[损失厌恶]] on investment decisions.
