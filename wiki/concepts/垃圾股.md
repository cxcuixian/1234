# 垃圾股

## Description

垃圾股 denotes stocks that the source regards as structurally poor and dangerous to hold. The source characterizes them as declining even when the broader market rises and continuing to decline when the market falls. A central risk is that temporary profits are eventually given back because the investor never truly understood the asset, as illustrated through [[entities/芒格|芒格]]'s investment perspective. The source recommends treating every decline as an opportunity to exit rather than rationalizing continued ownership, in contrast to approaches associated with [[entities/技术分析|技术分析]] and [[entities/卖出与风险退出|卖出与风险退出]].

The source advises selling junk stocks decisively after a sharp rise, particularly when a long upper wick signals a possible major decline. Taking profits early is presented as preferable to continuing to expect further gains.

## New Information (2026-09-10)

- Investors should not become emotionally attached to junk stocks because they can potentially repurchase them later at a better price. (This adds a behavioral warning that complements the existing recommendation to treat declines as exit opportunities.)
- The term is also applied to highly speculative technology themes whose valuations may not be supported by infrastructure, energy supply, business fundamentals, or grid stability. (This broadens the source-specific meaning of junk stock beyond general price weakness to include fundamentally unsupported technology investments.)
- The source specifically connects GPU servers, optical modules, liquid cooling, data centers, and electricity generation to the evaluation of high-technology stocks, arguing that unreliable wind or solar power and insufficient grid regulation can impose heavy costs on institutions and speculative investors. (This provides a concrete application of the junk-stock classification to technology and energy-investment analysis.)
