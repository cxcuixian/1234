# A股

## Definition

[[A股]] refers to the mainland Chinese stock market, particularly as an investment environment characterized by substantial uncertainty and recurring declines. The source emphasizes that investors should evaluate actual financial commitment, business fundamentals, and long-term value rather than relying on market narratives alone.

## Key Characteristics

- Exposed to substantial uncertainty, volatility, and recurring market declines.
- Requires analysis of companies’ actual financial commitment and underlying business fundamentals.
- Highlights the importance of emotional steadiness during periods of market stress.
- Encourages long-term conviction rather than short-term reactions to price movements.
- Connects stock ownership with company leadership and the prosperity of an industry.
- Contrasts [[散户思维]] with [[投资家思维]].
- Can be examined through [[数值分析]], [[控制论]], [[损失函数]], [[反馈学习]], and [[负反馈]].
- Relates market stability and investor behavior to [[稳态]] and [[控制工程]].
- Appears in the source alongside discussions of [[股票]], [[人工智能]], [[科学研究]], [[核心竞争力]], and [[定价权]].

## Applications

- Evaluating investment opportunities in the mainland Chinese stock market through financial and operational fundamentals.
- Assessing whether a company possesses durable [[核心竞争力]] and [[定价权]].
