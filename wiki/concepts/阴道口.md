# 阴道口

## Definition

The vaginal opening is the entrance to the vaginal canal. It is located below the urethral opening and forms part of the vulva. Its appearance and receptiveness may vary with sexual arousal, muscle tension, and lubrication.

## Key Characteristics

- Located below the urethral opening and leading into the vaginal canal.
- May appear tighter or more closed when arousal is low.
- May become softer and more receptive as arousal and lubrication increase.
- Must be distinguished from the urethral opening to avoid pain, injury, and distress.
- Gentle, slow, shallow contact and sufficient lubrication are recommended before [[手指进入]] or penile entry.
- It is anatomically distinct from [[外阴 ≠ 阴道]], the [[阴道]], [[尿道口]], [[会阴]], and [[肛门]].

## Applications

The term is used to:

- Explain the anatomical distinction between the vulva, vaginal canal, and nearby structures.
- Support accurate identification of the vaginal opening during sexual activity.
- Guide gradual entry using [[前戏]], [[润滑]], and [[语言确认]].
- Reduce discomfort associated with insufficient arousal, inadequate lubrication, or mistaken identification of the urethral opening.
- Provide context for discussions of [[同意]], [[表现焦虑]], [[第一次表现差]], and [[场景性勃起困难]].
- Explain how [[性兴奋]], [[海绵体充血]], [[交感神经]], [[副交感]], and [[睡眠和放松]] may affect sexual response.
- Clarify the anatomical context of [[阴蒂]], [[阴蒂包皮]], [[处女膜]], [[阴阜]], [[大阴唇]], [[小阴唇]], and [[阴道前壁]].
- Support safer sexual practices involving [[安全套]], [[怀孕或传染]], [[口交]], and gradual penetration.
- Distinguish vaginal anatomy from topics such as [[高潮、喷水、G点]], [[射精控制]], and [[排卵状态]].
