# 几何原本

## Definition

几何原本 is a foundational geometry text presented as a model for organizing definitions, basic facts, and deductive constructions. In the source, it also represents a method for asking how known information can be used to derive the unknown, connecting mathematical foundations with the broader construction of a systematic network of linked knowledge.

## Key Characteristics

- Organizes knowledge through definitions and basic facts.
- Emphasizes deductive reasoning and step-by-step construction.
- Illustrates how known information can be used to derive unknown results.
- Connects classical mathematical foundations with systematic knowledge organization.
- Serves primarily as a methodological and educational reference in the source.
- The source does not identify its author or discuss individual propositions.

## Applications

- Teaching foundational geometric reasoning and deductive construction.
- Demonstrating how definitions and basic facts support more advanced conclusions.
- Providing a model for solving problems by deriving unknown information from known conditions.
- Supporting the development of a systematic network of linked knowledge.
- Encouraging independent reconstruction and verification of reasoning.
