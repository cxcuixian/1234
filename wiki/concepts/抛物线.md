# 抛物线

## Definition

抛物线 is a geometric curve associated with quadratic equations and their root formulas. It connects symbolic mathematical relationships with physical systems involving reflection, aiming, illumination, and motion.

## Key Characteristics

- It is a conic section with a characteristic curved shape.
- It can represent the graph of a quadratic function.
- Its geometric properties are closely related to quadratic equations and [[concepts/求根公式|求根公式]].
- Its reflective properties support applications in optical, radio, and engineering systems.
- It provides a bridge between abstract mathematical models and engineered physical systems.

## Applications

- Satellite antennas use parabolic reflectors to collect and focus signals.
- Radio telescopes use the reflective geometry of a parabola to concentrate radio waves.
- Automobile headlights use parabolic reflectors to direct light into a focused beam.
- Missile trajectories can be modeled using parabolic approximations in appropriate conditions.
- Quadratic equations and [[concepts/二次方程|二次方程]] use parabolic graphs to connect algebraic roots with geometric points.
