# Beta blockers

## Definition

Beta blockers are a class of medications that reduce beta-adrenergic receptor activity. In the source, they are described as a commonly used pharmacological treatment option for [[Akathisia]], with [[Propranolol]] given as the principal example.

## Key Characteristics

- Used frequently when [[Akathisia]] occurs.
- [[Propranolol]] is presented as the principal example.
- Used alongside alternatives such as [[Clonidine]], [[Mirtazapine]], and [[Benzodiazepines]].
- Distinguished from [[Anticholinergic medications]], which the source states are not helpful for [[Akathisia]].
- Represent a pharmacological intervention for a [[Movement disorder]] associated with medication exposure.

## Applications

Beta blockers are used as a treatment option for [[Akathisia]], particularly when the condition occurs in the context of antipsychotic or other medication use. They may be considered alongside [[Clonidine]], [[Mirtazapine]], and [[Benzodiazepines]], with [[Propranolol]] serving as the principal example in the source.
