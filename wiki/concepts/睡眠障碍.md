# Sleep Disorder

## Definition

Sleep disorder refers to clinically meaningful problems involving sleep duration, timing, continuity, or quality. In the source, it is discussed in a mental-health context alongside manic symptoms, early emotional trauma, and lithium treatment. The source also includes an approximate prevalence claim, but does not identify the population, study, or diagnostic method supporting that percentage.

## Key Characteristics

- Can be considered a symptom, associated condition, or clinically relevant manifestation of broader psychological states.
- May occur alongside manic symptoms and other mental-health concerns.
- Assessment requires attention to sleep patterns, duration, timing, continuity, quality, and contextual factors.
