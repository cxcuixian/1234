# 逆向思维

## Definition

逆向思维 is a reasoning method that examines a situation from an opposing, unconventional, or less obvious direction rather than accepting the dominant perspective.

## Key Characteristics

- Challenges prevailing assumptions and conventional conclusions.
- Considers perspectives that are easy to overlook, including those of other market participants.
- Complements [[长期思维]], simplicity, and trend analysis.
- Encourages independent judgment instead of attachment to an existing position.
- Can be used to assess both opportunities and risks by examining the opposite outcome.

## Applications

- In investment analysis, it can support selling speculative stocks after excessive price increases instead of remaining emotionally attached to them.
- In market interpretation, it encourages considering what large funds and other participants may be thinking rather than viewing the market only from one's own position.
- In decision-making, it can reveal overlooked risks, challenge consensus views, and identify alternatives to an apparently obvious course of action.
- In combination with [[能力圈]], it can help define when a contrarian judgment is supported by sufficient understanding rather than by mere opposition.
