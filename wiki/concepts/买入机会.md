# Buying Opportunity

## Definition

A buying opportunity is a sufficiently reliable point at which an investor may enter a position. An apparent opportunity should not be acted on merely because prices have moved, since buying too early may not produce an immediate rise. A genuine opportunity is associated with technical evidence, such as a stock trading above a long-term moving average while that average turns upward.

## Key Characteristics

- Requires sufficient evidence rather than a simple price movement.
- May be indicated when a stock remains above a [[长期均线]] and the average turns upward, as described by [[均线拐头向上]].
- Emphasizes patience and avoidance of premature entry.
- Reflects the expectation that additional opportunities will continue to appear.
- Can be distinguished from unsuccessful timing after a [[大跌]] or a [[破线]].

## Applications

- Evaluating whether technical conditions justify opening an investment position.
- Avoiding premature purchases when a price movement lacks confirmation.
- Applying patience when waiting for a long-term moving-average trend to improve.
- Comparing entry timing strategies such as [[尾盘买入]].
- Assessing investment discussions involving [[中国巨石]], [[中际]], or [[孙宇晨]].
