# 乒乓球

## Definition

乒乓球 (table tennis) is a sport and institutional subject examined in the source through its historical development, broad social reach, and relationship to mass, professional, and nationally organized sport. Its accessibility, low cost, and relatively modest physical-strength requirements have supported its widespread adoption and influence.

## Key Characteristics

- Inexpensive equipment and relatively low participation costs.
- Easy to popularize across broad social groups.
- Does not require extreme physical strength, enabling wide participation.
- Connects [[群众体育]], [[职业体育]], and [[举国体育]].
- Develops through talent selection, tactical innovation, rule changes, and imitation of opponents.
- Illustrates how a relatively small sport can achieve broad institutional and cultural influence.
- Produces highly visible athletes through processes of [[明星化]].
- Has evolved through changes such as [[21分到11分]].

## Applications

乒乓球 is applied and organized across several overlapping contexts:

- Community and recreational participation within [[群众体育]].
- Elite training and competition within [[职业体育]].
- National talent development and competitive organization within [[举国体育]].
- Talent identification and development through [[人才选拔机制]].
- Tactical training involving [[陪练模仿对手打法]].
- Institutional analysis of how rules, training systems, and media visibility shape a sport’s development.
