# Cash Flow

## Definition

Cash flow is the movement of cash into and out of a business or investment. It is a central practical measure for evaluating financial resilience, operating quality, and long-term value creation.

## Key Characteristics

- Reflects the actual cash generated and used by an organization.
- Helps distinguish durable operating performance from superficial narratives or short-term excitement.
- Connects financial resilience with technological capability and long-term accumulation.
- Provides a practical basis for evaluating businesses and investments.
- May be strengthened by accumulated technology and productive capabilities over time.

## Applications

- Evaluating the financial health and resilience of businesses.
- Assessing the quality and durability of investment opportunities.
- Comparing operating performance beyond accounting results or market narratives.
- Analyzing how [[Technology]] and [[Capital Expenditure]] contribute to long-term cash generation.
- Supporting assessments of [[Long-Term Competitiveness]].
