# 理性

## Definition

Rationality is a learned capacity for regulating emotional responses, delaying impulsive action, and making decisions based on evidence, structure, and long-term consequences. It is not treated as an automatic human trait. Irrationality becomes more likely when a person feels that something must be done immediately; deliberately slowing down can help restore sound judgment.

## Key Characteristics

- Develops through learning, reflection, and repeated practice.
- Is weakened by urgency, emotional pressure, and impulsive reactions.
- Uses deliberate pauses to reduce the influence of fear, excitement, and other short-term emotions.
- Supports disciplined decisions under uncertainty.
- Connects personal emotional regulation with sound investment judgment.
- Encourages attention to structural changes rather than reacting to temporary fluctuations.

## Applications

- **Investment decisions:** Supports defensive positioning, disciplined selling, and evaluating whether a business has undergone a fundamental structural change.
- **Risk management:** Encourages pausing before acting when market movements or perceived threats create a sense of urgency.
- **Personal decision-making:** Helps individuals distinguish necessary action from emotionally driven reactions.
- **Lifelong development:** Can be strengthened through continued learning, reflection, and review of past decisions.
