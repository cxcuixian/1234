# 商誉

## Definition

商誉 refers to the reputation, credibility, and public trust associated with a company. Beyond its accounting meaning, the concept describes the corporate legitimacy gained through keeping promises, acting transparently, and accepting accountability.

## Key Characteristics

- Reflects public trust in a company’s conduct and commitments.
- Depends on credibility, transparency, and consistent accountability.
- Extends beyond an accounting asset to include corporate legitimacy.
- Can be weakened when companies fail to keep their word or conceal relevant information.
- The source uses 商誉 to criticize the lack of trustworthy corporate behavior among Chinese companies.

## Applications

- Evaluating a company’s reputation and institutional credibility.
- Assessing whether corporate statements and commitments are reliable.
- Analyzing the relationship between transparency, accountability, and public trust.
- Distinguishing formal accounting value from broader social and institutional legitimacy.
- Critiquing corporate behavior and its effects on stakeholder confidence.
