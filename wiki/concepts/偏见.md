# Bias

## Definition

Bias is a distortion of judgment that can arise when people fail to examine a problem from multiple perspectives. It differs from a neutral difference of opinion because it systematically affects how evidence, interpretations, or conclusions are evaluated.

## Key Characteristics

- Arises from insufficient consideration of alternative perspectives.
- Distorts judgment rather than representing a neutral disagreement.
- Can be reduced through broad learning and deliberate perspective-taking.
- Is closely related to confirmation bias.

## Applications

- Evaluating complex problems by considering multiple viewpoints.
- Improving decision-making through broader learning and evidence review.
- Identifying one-sided assumptions in analysis, discussion, and judgment.
- Designing education or review processes that encourage perspective-taking.
