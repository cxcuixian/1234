# 左右手训练

## Definition

左右手训练 is a method for responding to an injury affecting the dominant hand by developing functional ability in both hands. It treats the hands as fundamentally equivalent rather than assigning them permanently capable or incapable roles. Through repeated practice, mistakes, correction, and gradual adaptation, temporary physical limitation can become an opportunity to build bilateral ability.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats both hands as capable of development rather than dividing them into fixed roles.
- Uses repeated practice to support gradual motor adaptation.
- Regards mistakes as part of the learning and correction process.
- Converts a temporary limitation into an opportunity for broader physical capability.
- Emphasizes practical skill development following dominant-hand injury.

## Applications

左右手训练 can be applied during recovery from an injury affecting the dominant hand, especially when everyday tasks must be redistributed between the hands. It may support the gradual development of bilateral ability through routine activities, structured exercises, repeated correction, and adaptation to changing physical limitations.
