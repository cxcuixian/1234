# Long-distance relationship

## Definition

A long-distance relationship is a romantic relationship in which the partners live far enough apart that frequent in-person contact is difficult. In the source, this arrangement is judged negatively as unreliable and potentially wasteful of time.

## Key Characteristics

- Limited opportunities for regular face-to-face contact.
- Relationship viability is evaluated through physical presence and frequency of interaction.
- The source presents the arrangement through strongly emotional personal judgments rather than balanced analysis.
- The source favors finding a partner who can be met frequently.
- The source advises against compromising on the preference for regular in-person contact.

## Applications

- Evaluating whether a romantic relationship meets an individual’s practical needs.
- Clarifying personal preferences regarding physical presence and communication frequency.
- Comparing relationship arrangements according to the time, effort, and reliability they require.
- Supporting decisions to seek a partner who lives nearby or can be met regularly.
