# Music Copyright

## Definition

Music copyright refers to the legal rights associated with musical works and recordings, including the ability to receive royalties from their use. It can function as an asset capable of generating durable income with relatively low ongoing capital requirements.

## Key Characteristics

- May provide recurring royalty income over an extended period.
- Typically requires less ongoing physical investment than capital-intensive assets.
- Can support protection against inflation when royalty income increases with usage or pricing.
- May become easier to operate over time once the underlying rights and licensing arrangements are established.
- Is contrasted with assets that require repeated additional capital expenditure.

## Applications

Music copyright can be held or managed as an income-generating asset, licensed to third parties, or incorporated into an investment portfolio seeking durable cash flows and relatively low maintenance capital requirements.
