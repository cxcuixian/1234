# American Capitalism

## Definition

American capitalism is presented as an economic system driven primarily by market opportunities, fashionable investment narratives, and profit extraction. The concept is used critically to describe an orientation that prioritizes trends and monetization over deeper intellectual, mathematical, or moral concerns.

## Key Characteristics

- Emphasizes market opportunities and profitable investment narratives.
- Prioritizes monetization and customer-charging business models.
- Encourages attention to fashionable trends rather than substantive intellectual or moral questions.
- Can facilitate the exploitation of retail investors through speculative assets.
- Provides a broader economic context for criticism of speculative technology stocks and related social values.

## Applications

This concept can be used to analyze speculative investment practices, technology-stock promotion, commercialization strategies, and business models centered on extracting revenue from customers or retail investors. It also provides a framework for examining how market incentives may influence intellectual priorities and social values.
