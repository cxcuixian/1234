# Sound Values

## Definition

Sound Values is a guiding framework for selecting meaningful work and organizing long-term personal development. It emphasizes sustained effort in areas one likes and is capable of mastering, rather than distraction, superficial effort, opportunism, or external temptation. In the source, the concept connects personal ethics with technical growth through focused work with ABAQUS.

## Key Characteristics

- Guides decisions about work, learning, and long-term development.
- Favors work that combines personal interest with demonstrated ability.
- Emphasizes fully mastering one subject before moving on.
- Supports the construction of a structured personal knowledge system.
- Resists distraction, superficial effort, opportunism, and external temptation.
- Connects ethical self-direction with sustained technical practice.
- Encourages stable character, independent thinking, and understanding underlying principles.

## Applications

- Selecting professional or research work according to genuine interest and competence.
- Planning long-term technical development through sustained practice with ABAQUS.
- Organizing knowledge into a coherent personal knowledge system.
- Evaluating opportunities without being driven primarily by novelty, status, or short-term gain.
- Choosing depth and complete understanding over shallow exposure to many subjects.
- Establishing personal standards for resisting temptation and maintaining consistency.
