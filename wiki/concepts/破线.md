# 破线

## Definition

破线 describes the loss or downward breach of a significant price or chart level. In the source, it is presented as a warning condition expected to precede [[大跌]], rather than as an isolated trading signal. The subsequent decline may unfold gradually, so investors should not assume an immediate reversal.

## Key Characteristics

- Indicates a downward breach of an important price or chart level.
- Functions as a warning condition associated with a possible [[大跌]].
- Does not necessarily imply an immediate or complete decline.
- Requires interpretation alongside technical chart patterns, including [[长期均线]] and [[均线拐头向上]].
- Highlights the importance of [[耐心]] when evaluating entry timing and [[买入机会]].

## Applications

- Monitoring price charts for warning signs before making an investment decision.
- Assessing whether a breach represents a developing decline or a temporary fluctuation.
- Avoiding premature entry while waiting for clearer evidence of stabilization or reversal.
- Supporting disciplined position-entry decisions, including consideration of [[尾盘买入]].
- Comparing technical warning signals with broader market contexts such as [[餐饮市场]] and [[超额利润]].
