# Long-Term Thinking

## Definition

Long-term thinking is a preferred temporal orientation for learning, work, investment, and personal life. It emphasizes patience, delayed gratification, disciplined judgment, and attention to durable outcomes rather than impulsive decisions, quick profits, or emotional reactions to short-term price movements.

## Key Characteristics

- Prioritizes durable value over immediate results.
- Resists impulsive decisions and emotional reactions to short-term changes.
- Supports patience, saving, and delayed gratification.
- Emphasizes simplification, trend awareness, and attention to controllable matters.
- Encourages disciplined judgment in investment and personal decision-making.

## Applications

- **Investing:** Maintains patience and disciplined judgment during short-term market volatility.
- **Learning and work:** Favors consistent effort, compounding knowledge, and durable skill development.
- **Personal finance:** Encourages saving and reduces dependence on material consumption.
- **Personal life:** Helps preserve freedom from material pressure by prioritizing long-term choices over immediate rewards.
