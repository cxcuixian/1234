# 上梁不正下梁歪

## Definition

A proverb expressing the idea that misconduct, poor standards, or weak credibility at the top of an organization tend to spread throughout the organization. In the source context, it describes how unreliable management undermines shareholder confidence and weakens the investment case for the company.

## Key Characteristics

- Connects leadership behavior with the conduct and reliability of the broader organization.
- Emphasizes that poor example-setting by senior management can propagate downward.
- Treats management credibility as a material consideration in evaluating an investment.
- Supports decisive risk exit when trust in management has been lost.
- Applies to organizational culture, governance, accountability, and investor confidence.

## Applications

This concept can be used to assess whether management behavior reflects the standards expected throughout a company. In investment analysis, it frames a loss of confidence in management as a reason for retail investors to sell without hesitation. It also applies to evaluating organizational culture, leadership accountability, and the relationship between executive conduct and company-wide reliability.
