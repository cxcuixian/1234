# 怨恨最后会反噬自己

## Definition

怨恨最后会反噬自己 describes the idea that sustained resentment eventually harms the person who harbors it. The concept frames this consequence as arising from the interconnectedness of all things, rather than merely from an interpersonal grievance. It functions both as a psychological observation and as a moral warning.

## Key Characteristics

- Sustained resentment ultimately damages the person who maintains it.
- The consequence is connected to the interdependence of all things.
- It presents resentment as both a psychological burden and a moral risk.
- It is associated with [[尊重他人]], [[善待自己]], and guidance without control.
- It complements the principle that [[稳定最关键]].

## Applications

- Encouraging people to recognize the personal costs of prolonged resentment.
- Supporting reflection on emotional habits and interpersonal conflict.
- Framing forgiveness or emotional release as forms of self-care.
- Guiding ethical behavior through awareness of mutual interconnectedness.
- Providing a caution against allowing grievances to dominate judgment or conduct.
