# 法制

## Definition

法制 refers to governance through an established legal system and the institutional enforcement of law. It describes how formal rules, governmental institutions, and legal procedures regulate social life, political authority, individual conduct, and personal liberty. 法制 is analytically distinct from democracy: a society may possess a legal order without being democratically governed.

## Key Characteristics

- Relies on established laws, institutions, and enforcement procedures.
- Connects government authority with formal legal constraints.
- Regulates political systems, social relationships, and personal conduct.
- Is distinct from democratic participation and representation.
- Provides an institutional framework for defining and protecting individual liberty.
- Relates external legal restrictions to the internalization of norms through individual conscience.
- Can be examined alongside [[正义]], [[价值观]], [[功利主义]], and [[自由主义]].

## Applications

- Analyzing how governments establish, interpret, and enforce legal rules.
- Distinguishing legal order from democracy when comparing political systems.
- Examining the institutional conditions of [[个人自由]].
- Studying how law regulates economic systems such as [[资本主义]] and market coordination through [[看不见的手]].
- Evaluating the relationship between formal institutions, social organization, [[生产方式]], and [[社会形态]].
- Assessing how legal norms interact with personal morality, conscience, and civic conduct.
- Comparing legal regulation with informal constraints arising from scarcity, specialization, and social cooperation, including [[资源稀缺]] and [[分工]].
