# 怀疑主义

## Definition

怀疑主义 (Skepticism) is a complementary stance to [[实践主义]]. It involves questioning assumptions, examining evidence, and resisting unjustified confidence. In this context, it is used to reduce uncertainty by scrutinizing claims about inflation, interest rates, reputation, threats, relationships, market trends, and terminology.

## Key Characteristics

- Questions apparent explanations, expected effects, and widely accepted narratives.
- Tests whether inflation and interest rates may alter outcomes or risk.
- Examines whether reputation, threats, or relationships create leverage or hidden dependencies.
- Resists following market热点 without independent analysis.
- Distinguishes familiarity with terminology from genuine understanding.
- Functions as a practical tool for reducing uncertainty rather than as a rejection of all knowledge.

## Applications

- Evaluating investment ideas affected by inflation, interest rates, or market热点.
- Identifying leverage and dependency in professional or personal relationships.
- Challenging claims based primarily on reputation, threats, or authority.
- Checking whether specialized terminology reflects actual understanding.
- Complementing [[实践主义]] by testing assumptions before acting.
- Supporting awareness of [[风险永久损失]] and the limits described by [[能力边界]].
