# Success

## Definition

Success is an outcome that may follow from avoiding predictable forms of failure and finding a path suited to the individual. It is not governed by a single universal formula; effective routes can vary substantially between people and circumstances.

## Key Characteristics

- Often depends on recognizing and avoiding predictable forms of [[Failure]].
- Requires an approach suited to the individual rather than simple imitation of others.
- Is connected with [[Lifelong learning]] and [[Extensive reading]].
- Benefits from attention to objective facts, probability, and incentives.
- Is better treated as an object of investigation and pursuit than as a fixed formula.
- Can result from different strategies under different conditions.

## Applications

Success can be used as a framework for evaluating personal goals, professional development, education, and decision-making. It encourages individuals to study relevant facts, understand probabilities and incentives, learn continuously, and identify strategies appropriate to their own circumstances. It also supports critical evaluation of advice that presents one supposedly universal route to success.
