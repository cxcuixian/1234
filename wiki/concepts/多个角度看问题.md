# Examining an Issue from Multiple Perspectives

## Definition

Examining an issue from multiple perspectives is a method of considering more than one viewpoint, framework, or body of evidence before reaching a judgment. It helps protect against bias and forms part of a disciplined approach to decision-making.

## Key Characteristics

- Considers multiple viewpoints before forming a conclusion.
- Reduces the influence of personal bias and premature judgment.
- Draws on knowledge from mathematics, probability, economics, psychology, history, and the natural sciences.
- Supports objective evaluation of success, failure, incentives, and claims of authority.
- Encourages disciplined reasoning rather than reliance on a single explanation or perspective.

## Applications

This method can be applied when evaluating the causes of success or failure, assessing incentives, interpreting uncertain outcomes, and judging the credibility of authorities or institutions. It is also useful in personal decision-making, strategic analysis, research, and lifelong learning.
