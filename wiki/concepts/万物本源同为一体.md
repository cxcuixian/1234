# All Things Share One Origin and Are Ultimately One

## Definition

All Things Share One Origin and Are Ultimately One is the philosophical proposition that all things ultimately arise from a common origin or participate in an underlying unity. In the source, this proposition introduces a moral perspective on how people should treat others and themselves. It is connected to the claim that resentment can ultimately harm the person who maintains it, and it supports relationships grounded in respect, compassion, trust, and non-coercion.

## Key Characteristics

- Presents all things as connected through a common origin or underlying unity.
- Provides a foundation for treating others and oneself with respect and compassion.
- Connects sustained resentment with possible self-directed harm.
- Favors trust and voluntary interaction over coercion and possessiveness.
- Treats each person as retaining authority over their own choices and responses.

## Applications

- Encouraging respectful and compassionate conduct in interpersonal relationships.
- Reframing resentment as a burden that may damage the person who holds it.
- Supporting requests that preserve the other person’s freedom to choose.
- Promoting trust-based relationships rather than relationships based on control or possession.
- Guiding self-care through the recognition that oneself is also part of the shared whole.
