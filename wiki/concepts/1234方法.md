# 1234方法

## Definition

1234方法 is a lightweight organizational method based on a simple and practical structure. Its value lies less in following a prescribed sequence than in understanding the reasoning behind its categories and adapting them to practical needs.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses a clear, low-complexity organizational structure.
- Emphasizes practical usability over rigid procedural compliance.
- Requires understanding the method’s underlying rationale.
- Supports adaptation according to individual circumstances.
- Reflects a preference for simple tools with meaningful application.

## Applications

1234方法 can be used to organize information, tasks, or other materials where a lightweight categorization system is more useful than a complex framework. It is particularly suitable for personal knowledge management and everyday organization when the user adapts the structure to fit their context.
