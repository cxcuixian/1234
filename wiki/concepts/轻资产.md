# 轻资产

## Definition

轻资产 describes a business or investment model that can expand or increase prices without requiring proportionate additional capital. This characteristic is treated as an important test of resistance to inflation.

## Key Characteristics

- Growth does not require proportionate increases in capital investment.
- Price increases can be implemented without correspondingly large new expenditures.
- The model contrasts with businesses that require continual reinvestment in physical infrastructure.
- Music rights and branded consumer products are examples of economics that may exhibit light-asset characteristics.
- Railways, power stations, and similar infrastructure-intensive operations are counterexamples.

## Applications

轻资产 can be used to evaluate whether a business is likely to preserve or improve its real returns during inflation. It is also useful when comparing asset-light intellectual-property or brand-based businesses with capital-intensive operations that require continuous reinvestment.
