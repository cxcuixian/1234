# Empathy

## Definition

Empathy is the capacity to recognize, understand, or share another person’s emotional experience. In the source, it connects responses to other people’s suffering and happiness with moral judgment and the view that society reflects individual character. It contributes a psychological dimension to broader questions about morality, justice, and personal character.

## Key Characteristics

- Involves recognizing or sharing another person’s emotional experience.
- Relates both to suffering and to happiness.
- Influences moral judgment and evaluations of conduct.
- Connects individual character with social relationships.
- Supports inquiry into how people understand themselves and others.
- Provides a psychological basis for discussions of morality, justice, and personal conscience.

## Applications

- **Moral reasoning:** Considering how actions affect other people’s well-being.
- **Interpersonal understanding:** Interpreting the emotions, needs, and perspectives of others.
- **Social analysis:** Examining how social relationships reflect and shape individual character.
- **Ethical reflection:** Connecting emotional experience with questions of justice, values, and personal responsibility.
- **Institutional evaluation:** Assessing whether social, economic, or legal arrangements respond adequately to human suffering and happiness.
