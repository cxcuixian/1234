# 餐饮市场

## Definition

[[餐饮市场]] is a large commercial market characterized by substantial demand and available business opportunities. However, the existence of business opportunities does not imply the availability of [[超额利润]]. The concept therefore frames market size as compatible with intense competition and limited margins.

## Key Characteristics

- Large commercial market with sufficient consumer demand.
- Contains identifiable business opportunities.
- Does not inherently provide excess profits.
- May involve intense competition and constrained profit margins.
- Is distinct from the source’s stock-specific technical analysis involving indicators such as [[长期均线]] and [[均线拐头向上]].

## Applications

[[餐饮市场]] can be used to assess whether a market has sufficient demand and commercial opportunities while separately evaluating competition, pricing pressure, operating costs, and potential profit margins. It is also useful for distinguishing market accessibility from the ability to earn [[超额利润]].
