# Capital Expenditure

## Definition

Capital expenditure refers to the funds a business invests in long-lived assets and in maintaining or expanding its ongoing operational capacity. It is a key factor in evaluating business returns, asset durability, and resistance to inflation.

## Key Characteristics

- Supports the acquisition, replacement, or improvement of long-lived assets.
- May require continuous reinvestment even when the underlying assets appear durable.
- Can reduce returns when substantial funding is needed to maintain operating capacity.
- Provides a contrast between capital-intensive businesses and light-asset businesses.
- Helps assess whether a business can raise prices without making proportionate additional investments.
- Is central to evaluating long-term returns and inflation resistance.

## Applications

Capital expenditure is used to evaluate the quality and sustainability of a business. Analysts examine continuing investment requirements in infrastructure such as railways and power stations to determine whether apparently durable assets are economically attractive. They also compare capital-intensive businesses with light-asset businesses that may raise prices without significant additional investment.
