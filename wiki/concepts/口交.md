# 口交

## Definition

口交 is a form of sexual stimulation involving the mouth, lips, or tongue. It is presented as an optional form of consensual foreplay and external stimulation, not as a compulsory sexual act. It should occur only when both partners clearly want it and remain willing to continue.

- Requires informed, ongoing, and freely given consent from both partners.
- Should begin gradually, often with contact involving the thighs and broader vulvar area before focusing on the clitoris.
- Gentle pressure and a steady rhythm are generally preferable to sudden or forceful stimulation.
- Teeth should be controlled, and painful facial stubble should be avoided.
- Hygiene and relevant oral-health conditions should be considered.
- Oral sex should be avoided during contagious oral conditions or other circumstances that could increase transmission risk.
- Partner feedback should guide changes in pressure, rhythm, or location.
- Withdrawal, pain, discomfort, or a request for less pressure should be treated as a reason to adjust or stop.

## Applications

- As external stimulation when penetration is unwanted, uncomfortable, or not appropriate.
- As a way to explore individual preferences through gradual stimulation and verbal or nonverbal feedback.
- As part of mutually negotiated sexual activity involving hygiene precautions and attention to health risks.
