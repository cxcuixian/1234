# 语言的目的

## Definition

语言的目的 is the instrumental view that language serves to solve problems through tools. It emphasizes usefulness, communication, action, and practical judgment rather than treating language solely as an object of cultural or formal study.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats language as a practical instrument for solving problems.
- Connects communication with action and decision-making.
- Emphasizes usefulness and concrete results.
- Frames knowledge and tools in relation to their real-world effectiveness.
- Encourages practical judgment when applying language to specific situations.

## Applications

This concept can guide communication design, technical explanation, education, teamwork, and problem-solving. It is especially relevant when evaluating whether language, knowledge, or tools produce concrete outcomes in engineering, manufacturing, organizational coordination, and everyday decision-making.
