# Protection Fee

## Definition

**保护费** is a figurative term for money that economic participants are portrayed as being compelled to hand over in China. In the source, it describes unavoidable or quasi-compulsory economic extraction rather than a formally defined fee, and conveys criticism of authorities for insufficiently considering its economic effects.

## Key Characteristics

- Refers to compulsory or quasi-compulsory payments rather than an official fee category.
- Frames economic extraction as unavoidable for money earned in China.
- Carries a critical and resentful tone toward state revenue-seeking behavior.
- Is used rhetorically to emphasize the perceived burden imposed on economic participants.

## Applications

The term may be used in commentary about taxation, administrative charges, regulatory payments, or other state-linked transfers that are perceived as excessive, unavoidable, or insufficiently justified. It is analytical and rhetorical rather than a precise legal or accounting designation.
