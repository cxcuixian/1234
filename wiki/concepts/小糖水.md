# Small Sugary Consumables

## Definition

An informal expression for small sugary consumables, likely including sweet drinks and minor sugar-based treats. The term identifies everyday indulgences as targets for consumption restraint because they may function as gradual or chronic sources of harm.

## Key Characteristics

- Refers to low-scale, everyday sugary consumption rather than nutrition in general.
- Includes sweet drinks and small sugar-based treats.
- Connects minor indulgence with desire control and gradual harm.
- Is framed as a specific target of restraint.

## Applications

- Identifying small habitual purchases or consumables to reduce.
- Supporting personal efforts to control cravings and discretionary consumption.
- Describing the cumulative risks of seemingly minor indulgences.
