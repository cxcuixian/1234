# Psychology

## Definition

Psychology is the field that studies behavior, cognition, emotion, and the mental processes underlying human judgment and decision-making. In the source, it is presented as one of several fields needed to examine decisions from multiple perspectives.

## Key Characteristics

- Examines how mental processes and emotions influence behavior.
- Identifies predictable judgment errors and behavioral distortions.
- Includes mechanisms such as [[Loss aversion]], [[Confirmation bias]], envy, pride, authority effects, and conformity.
- Supports the analysis of learning, incentives, success, and failure.
- Helps explain why people may make systematically biased decisions.

## Applications

Psychology can be applied to recognize and reduce decision-making biases, design more effective [[Incentives]], improve learning, analyze success and failure, and understand how social pressures and emotional responses affect behavior.
