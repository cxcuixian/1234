# Computational Bulldozer

## Definition

Computational Bulldozer is a metaphor for computation functioning as a powerful force that rapidly advances through difficult intellectual or scientific terrain. It characterizes large-scale computation as an engine of progress that can accelerate work beyond what unaided abstract human reasoning can accomplish.

## Key Characteristics

- Metaphorical rather than technically defined.
- Presents computation as a force for overcoming difficult intellectual obstacles.
- Emphasizes the scale and speed of computational progress.
- Connects computation with software engineering, scientific discovery, and higher mathematics.
- Suggests that computation can extend or surpass the practical limits of unaided human reasoning.

## Applications

- Describing how large-scale computation accelerates [[Scientific Research]].
- Characterizing computational methods that enable advances in [[Scientific Discovery]].
- Explaining the role of computation in complex [[Mathematics]] and theoretical work.
- Framing software engineering and computational infrastructure as drivers of large-scale intellectual progress.
- Discussing [[AI]] and other computational systems as tools for exploring difficult problem spaces.
