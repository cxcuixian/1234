# 肩胛引体

## Definition

肩胛引体 is a scapular pull-up technique that emphasizes controlled movement of the shoulder blades before the arms contribute substantially. The movement involves depressing the shoulder blades and then raising them back under control, supporting better pull-up mechanics and directing effort toward the back.

## Key Characteristics

- Focuses on controlled scapular depression and elevation.
- Uses the shoulder blades as the primary focus before adding substantial arm movement.
- Supports lat engagement during pulling exercises.
- Encourages full-range movement.
- Helps maintain space between the shoulders and ears.
- Shifts emphasis from relying only on the arms to engaging the back.

## Applications

- Used as a preparatory drill for [[引体向上]].
- Helps improve scapular control and pulling mechanics.
- Can be incorporated into warm-ups or strength-training routines.
- Supports technique practice for more effective back engagement during pull-ups.
