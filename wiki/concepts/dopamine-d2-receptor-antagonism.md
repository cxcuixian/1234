# Dopamine D2 receptor antagonism

## Definition

Dopamine D2 receptor antagonism is the blockade of dopamine D2 receptors by a drug. This mechanism is a major reason that typical antipsychotics commonly cause [[concepts/extrapyramidal-symptoms|extrapyramidal symptoms]]. [[entities/haloperidol|Haloperidol]] and [[entities/fluphenazine|fluphenazine]] are typical antipsychotics frequently associated with these effects.

## Key Characteristics

- Reduces dopaminergic signaling through dopamine D2 receptors.
- Is strongly associated with extrapyramidal symptoms produced by many typical antipsychotics.
- Can contribute to [[concepts/dystonia|dystonia]], [[concepts/akathisia|akathisia]], [[concepts/parkinsonism|parkinsonism]], pseudoparkinsonism, and [[concepts/tardive-dyskinesia|tardive dyskinesia]].
- Is particularly relevant to dopamine signaling in the [[concepts/nigrostriatal-pathway|nigrostriatal pathway]].
- Contrasts with many atypical antipsychotics, which generally have lower D2 receptor affinity or greater [[concepts/serotonin-5-ht2a-receptor-affinity|serotonin 5-HT2A receptor affinity]] and therefore lower rates of extrapyramidal symptoms.
- Treatment and monitoring may involve [[concepts/anticholinergic-medications|anticholinergic medications]], rating scales for extrapyramidal symptoms, or other movement-disorder interventions.

## Applications

Dopamine D2 receptor antagonism is used to explain the adverse-effect profiles of typical and atypical antipsychotics in clinical pharmacology. It supports the assessment of medication-related movement disorders, including acute dystonia, oculogyric crisis, akathisia, parkinsonism, and tardive dyskinesia. The mechanism is also relevant when interpreting findings from [[entities/catie-trial|clinical trials]] and when selecting or evaluating treatments such as [[entities/procyclidine|procyclidine]], [[entities/benztropine|benztropine]], [[entities/propranolol|propranolol]], [[entities/amantadine|amantadine]], [[entities/tetrabenazine|tetrabenazine]], and [[entities/deutetrabenazine|deutetrabenazine]].
