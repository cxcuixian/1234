# 中国通缩

## Definition

中国通缩 refers to deflationary pressure in China, characterized by weakness in prices and economic demand. The source presents it as part of a short macroeconomic comparison with U.S. inflationary pressure.

## Key Characteristics

- Involves downward or weak price dynamics.
- Is associated with subdued economic demand.
- Is contrasted with [[美国通胀压力]] in a cross-country macroeconomic comparison.
- Is treated as relevant to portfolio construction despite the absence of statistical evidence in the source.
- Supports attention to income generation and risk management in investment decisions.

## Applications

中国通缩 is used as a macroeconomic context for evaluating investment preferences. In the source, the contrast between Chinese deflationary pressure and U.S. inflationary pressure supports a preference for high-dividend stocks and frames the importance of income and risk management. It may also inform comparisons involving [[美债收益率]], [[景气行业]], and [[无风险套利]].
