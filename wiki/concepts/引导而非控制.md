# 引导而非控制

## Definition

**引导而非控制** (Guidance Rather Than Control) is an interpersonal principle that favors influence, respect, trust, and voluntary choice over coercion or possession. It emphasizes making kind requests rather than issuing commands and recognizing that individuals retain agency over their own decisions, including health-related choices.

## Key Characteristics

- Prioritizes guidance and influence over coercion.
- Respects other people’s autonomy and voluntary choices.
- Encourages trust rather than possessiveness.
- Favors kind requests instead of commands.
- Treats vulnerable people with dignity and respect.
- Applies the same principle of care to oneself and to others.
- Recognizes that health-related decisions, including smoking, belong to the individual concerned.

## Applications

- **Family relationships:** Supporting autonomy while offering guidance and care.
- **Caregiving:** Helping vulnerable people make informed choices without treating them as possessions.
- **Interpersonal communication:** Replacing commands and pressure with respectful requests.
- **Health-related conversations:** Discussing behaviors such as smoking without attempting to control another person’s decisions.
- **Supportive relationships:** Building trust by combining concern with respect for personal agency.
