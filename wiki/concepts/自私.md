# Selfishness

## Definition

Selfishness is the tendency to place one’s own interests ahead of obligations to other people or to the group. In the source, it appears as a negative personal quality contrasted with loyalty, valuable people, and stable teams. The concept suggests that self-centered behavior can undermine collective stability, cooperation, and trust.

## Key Characteristics

- Prioritizes personal interests over shared responsibilities.
- Conflicts with loyalty and mutual commitment.
- Can weaken trust and cooperation within a group.
- May undermine the stability of teams and relationships.
- Is treated as a character weakness rather than formally defined in the source.

## Applications

Selfishness can be used to analyze behavior in teams, partnerships, friendships, and other cooperative settings. It helps describe situations in which an individual pursues personal gain at the expense of group stability, reliable relationships, or obligations to others. It can also support reflection on character, trust, and the qualities required to build stable teams.
