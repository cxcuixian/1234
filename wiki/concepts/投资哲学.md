# Investment Philosophy

## Definition

投资哲学 is a long-term investing framework centered on accepting failure, rejecting leverage, carefully evaluating value, and maintaining patience. It distinguishes between a good company and a cheaply priced company, while recognizing that apparently cheap assets may carry significant costs or risks in other forms.

## Key Characteristics

- Accepts that investment decisions can fail and treats failure as part of the process.
- Rejects leverage as a source of avoidable financial risk.
- Evaluates valuation using measures such as [[市净率]].
- Examines [[定价权]] and [[长期竞争力]] when assessing business quality.
- Distinguishes between business quality and purchase price.
- Recognizes that low apparent prices may conceal operational, competitive, or financial costs.
- Supports long-term investment in [[成长股]] when growth and competitiveness justify the valuation.
- Emphasizes [[耐心]] and the willingness to wait for attractive opportunities.
- Is directly associated with the investment examples of [[巴菲特]] and [[伯克希尔]].

## Applications

- Assessing whether a high-quality business is available at a reasonable price.
- Comparing asset valuation with business durability and competitive position.
- Using [[市净率]] and other valuation measures as inputs rather than complete decision rules.
- Evaluating whether a company’s [[定价权]] can support long-term returns.
- Avoiding leveraged investments when the downside risk is difficult to control.
- Deciding when to hold cash and wait rather than invest in an unattractive opportunity.
- Analyzing [[成长股]] through the relationship between expected growth, valuation, and [[长期竞争力]].
