# 性兴奋

## Definition

性兴奋 is a bodily and emotional state of sexual arousal involving increased desire, genital lubrication and swelling, warmth, and willingness to continue sexual activity. It is distinct from consent: physiological responses such as wetness do not authorize every sexual act. Sexual arousal develops gradually and is influenced by touch, emotional safety, affection, hormones, medication, temperature, stress, and relaxation.

## Key Characteristics

- May involve increased sexual desire, genital lubrication, swelling, warmth, and sensitivity.
- Develops gradually rather than occurring as an instantaneous or uniformly predictable response.
- Can be affected by touch, affection, perceived safety, hormones, medication, temperature, stress, and relaxation.
- Physiological arousal does not itself constitute consent.
- May provide a physiological rationale for extended [[前戏]] and the use of [[润滑]].
- Readiness for penetration should be communicated through [[语言确认]] and should not be inferred solely from physical responses.
- Arousal may be influenced by autonomic nervous system activity, including [[交感神经]] and [[副交感]] responses.

## Applications

- Sexual communication: distinguish bodily arousal from explicit agreement and use [[语言确认]] before continuing or changing sexual activity.
- Preparation for penetration: allow sufficient time for arousal and lubrication, consider [[前戏]], and wait until the partner communicates readiness.
- Sexual health education: explain why wetness, swelling, or warmth are not permission for every sexual act.
- Addressing performance concerns: recognize that stress, pressure, and [[表现焦虑]] can affect arousal and may contribute to [[场景性勃起困难]].
- Safer sexual practice: combine arousal and readiness with [[安全套]] use and communication about [[怀孕或传染]] risks.
- Anatomical education: distinguish external vulvar responses from vaginal anatomy, including [[外阴 ≠ 阴道]], [[阴蒂]], [[阴道口]], [[阴道]], and [[会阴]].
