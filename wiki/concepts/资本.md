# 资本

## Definition

资本 is a productive financial resource that can continue working on behalf of its owner. In personal finance, it is associated with avoiding debt, restraining consumption, and benefiting from compound growth over the long term.

## Key Characteristics

- Functions as a productive resource rather than merely as stored wealth.
- Can generate returns continuously for its owner.
- Supports long-term accumulation through [[复利]].
- Is associated with debt avoidance and [[克制消费]].
- Treats financial simplicity as more reliable and less emotionally demanding than labor or discretionary spending.
- Forms part of a broader strategy of [[控制自己的欲望]] and sustained wealth building.

## Applications

- Saving and investing surplus income instead of relying on debt.
- Building a long-term portfolio designed to benefit from compound growth.
- Using financial assets to reduce dependence on continuous labor.
- Applying disciplined consumption habits to increase investable capital.
- Structuring personal finances around simplicity, patience, and accumulation.
