# Practicality

## Definition

Practicality is the principle of prioritizing usefulness, problem-solving effectiveness, and real-world applicability when evaluating ideas, products, workflows, and engineering approaches. It favors solving concrete problems and enabling effective action over abstract positioning or unnecessary complexity.

It favors concrete utility over abstract novelty, status signaling, or superficial technological enthusiasm.
## Key Characteristics

- Prioritizes usefulness and outcomes over abstract positioning.
- Evaluates ideas and products by their ability to address real problems.
- Encourages clear expression of requirements so tools and collaborators can act effectively.
- Supports disciplined allocation of time, resources, and engineering effort.
- Connects practical execution with [[系统工程]], [[工作流]], and [[ai创业]].
- Places practical problem-solving before clear requirements expression, product development, and AI-related business activity.
## Applications

- **AI entrepreneurship:** Focus product development on genuine user problems and measurable utility.
- **Workflows:** Define requirements clearly so people and tools can execute tasks with minimal ambiguity.
- **Resource allocation:** Direct time, funding, and technical effort toward work with practical value.
- **Engineering:** Avoid unnecessary complexity and favor maintainable solutions that solve the intended problem.
- **Collaboration:** Use precise requirements and actionable goals to improve coordination.
- **Implementation:** Apply the standard to controllability, performance, implementation, workflow standardization, and converting abstract ideas into concrete actions.
