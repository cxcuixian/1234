# Labia minora

## Definition

The labia minora are the thinner inner pair of skin folds located inside the [[大阴唇|labia majora]] and forming part of the external genital anatomy. Their color and thickness vary naturally. During sexual arousal, increased blood flow may make the folds fuller and more sensitive.

## Key Characteristics

- They are inner folds of the external genitalia, distinct from the vagina.
- Their size, shape, color, and thickness vary considerably between individuals.
- Both their inner and outer surfaces may be sensitive to touch.
- Increased blood flow during arousal can cause temporary fullness.
- Gentle, wet, and smooth stimulation is generally preferable to dry rubbing.
- Sensitivity and preferred stimulation vary between individuals and should be guided by comfort and consent.

## Applications
