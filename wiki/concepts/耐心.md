# Patience

## Definition

耐心 is the capacity to delay action until conditions become more favorable or sufficiently confirmed. In this context, it is treated as an investment capability rather than merely a personality trait: waiting preserves the ability to respond to future opportunities, allows a price decline or rebound to develop, and reduces impulsive purchases of [[中国巨石]].

## Key Characteristics

- Treats waiting as an active form of strength and investment discipline.
- Avoids entering a position solely because an opportunity appears immediately available.
- Allows time for price declines, rebounds, and technical signals to develop.
- Emphasizes confirmation, including movement around [[长期均线]] and signs such as [[均线拐头向上]].
- Helps prevent impulsive purchases and reduces the risk of entering before a [[破线]] or rebound has been adequately confirmed.
- Recognizes that investment opportunities will continue to appear, making immediate entry unnecessary.

## Applications

Patience can be applied when evaluating a potential purchase of [[中国巨石]], particularly after a sharp decline or near a possible technical breakdown. An investor may wait for a clearer rebound, confirmation around [[长期均线]], or a suitable [[尾盘买入]] opportunity instead of reacting to short-term price movement. This principle also supports disciplined timing when assessing [[买入机会]], [[大跌]], and the development of [[超额利润]].
