# Customer Certification

## Definition

Customer certification is a business and production step in which a customer validates or approves a product, process, or supplier. It serves as a practical gate between technical preparation—such as working from formulas and rewriting documentation—and stable mass production.

## Key Characteristics

- Involves customer validation or approval of a product, process, or supplier.
- Occurs after technical preparation and documentation refinement.
- Precedes [[稳量产|stable mass production]] and reliable delivery.
- Functions as a practical transition from technical readiness to commercial or production readiness.
- Requires personal verification because AI-generated conclusions may be false or unreliable.
- Connects technical work with customer confidence and operational execution.

## Applications

- Validating a product before moving into stable mass production.
- Approving a supplier or manufacturing process for ongoing use.
- Confirming that rewritten procedures and formula-based work meet customer requirements.
- Establishing a customer-accepted baseline for reliable delivery.
- Personally checking AI-assisted analyses before using them in customer-facing or production decisions.
