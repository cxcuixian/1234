# Gravity

## Definition

Gravity is a physical force invoked as a constraint that operates independently of human intention. In the source context, it functions as an analogy for downward market movement and the limits of wishful thinking: emotional conviction cannot reverse an adverse direction when underlying forces are already acting.

## Key Characteristics

- Operates independently of personal intention or belief.
- Represents an unavoidable directional force or constraint.
- Illustrates why emotional conviction cannot override adverse underlying conditions.
- Reinforces recognition of risk, disciplined selling, and realistic expectations.
- Emphasizes the distinction between understanding a force and taking appropriate action.

## Applications

Gravity serves as an analogy for market downside movement, particularly when investors continue holding declining securities because of hope or emotional attachment. It supports the practical application of [[Holding Discipline]] and [[Understanding and Action]] by emphasizing the need to recognize adverse forces and respond through disciplined risk management. The analogy is especially relevant to [[Retail Investors]], [[Penny Stocks]], and [[Selling and Risk Exit]].
