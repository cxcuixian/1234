# 会阴

- Located between the [[阴道口]] and [[肛门]].
- May be included in gentle, gradual external stimulation.
- Requires attention to the partner’s comfort, response, and [[同意]].
- Should not be treated as an entrance or penetrated indiscriminately.
- Its treatment reflects the broader principles of gentle exploration, communication, and avoiding [[表现焦虑]].
- May be considered alongside other external regions, including the [[外阴 ≠ 阴道]], [[阴阜]], [[大阴唇]], [[小阴唇]], and [[阴蒂包皮]].
