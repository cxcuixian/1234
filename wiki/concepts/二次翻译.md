# Intermediate Translation

## Definition

Intermediate translation is the step of converting English into another familiar language before understanding its meaning. In English learning, this process is treated as an obstacle to developing direct and natural comprehension of English.

## Key Characteristics

- Inserts a familiar-language translation step between English input and comprehension.
- Can slow down understanding and encourage dependence on the learner’s first language.
- Removing this step is presented as a central principle of effective English learning.
- Requires regular practice and gradual adaptation to English-language input.
- Emphasizes direct comprehension rather than word-for-word mental translation.

## Applications

Intermediate translation is relevant to English-learning methods that aim to build direct comprehension and more natural language processing. Learners can reduce reliance on it through regular exposure, repeated practice, and gradual adjustment to English without mentally converting every expression into another language.
