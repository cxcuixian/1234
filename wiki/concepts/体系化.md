# 体系化

## Definition

体系化 is the method of organizing knowledge into a coherent, interconnected structure rather than accumulating disconnected facts. It involves linking ideas, using known information to infer the unknown, and reconstructing understanding from first principles. This approach makes accumulated knowledge more useful for reasoning, learning, and practical application.

## Key Characteristics

- Organizes isolated facts into an interconnected knowledge structure.
- Emphasizes relationships between concepts and the transfer of understanding across domains.
- Uses known information to infer and investigate the unknown.
- Encourages reasoning from first principles and reconstructing ideas independently.
- Connects mathematics, science, and practical applications.
- Treats learning and thinking as cumulative disciplines rather than passive information collection.
- Improves the usefulness, retrievability, and applicability of accumulated knowledge.

## Applications

- Building a structured personal knowledge system instead of maintaining disconnected notes.
- Reconstructing mathematical and scientific ideas to develop deeper understanding.
- Applying foundational principles to unfamiliar problems.
- Connecting abstract concepts with business, engineering, and other practical contexts.
- Using relationships among known ideas to generate new hypotheses or deductions.
- Developing independent thinking through deliberate reconstruction and application.
