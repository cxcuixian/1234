# 核心竞争力

## Definition

核心竞争力 is a distinctive and durable capability that enables an individual, company, or industry participant to create sustained value. It can support [[定价权]] and reduce dependence on temporary narratives, superficial results, or easily copied procedures. The concept emphasizes understanding why something works rather than merely reproducing visible actions or outcomes.

## Key Characteristics

- Creates durable value rather than short-term results.
- Is difficult for competitors to copy or replace.
- Supports the development of [[定价权]].
- Depends on underlying mechanisms and causal understanding.
- Helps distinguish enduring capability from temporary market narratives.
- Favors companies with leading positions in prosperous industries.
- Can be evaluated through the interaction of industry position, operational capability, and long-term results.

## Applications

- **Investment analysis:** Identifying companies whose leading positions and capabilities can sustain value creation, consistent with [[投资家思维]] rather than [[散户思维]].
- **Business strategy:** Determining which organizational capabilities justify investment, differentiation, and pricing power.
- **Industry analysis:** Assessing whether a participant’s position within [[行业的上游，中游，下游]] reflects a durable advantage.
- **Technology evaluation:** Distinguishing genuine capability from temporary enthusiasm surrounding [[人工智能]] or other emerging technologies.
- **Research and engineering:** Understanding mechanisms through [[科学研究]], [[数值分析]], and [[控制工程]] rather than copying procedures without understanding their causes.
