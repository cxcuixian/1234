# 稳量产

## Definition

稳量产 is the method of achieving stable, repeatable mass production rather than merely completing a one-off prototype. It follows [[客户认证]] and centers on formulas, process control, and rewritten documentation so that consistent results can be delivered at scale.

## Key Characteristics

- Emphasizes repeatability and consistent output.
- Follows customer certification and verification.
- Uses formulas and standardized, rewritten documentation to control the process.
- Converts practical knowledge into dependable execution.
- Requires checking, process refinement, and the ability to reproduce results at scale.

## Applications

- Transitioning a validated prototype into reliable commercial production.
- Establishing production procedures after [[客户认证]].
- Rewriting undocumented operational knowledge into repeatable processes.
- Verifying that products or services can be delivered consistently across production cycles.
- Supporting scalable execution in contexts influenced by [[体系化]], [[知识联系网]], and [[复利的力量]].
