# Long-Term Moving Average

## Definition

Long-term moving average refers to a moving-average indicator used as a long-term technical reference in stock trading. In the source discussion, a purchase in [[中国巨石]] is considered only after the stock stabilizes above this indicator, rather than immediately buying during a volatile rebound.

## Key Characteristics

- Provides a long-term reference for evaluating price direction and trend stability.
- Encourages confirmation that the stock has stabilized above the indicator before buying.
- Reflects patience and disciplined timing rather than impulsive participation in a rebound.
- Is associated with identifying a genuine [[买入机会]] and confirming that the trend is turning upward.
- Can help distinguish a sustained recovery from a temporary move after a [[大跌]] or [[破线]] event.

## Applications

Long-term moving average can be applied as a confirmation condition in stock-trading decisions. For [[中国巨石]], the discussed approach is to wait until the price stabilizes above the indicator before considering a purchase. This approach supports [[耐心]], complements [[均线拐头向上]] as a trend signal, and may be used alongside [[尾盘买入]] when evaluating whether a late-session entry represents a sufficiently confirmed opportunity.
