# Chemical Etching

## Definition

Chemical etching, also known as 化学腐蚀, is a chemical process used to selectively remove material during printed circuit board fabrication. It transfers a circuit pattern onto a conductive layer after photoresist application, circuit masking, and development.

## Key Characteristics

- Selectively removes exposed conductive material through chemical reactions.
- Functions as a central pattern-transfer step in the PCB manufacturing process.
- Belongs to the subtractive manufacturing route.
- Involves a practical trade-off between manufacturing cost and production yield.
- The supplied source does not specify particular etchants or process parameters.

## Applications

Chemical etching is used in PCB fabrication to form conductive traces and other circuit features after pattern definition. It supports subtractive manufacturing workflows and influences process economics through its relationship with manufacturing cost and yield.
