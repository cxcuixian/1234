# 庄家

## Definition

庄家 refers to a powerful market participant or coordinated group believed to possess sufficient capital, information, or trading influence to affect a stock’s price and trading dynamics. The term describes an asymmetry between concentrated market power and dispersed individual traders. In the source, the 庄家 of 华电辽能 is accused of repeatedly extracting money from retail investors through speculative and psychologically exploitative market behavior.

## Key Characteristics

- Represents concentrated influence over a security’s price or trading activity.
- Is commonly contrasted with dispersed individual traders such as [[散户]].
- Is associated with speculation, market manipulation allegations, and crowd behavior.
- May exploit human tendencies including fear, greed, loss aversion, and the desire to recover losses.
- Is often discussed through perceived trading patterns rather than independently verifiable ownership or coordination.

## Applications

The term is used to analyze suspected price manipulation, dominant trading behavior, and conflicts between powerful market participants and retail investors. It can also be applied when discussing speculative strategies such as [[低开抄底]], investor reactions to losses, and the behavioral mechanisms described by [[损失厌恶]]. In relation to [[华电辽能]], it characterizes the alleged market force blamed for repeatedly profiting at the expense of retail traders.
