# 趋势指标

## Definition

趋势指标 is a practical market-reading tool for interpreting stock-price movement and selecting an appropriate trading rhythm. It emphasizes the relationship between buying and selling pressure, trading volume, and the recognition that no stock rises or falls indefinitely.

## Key Characteristics

- Interprets price movement through the balance between buying and selling pressure.
- Treats rising prices as associated with buying that exceeds selling.
- Uses trading volume as an important liquidity and confirmation signal.
- Warns against trading stocks with little or no trading volume.
- Supports timing decisions and the selection of an appropriate trading rhythm.
- Rejects the assumption that any stock will rise forever or fall forever.

## Applications

- Assessing whether a price movement is supported by sufficient buying activity.
- Filtering out illiquid stocks with inadequate trading volume.
- Adjusting entry, holding, and exit timing to the observed market trend.
- Combining trend interpretation with disciplined loss recognition and broader analytical principles.
