# 三好一凑合

## Definition

三好一凑合 is a relationship-evaluation shorthand used by the source. It describes a partner whose emotional condition, temperament, and values are good, while appearance is merely acceptable. The phrase distinguishes essential relational qualities from preferences that may reasonably be compromised.

## Key Characteristics

- Prioritizes emotional condition, temperament, and values in partner evaluation.
- Treats appearance as a negotiable preference rather than an essential criterion.
- Encapsulates a broader compatibility assessment involving family background, money, conflict, travel, co-working, cohabitation, and hardship.
- Encourages evaluation based on long-term relationship quality rather than superficial attractiveness.
- Functions as a concise heuristic rather than a universal relationship standard.

## Applications

三好一凑合 can be used as a shorthand when evaluating romantic compatibility and deciding which partner characteristics are fundamental versus negotiable. It may also support structured discussions about expectations, trade-offs, and long-term resilience in relationships, especially alongside considerations such as financial conditions, conflict management, shared living, and facing hardship together.
