# Sense of Participation

## Definition

Sense of participation is the subjective understanding and involvement that emerges through directly performing a process. It emphasizes embodied experience: people cannot fully perceive or understand this dimension through abstract discussion alone without participating in the work themselves.

## Key Characteristics

- Arises through direct involvement in practical work.
- Combines subjective understanding with active participation.
- Connects experience with [[Systematization and Standardization]], [[Workflow]], and [[Turning Abstraction into Concrete Form]].
- Is strengthened by planning, taking action, and documenting ideas in writing.
- Distinguishes embodied knowledge from purely theoretical discussion.

## Applications

- Designing work processes that allow participants to learn through practice.
- Improving workflows through direct observation and hands-on experience.
- Converting abstract ideas into concrete plans, actions, and written procedures.
- Supporting systematization and standardization by grounding them in operational experience.
- Evaluating whether a process is genuinely understood by examining practical involvement.
