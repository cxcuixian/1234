# 幸福

## Definition

幸福 is a personal and family-oriented value used to evaluate whether daily activity, work, and responsibility are meaningful. It contrasts purposeful effort with being busy without understanding the purpose of that activity, while emphasizing family, persistence, and acceptance of imperfection.

## Key Characteristics

- Serves as a criterion for judging whether activity and responsibility have meaningful direction.
- Connects personal well-being with family priorities.
- Places family above competing demands.
- Recognizes that meaningful effort does not require perfection.
- Contrasts purposeful living with unreflective busyness.
- Encourages reflection on the purpose behind work and daily commitments.

## Applications

幸福 can be applied when:

- Evaluating whether work and daily activity support meaningful personal goals.
- Balancing professional responsibilities with family priorities.
- Reflecting on whether busyness has a clear and worthwhile purpose.
- Assessing decisions that affect personal well-being and family life.
- Maintaining effort and responsibility despite imperfect results.
