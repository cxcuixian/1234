# 收购

## Definition

收购 refers to the acquisition of one organization or business by another. In this context, it is considered from the perspective of employee outcomes, including financial compensation, payment timing, fairness, personal agency, and the pursuit of benefits that are owed.

## Key Characteristics

- Can be a positive event for employees when it provides meaningful financial compensation or improves their overall outcome.
- Makes employee rewards and the timing of payment especially important.
- Delayed compensation may lose practical value or become effectively worthless.
- Connects acquisition outcomes with fairness and employees’ ability to pursue what they are owed.
- Emphasizes incentives and practical consequences rather than organizational structure or transaction mechanics.

## Applications

- Evaluating whether an acquisition produces a fair and beneficial outcome for employees.
- Assessing the value and timing of employee compensation during an acquisition.
- Designing incentives and retention arrangements connected to acquisition events.
- Identifying situations in which employees need to actively claim promised compensation or benefits.
- Comparing acquisition outcomes based on their effects on employee welfare and agency.
