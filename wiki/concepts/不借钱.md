# 不借钱

## Definition

不借钱 is a personal financial rule of avoiding borrowing money. It treats debt as a potential source of future obligations, stress, reduced flexibility, and difficult-to-reverse mistakes. In the source, it appears alongside avoiding trouble and keeping away from foolish people as part of a broader risk-avoidance strategy.

## Key Characteristics

- Emphasizes avoiding financial obligations created through borrowing.
- Reduces exposure to repayment pressure, interest costs, and interpersonal accountability.
- Supports financial discipline, balance-sheet conservatism, and loss avoidance.
- Functions as a preventive rule rather than a response to financial distress.
- Connects personal financial decisions with a broader strategy of minimizing avoidable risks.

## Applications

- Setting a personal policy against consumer debt or informal borrowing.
- Evaluating whether a purchase should be postponed until sufficient funds are available.
- Limiting financial commitments that could increase stress or reduce future flexibility.
- Applying a conservative risk-management approach to personal finances.
- Supporting discussions of [[避免麻烦]] and [[损失厌恶]] in financial decision-making.
