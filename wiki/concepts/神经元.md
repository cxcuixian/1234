# Neuron

## Definition

A neuron is a functional or logical unit within a network that processes and transmits signals. In the source note, neurons are discussed alongside relays, synapses, information processes, and communication structures, supporting a functional comparison between biological and engineered systems.

## Key Characteristics

- Functions as a unit for information processing within a network.
- Transmits signals through interconnected communication structures.
- Participates in computation, feedback, and regulation.
- Can be analyzed using principles shared by biological organisms and engineered control systems.
- Relates to network organization, signal transmission, and adaptive behavior.

## Applications

Neurons are studied in neuroscience, artificial intelligence, control engineering, and communication theory. Their organization and signaling mechanisms provide models for computational systems, feedback-based learning, distributed information processing, and the analysis of control and communication in living organisms and machines.
