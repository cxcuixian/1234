# 制造成本和良率

## Definition

制造成本和良率 is the paired evaluation of the economic resources required to manufacture a product and the proportion of manufactured output that meets quality requirements. In advanced PCB and packaging, it requires material choices and fabrication methods to be assessed for both technical performance and production feasibility.

## Key Characteristics

- Combines production economics with the percentage of usable output.
- Evaluates materials, fabrication processes, routing density, and packaging structures together rather than in isolation.
- Connects technical performance with manufacturing scalability and process robustness.
- Helps distinguish purchase price from total cost, including waste, rework, yield loss, and process complexity.
- Is particularly relevant to [[PCB电路板]], [[2.5D封装]], and [[硅中介层]]-based manufacturing decisions.

## Applications

- Comparing advanced PCB materials and fabrication methods.
- Assessing whether increased [[走线密度]] justifies additional manufacturing complexity.
- Evaluating the production viability of [[2.5D封装]] and silicon-interposer designs.
- Estimating the economic impact of material waste, process defects, rework, and unusable output.
- Supporting supply-chain and product decisions where a lower purchase price may not result in a lower [[综合成本]].
- Linking manufacturing decisions to broader considerations such as [[产业链]] structure and [[团队稳定性]].
