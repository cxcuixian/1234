# 占有

## Definition

占有 refers to possessiveness or an attempt to exercise ownership-like control over another person or relationship. It is contrasted with trust and is not an appropriate substitute for confidence, respect, or recognition of personal autonomy.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats another person or relationship as something to possess or control.
- Conflicts with trust, respect, and the other person’s autonomy.
- May contribute to coercive or controlling interpersonal behavior.
- Is contrasted with requesting cooperation rather than issuing commands.
- Should not replace confidence in the other person’s choices or intentions.

## Applications

The concept is useful for analyzing controlling behavior in personal relationships, especially when possessiveness is presented as care, loyalty, or protection. It also applies to communication practices that distinguish respectful requests from coercive commands and that preserve the other person’s right to choose.
