# 能力圈

## Definition

能力圈, or the circle of competence, is the boundary of subjects and decisions that a person understands sufficiently to judge well. Recognizing this boundary encourages humility, supports genuine learning, and helps clarify fundamental problems. Delegating a question to artificial intelligence does not replace personal comprehension.

## Key Characteristics

- Defines the limits of reliable personal understanding and judgment.
- Encourages humility about the boundaries of one’s knowledge.
- Supports focused learning of fundamental problems rather than superficial familiarity.
- Helps distinguish understandable opportunities from fashionable but poorly understood speculation.
- Cannot be expanded merely by asking artificial intelligence to answer questions on one’s behalf.
- Is especially relevant to investment decisions involving unfamiliar technologies.

## Applications

- **Investment analysis:** Remain within one’s circle of competence to evaluate understandable opportunities and avoid poorly understood technology speculation.
- **Decision-making:** Identify whether a question can be judged adequately before committing to a course of action.
- **Learning:** Use awareness of knowledge boundaries to select subjects for deeper study.
- **Use of artificial intelligence:** Treat AI-generated answers as inputs for investigation rather than substitutes for personal understanding.
- **Risk management:** Decline decisions whose key assumptions or mechanisms cannot be explained clearly.
