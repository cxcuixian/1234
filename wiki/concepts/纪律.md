# 纪律

## Definition

纪律 is the behavioral structure that enables a person to act against comfortable, impulsive, or emotionally attractive choices. In financial decision-making, it involves patience, restraint, repeated investment, and maintaining a long-term focus on financial independence.

## Key Characteristics

- Encourages buying during market declines and selling during rises when appropriate.
- Treats each unit of money as a vote for future freedom.
- Emphasizes that spending less can be as important as earning more.
- Requires patience and the ability to resist short-term emotional impulses.
- Supports consistent investment and long-term accumulation.

## Applications

纪律 can be applied to personal finance, investment decisions, budgeting, and long-term planning. It helps individuals maintain investment routines during market volatility, limit unnecessary consumption, and prioritize future financial freedom over immediate comfort.
