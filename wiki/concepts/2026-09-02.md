# 2026-09-02

## Definition

An informal set of observations concerning public figures, taxation, Chinese equities, and restaurant-industry economics. It combines criticism of [[孙宇晨]], concerns about state revenue-seeking and its consequences, short-term investment analysis, and reflections on patience, timing, and the limited profitability of the restaurant market.

## Key Characteristics

- Expresses a strongly negative view of [[孙宇晨]].
- Argues that the state may seek revenue from economic participants without sufficiently considering broader consequences, using [[保护费]] as an interpretive frame.
- Discusses [[中国巨石]] and [[中际]] in relation to short-term price movements, [[长期均线]], [[大跌]], [[破线]], and [[均线拐头向上]].
- Favors waiting for confirmation and a suitable [[买入机会]] rather than purchasing prematurely after a breakdown.
- Emphasizes [[耐心]], recognizing that a stock may require time to recover.
- Describes the [[餐饮市场]] as large and commercially active but generally lacking [[超额利润]].
- Ends with personal regret about having previously told others something and uncertainty about the value of further action.

## Applications

- Provides a framework for discussing cautious entry timing in volatile equity markets.
- Illustrates how technical signals and confirmation can be combined with patience in investment decisions.
- Supports analysis of the difference between market size and profitability in the restaurant industry.
- Records an informal perspective on taxation, state revenue extraction, and perceived economic consequences.
- Preserves a reflective account of communication, regret, and decisions about whether to act further.
