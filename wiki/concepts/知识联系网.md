# 知识联系网

## Definition

知识联系网 describes knowledge as an interconnected network of relationships rather than a flat collection of isolated facts. It emphasizes systematization and the ability to derive unknown conclusions from known premises through connected reasoning.

## Key Characteristics

- Treats knowledge as a structured network of concepts, premises, methods, and conclusions.
- Explains understanding as a process of connecting known information to derive new conclusions.
- Links foundational knowledge with later applications and practical judgments.
- Shows how mathematical formulas, scientific applications, business decisions, and personal decisions can mutually reinforce one another.
- Encourages beginning with foundational principles and developing understanding through connected reasoning.
- Supports [[体系化]] and [[独立思考]] by making relationships among ideas explicit.

## Applications

- **Mathematics and science:** Connecting foundational concepts such as [[二次方程]], [[复数]], and [[抛物线]] to formulas, models, and applications.
- **Business analysis:** Relating [[利润函数]], [[客户认证]], and [[稳量产]] to business judgments and [[优势累积]].
- **Investment and decision-making:** Connecting [[巴菲特]]’s reasoning, [[开市客]] as a business example, and [[人对于大方向的把握]].
- **Personal learning:** Applying [[自己做一遍才是最好的成长方式]] to strengthen links between theory and experience.
- **Reasoning and problem-solving:** Using [[逻辑]], [[体系化]], and [[独立思考]] to derive conclusions from established premises.
- **Long-term development:** Understanding [[复利的力量]], [[顺势而为]], and [[避蠢能力]] as interconnected principles for improving decisions over time.
- **Technology and new fields:** Relating [[ai]] to broader systems of knowledge and reasoning.
