# 能源稳定性

## Definition

能源稳定性 is the ability of an energy system to provide dependable electricity over time, despite fluctuations in generation, demand, and operating conditions.

## Key Characteristics

- Requires consistent electricity generation and delivery over time.
- Is affected by intermittency, including unstable wind generation and nighttime interruptions in solar generation.
- Can be supported by stable generation sources such as nuclear power.
- Depends on balancing mechanisms, including energy storage and [[电网调节]].
- Acts as a practical constraint on data centers, [[GPU服务器]], and other high-technology infrastructure.
- Highlights physical and operational costs that may be underestimated during the expansion of artificial intelligence infrastructure.

## Applications

能源稳定性 is used to evaluate whether an energy system can reliably support data centers, GPU servers, industrial facilities, and other electricity-intensive infrastructure. It is also relevant to comparisons between renewable and stable generation sources, the design of storage systems, grid planning, and assessments of the infrastructure requirements associated with [[人工智能]].
