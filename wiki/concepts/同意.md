# 同意

## Definition

Consent is the voluntary, informed, and ongoing agreement of all participants to engage in a specific sexual activity. It requires mutual willingness, sobriety, and freedom from coercion or deception. Physical arousal or wetness does not constitute permission for any particular act.

Consent is an essential condition for sexual activity and an ongoing, situation-specific process. This discussion applies only to adults who are willing and conscious, with no coercion, deception, monetary exchange, intoxication-based initiation, non-consensual recording, or involvement of minors.

## Key Characteristics

- Applies only to adults who are mutually willing and able to make decisions.
- Must be free from coercion, deception, intoxication-based escalation, non-consensual recording, and any involvement of minors.
- Is specific to each activity and should be confirmed before penetration or other more private activities.
- Requires ongoing verbal and behavioral communication.
- Verbal or behavioral confirmation should be sought before continuing, especially when moving toward penetration or introducing a new activity.
- Physiological responses such as genital wetness or arousal do not authorize every sexual act.
- Can be withdrawn at any time.
- Pain, withdrawal, discomfort, hesitation, or a request to stop must be respected immediately.
- Supports safety, trust, communication, and the decision to slow down or end an activity.

## Applications

- Establishing clear boundaries before and during sexual activity.
- Confirming willingness before penetration or a transition to more private activities.
- Confirming willingness when introducing a new activity.
- Distinguishing physiological responses from actual permission.
- Using clear verbal or behavioral communication to assess whether participants want to continue.
- Responding appropriately when a participant experiences pain, hesitation, withdrawal, or asks to stop.
- Creating safer conditions for communication and reducing pressure related to sexual performance.
