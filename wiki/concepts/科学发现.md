# Scientific Discovery

## Definition

Scientific discovery is the emergence of new scientific knowledge through investigation, problem solving, experimentation, and computation. It may be supported by researchers’ ideas, AI-guided experimentation, software engineering, and computational tools that reveal previously unknown patterns or results rather than merely automating routine work.

## Key Characteristics

- Produces new knowledge, explanations, or understanding.
- Emerges through investigation, experimentation, problem solving, and computation.
- Can be supported by human researchers, artificial intelligence, software engineering, and mathematical methods.
- May involve computational tools that generate or reveal discoveries internally.
- Extends human exploration of the unknown through mathematics and abstraction.

## Applications

Scientific discovery applies to research programs that use AI-guided experiments, computational modeling, software systems, and mathematical abstraction to investigate unanswered questions. It can support the identification of new patterns, the development of hypotheses, the exploration of complex systems, and the generation of knowledge beyond routine automation.
