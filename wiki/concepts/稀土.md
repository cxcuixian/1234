# 稀土

## Definition

稀土 refers to a group of chemical elements, generally comprising the lanthanides together with scandium and yttrium, that are important inputs for advanced materials, electronics, permanent magnets, and other industrial technologies.

## Key Characteristics

- Includes elements such as neodymium, dysprosium, and terbium.
- Supports the production of high-strength permanent magnets.
- Has strategic importance because supply chains and processing capacity are concentrated in specific regions.
- Connects materials science with advanced manufacturing, electronics, energy, communications, and industrial policy.
- Is discussed primarily as a strategic industrial-materials category rather than as a detailed chemistry topic.

## Applications

稀土 is used in high-strength permanent magnets, electric motors, wind turbines, consumer electronics, communications equipment, defense technologies, and other advanced industrial products. Its production and processing also influence downstream supply chains, technology manufacturing, and resource-security strategies.
