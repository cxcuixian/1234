# Permanent Loss of Capital

## Definition

Permanent loss of capital is the lasting destruction or impairment of an asset’s fundamental value, as opposed to temporary market volatility or short-term price fluctuations. The concept emphasizes evaluating whether an asset can suffer an irreversible decline in value.

## Key Characteristics

- Distinguishes permanent impairment from temporary market-price changes.
- Requires a reasonable valuation, even when investing in value stocks.
- Encourages investors to reduce uncertainty where possible.
- Focuses on fundamental business and asset quality rather than short-term quotations.
- Supports deep understanding, patience, and disciplined decision-making.

## Applications

Investors can use this concept when assessing whether a low-priced asset is genuinely undervalued or instead exposed to permanent deterioration. It is also useful for evaluating business quality, financial resilience, competitive position, and the risks that could permanently impair an investment. The concept encourages investors to remain patient during temporary volatility while acting decisively when fundamental value is at risk.
