# Team Stability

## Definition

Team stability is a practical criterion for evaluating whether an organization or supplier can reliably complete its commitments over time. It connects the continuity and credibility of a team with execution quality, total cost, and the justification for paying a higher quoted price.

## Key Characteristics

- Focuses on whether work can actually be completed, rather than relying only on reputation or price comparisons.
- Treats team continuity and execution capability as indicators of organizational reliability.
- Helps explain why a higher price may be justified when it reduces delivery and coordination risks.
- Supports long-term trust between customers, suppliers, and other partners.
- Is especially relevant when evaluating suppliers whose performance depends on specialized knowledge and sustained collaboration.

## Applications

Team stability can be used when assessing suppliers, contractors, technology partners, or organizations responsible for complex and long-term work. In the critique of [[Starry Sky Co., Ltd.]], it functions as a practical basis for comparing credibility and execution capability with reputation and low quoted prices. It also contributes to evaluating [[Total Cost]], since unstable teams may create delays, rework, coordination expenses, and replacement costs.
