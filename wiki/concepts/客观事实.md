# Objective Facts

## Definition

Objective facts are verifiable information used as a preferred basis for evaluating claims, making decisions, and explaining outcomes. They provide an alternative to judgment driven by bias, authority, conformity, or emotion.

## Key Characteristics

- Relies on evidence that can be independently examined or verified.
- Supports impartial evaluation of claims and decisions.
- Is considered alongside [[Probability]] and [[Incentives]] to explain why outcomes occur.
- Helps reduce the influence of bias, authority, conformity, and emotionally driven judgment.
- Encourages broad reading, comparison of perspectives, and resistance to [[Confirmation Bias]].

## Applications

- Evaluating competing claims using evidence rather than authority or popularity.
- Making decisions with reference to observable conditions and likely outcomes.
- Comparing [[Multiple Perspectives]] to identify assumptions and reduce one-sided reasoning.
- Investigating why events or outcomes occur by considering facts, probabilities, and incentives.
- Challenging conclusions that appear to reflect [[Bias]] or emotional judgment.
