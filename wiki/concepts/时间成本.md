# Time Cost

## Definition

Time cost is the value consumed or lost when time is spent on low-impact activities instead of more productive or valuable alternatives. It includes both wasted personal time and the opportunity cost of delaying or misallocating capital.

## Key Characteristics

- Arises when time is spent without producing meaningful progress or value.
- Often appears as repeated discussion, complaint, or activity that does not change outcomes.
- Reflects the opportunity cost of choosing one use of time over another.
- In investing, is connected with leverage, patience, waiting, and long-term competitiveness.
- Can be overlooked when people complain about lacking time while simultaneously wasting it.

## Applications

- **Personal productivity:** Identifying repetitive discussions, ineffective meetings, and low-impact tasks.
- **Decision-making:** Comparing the value of an activity with the alternatives that time could support.
- **Investing:** Evaluating the cost of delayed returns, prolonged holding periods, and inefficient capital allocation.
- **Long-term strategy:** Recognizing that patience and waiting can be valuable when they support durable competitive advantages.
