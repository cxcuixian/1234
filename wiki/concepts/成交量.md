# Trading Volume

## Definition

Trading volume is the amount of buying and selling activity associated with a stock during a given period. The source presents sufficient volume as a practical prerequisite for considering a stock, since low-volume positions may lack enough market activity to produce a timely price increase.

## Key Characteristics

- Measures the level of buying and selling activity in a stock.
- Helps indicate whether a position has sufficient market participation.
- Low volume can delay price movement and make an investment of time inefficient.
- The concept reflects a skeptical view of retail stock-market performance.
- Entering an illiquid stock is framed as a waste of time rather than merely as a higher-risk decision.

## Applications

Trading volume can be used as a preliminary screening criterion when evaluating stocks. Investors may exclude low-volume positions to avoid markets where insufficient participation could delay price appreciation or make the investment thesis difficult to realize.
