# 控制情绪

## Definition

控制情绪 is a practical discipline for regulating emotional reactions so that financial and life decisions remain deliberate rather than impulsive. It is especially important in investing, where greed, fear, and anxiety can distort judgment.

## Key Characteristics

- Reduces impulsive decisions driven by greed, fear, anxiety, or excitement.
- Encourages patience and deliberate evaluation before acting.
- Supports sound judgment when facing uncertainty or financial pressure.
- Complements avoiding borrowing and refusing to engage with matters one does not understand.
- Emphasizes understanding and self-discipline over cleverness or short-term reactions.

## Applications

- **Investing:** Helps investors avoid panic selling, excessive risk-taking, and decisions driven by market excitement.
- **Personal finance:** Supports disciplined spending and reduces emotionally motivated borrowing or purchasing.
- **Decision-making:** Creates space for analysis before responding to stressful or uncertain situations.
- **Risk management:** Helps prevent avoidable failures by limiting reactions that can lead to serious mistakes.
