# Workflow

## Definition

Workflow is a structured sequence that connects ideas, plans, actions, and tangible records. It turns abstract intentions into concrete steps and links disciplined execution with continuous improvement, participation, and practical problem solving.

## Key Characteristics

- Connects conceptual thinking with planned and performed actions.
- Converts abstractions into concrete, traceable steps through tools such as pen and paper.
- Emphasizes learning through actual execution rather than relying solely on theoretical understanding.
- Supports systematization and standardization through practical work.
- Encourages continuous optimization, participation, and problem solving.
- Produces tangible records that support review and improvement.

## Applications

- Designing repeatable procedures for individual or team work.
- Translating plans and ideas into actionable task sequences.
- Documenting work so that processes can be reviewed, standardized, and improved.
- Supporting system engineering and operational management.
- Facilitating collaborative problem solving and continuous optimization.
- Using handwritten notes or other concrete records to clarify complex work.
