# 计算

## Definition

计算 (computation) is the use of computational resources, methods, and abstractions to explore problems, produce results, and extend human intellectual capacity. In this framing, it operates alongside [[软件工程]] as an enabling infrastructure and a source of leverage for difficult scientific and technical problems.

## Key Characteristics

- Provides large-scale, persistent capacity for exploring problem spaces.
- Can accelerate progress when combined with [[软件工程]].
- Supports internal scientific discovery and the development of abstractions.
- Enables work involving advanced mathematics and complex models.
- Functions both as infrastructure and as an intellectual force.
- Can be viewed through the metaphor of a computational bulldozer: relentless, scalable, and capable of producing results across broad domains.

## Applications

- Accelerating [[科研]] through large-scale simulation, experimentation, and analysis.
- Supporting [[科学发现]] by exploring hypotheses and complex problem spaces.
- Enabling computational approaches in engineering, including tools such as [[ABAQUS]].
- Developing mathematical abstractions and methods for difficult human problems.
- Extending software systems with scalable computational capabilities.
- Providing infrastructure for AI research and other computation-intensive fields.
