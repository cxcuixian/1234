# 机会来临的时候要把握住

## Definition

机会来临的时候要把握住 is a practical principle of recognizing favorable conditions and acting decisively before the opportunity passes. It connects timing, conviction, and execution, while emphasizing that action should be guided by independent judgment and one’s own understanding rather than imitation.

## Key Characteristics

- Encourages decisive action when favorable conditions emerge.
- Links opportunity recognition with timely execution.
- Follows a warning that the market punishes companies without substantive results.
- Emphasizes maintaining one’s own style and independent understanding.
- Balances responsiveness to external conditions with personal conviction.

## Applications

- Making timely business or investment decisions when market conditions become favorable.
- Responding quickly to openings after evaluating risks and expected results.
- Avoiding passive imitation by adapting opportunities to one’s own capabilities and judgment.
- Translating strategic insight into concrete execution before conditions change.
