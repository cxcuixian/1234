# 四看

## Definition

四看 is a compact framework for evaluating a romantic or cohabiting relationship. It assesses compatibility across everyday life and difficult circumstances rather than relying on attraction alone. The framework follows discussion of emotional quality, temperament, values, and acceptable appearance, and extends evaluation to family background, money, conflict, travel, work, finance, cohabitation, and periods of difficulty.

## Key Characteristics

- Evaluates relationship compatibility through multiple dimensions of real life.
- Considers both ordinary circumstances and stressful or difficult situations.
- Places practical compatibility alongside emotional attraction.
- Addresses family background, financial attitudes, conflict handling, work, travel, and cohabitation.
- Complements [[三好一凑合]] and the source’s broader discussion of relationship expectations.
- Encourages observation of behavior and stability over time rather than relying on first impressions.

## Applications

- Evaluating whether a romantic relationship is suitable for long-term commitment.
- Assessing compatibility before marriage or cohabitation.
- Identifying potential disagreements about money, work, family, travel, and daily routines.
- Testing whether partners can manage conflict and periods of low performance or adversity.
- Structuring conversations about values, expectations, finances, and shared responsibilities.
