# 大跌

## Definition

大跌 refers to a substantial decline in a security’s price. It may occur after an important technical level is broken and represents a market event that investors should anticipate and evaluate rationally rather than react to impulsively.

## Key Characteristics

- Involves a sharp and substantial fall in a security’s price.
- May follow the breaking of an important technical level, or [[破线]].
- Does not necessarily indicate permanent weakness; a stock may recover quickly after a sharp decline.
- The source contrasts the resilience of [[中国巨石]] with the weaker condition of [[中际]].
- Encourages patience and disciplined risk assessment rather than emotional selling.
- May create a potential [[买入机会]] when the underlying security remains fundamentally resilient.

## Applications

- Monitoring securities after they break significant technical levels.
- Assessing whether a sharp fall reflects temporary weakness or a deterioration in the underlying investment case.
- Comparing the resilience of [[中国巨石]] with the weaker condition of [[中际]].
- Evaluating whether conditions such as [[均线拐头向上]] or support from a [[长期均线]] indicate possible recovery.
- Applying [[耐心]] when deciding whether to hold, reduce, or add to a position after a major decline.
- Considering market context, including [[餐饮市场]], [[超额利润]], and [[保护费]], when interpreting price weakness.
- Distinguishing a disciplined [[尾盘买入]] decision from an impulsive response to a sell-off.
- Using the discussion dated [[2026-09-02]] as a reference for evaluating this market event.
