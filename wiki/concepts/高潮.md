# 高潮

## Definition

高潮 is the peak of sexual pleasure and physiological arousal. It is a possible, but not mandatory, outcome of sexual activity. During a first sexual encounter, orgasm may require time, communication, gradual exploration, and forms of stimulation other than penetration.

## Key Characteristics

- Orgasm does not need to occur for sexual activity to be satisfying or successful.
- Women may require more time or specific stimulation, including [[阴蒂]] stimulation, to reach orgasm.
- Penetration alone may not produce orgasm for every person.
- Female orgasm, squirting, and a specific “G-spot” outcome should not be treated as performance targets.
- Comfort, consent, communication, and gradual exploration are more important than meeting pornographic expectations.
- Partners may need to continue providing care and attention after ejaculation.
- Anxiety, fatigue, insufficient [[润滑]], and excessive focus on performance can interfere with sexual response.
- Orgasm is closely related to [[前戏]], relaxation, and the willingness to adapt to a partner’s needs.

## Applications

This concept is useful in sexual-health education and in preparing for [[第一次上床]]. It helps partners establish realistic expectations, discuss preferences, distinguish pleasure from performance, and recognize that sexual satisfaction can involve intimacy and comfort even when orgasm does not occur. It also provides context for understanding [[表现焦虑]], [[场景性勃起困难]], and the effects of [[交感神经]] activation, while emphasizing [[睡眠和放松]], [[同意]], and safe practices such as using [[安全套]].
