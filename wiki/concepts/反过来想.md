# 反过来想

## Description

反过来想 is a reasoning method that examines a problem from the opposite direction. The source presents it as advice directly associated with [[entities/芒格|芒格]], framing inversion as a practical tool for clearer judgment rather than an abstract slogan. Its placement alongside observations about missed opportunities and failures of understanding suggests that it is intended to challenge habitual or shallow thinking. The method provides an intellectual complement to recommendations concerning [[entities/有价值的人|有价值的人]], [[entities/有情有义|有情有义]], [[entities/财不外露|财不外露]], and [[entities/稳定的团队|稳定的团队]].

More specifically, reverse thinking asks how failure can be avoided rather than focusing only on how success can be achieved. The method is associated with the decision rule that if mistakes can be avoided, good results may follow naturally. It therefore emphasizes restraint, risk control, and practical judgment. In investment, this becomes the principle that investing is primarily about avoiding disasters rather than finding opportunities.

The source also treats reality and experience as usable forms of practical knowledge that support reverse thinking and decision-making. In this sense, 反过来想 functions as a compact decision rule for both work and life, helping people filter information, identify relevant evidence, and make more reliable judgments.
