# 激励

## Definition

激励 is the set of rewards, motivations, and contextual forces that shape behavior and influence outcomes. It provides a framework for explaining why individuals or organizations pursue particular actions and how those actions contribute to success or failure.

## Key Characteristics

- Connects rewards and motivations with behavioral outcomes.
- Helps explain differences in performance, decisions, and accountability.
- Operates across economic, psychological, organizational, and competitive contexts.
- Can be shaped by pricing, perceived product value, and expected benefits.
- Supports analysis of both successful and unsuccessful outcomes.

## Applications

- Analyzing how rewards and motivations influence individual behavior.
- Evaluating accountability and decision-making within organizations.
- Comparing competitors, such as Microsoft and WPS, where pricing and product value may affect user behavior.
- Designing policies, products, or management practices that encourage desired outcomes.
- Explaining performance differences through economic and psychological factors.
