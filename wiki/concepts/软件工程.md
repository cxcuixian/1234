# 软件工程

## Definition

软件工程 is an organized engineering discipline for designing, developing, deploying, and maintaining useful software systems. In the source context, it is treated as a major component of a broader computational and scientific workflow, working alongside parallel computation to transform ideas and computational capacity into practical systems that support scientific discovery.

## Key Characteristics

- Applies systematic engineering practices to the development and operation of software systems.
- Connects computational capabilities with practical, deployable tools and workflows.
- Supports automation, efficiency, scalability, and reliable execution.
- Works alongside parallel computation as part of a broader scientific and computational workflow.
- Provides infrastructure for AI-guided exploration and the practical implementation of research ideas.

## Applications

Software engineering can support scientific discovery by turning computational methods, AI-generated ideas, and available computing resources into usable systems. Applications include automation of research workflows, development of computational tools, integration with parallel-computing systems, efficient deployment of AI-assisted exploration platforms, and practical implementation of scientific models and simulations such as those used with [[ABAQUS]].
