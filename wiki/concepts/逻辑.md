# Logic

## Definition

Logic is a foundational discipline for structuring reasoning, evaluating evidence, deriving conclusions, and solving problems. It emphasizes clear definitions, reliable facts, valid inference from known information to unknown conclusions, and independent verification.

## Key Characteristics

- Provides a disciplined framework for reasoning and problem solving.
- Relies on precise definitions and basic, verifiable facts.
- Derives unknown information from known premises through valid inference.
- Requires conclusions to be checked independently.
- Connects with syllogisms, geometry, mathematical constants, and classical intellectual history.
- Helps people identify and correct false conclusions, including those produced by AI.
- Supports independent thinking and resistance to avoidable errors.

## Applications

- Analyzing arguments and identifying invalid reasoning.
- Solving mathematical problems, including quadratic equations and geometric problems.
- Building systematic knowledge networks across related fields.
- Verifying AI-generated conclusions before accepting or applying them.
- Supporting business decisions involving profit functions, customer certification, and stable mass production.
- Improving decision-making through independent analysis and awareness of long-term advantages.
