# 反馈

## Definition

反馈 is a method of iterative adjustment based on observed results or behavior. It supports correction, adaptation, and improvement by using information about an action’s outcome to guide subsequent actions.

## Key Characteristics

- Uses observed results or behavior to inform future adjustments.
- Operates through iterative cycles of action, observation, and correction.
- Supports adaptation to changing conditions or responses.
- Applies to both physical behavior and computational learning.
- In neural networks, feedback is associated with weights and the formation of learning patterns.

## Applications

- In table tennis, a player observes an opponent’s actions and changes their own responses accordingly.
- In neural networks, feedback contributes to weight adjustment and the development of learned patterns.
- In broader control and learning processes, feedback helps reduce errors and improve performance over repeated iterations.
