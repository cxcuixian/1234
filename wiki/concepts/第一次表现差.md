# 第一次表现差

## Definition

**第一次表现差** describes awkward, disappointing, or disrupted sexual performance during an initial partnered sexual encounter. It may include losing an erection while putting on a condom, accidentally causing pain, difficulty locating the vaginal entrance, ejaculating quickly, or losing firmness after a partner reports discomfort. The concept frames these outcomes as common situational difficulties rather than evidence of physical weakness, inadequate masculinity, or permanent dysfunction.

## Key Characteristics

- Often occurs during a person’s first partnered sexual experience or another highly pressured initial encounter.
- May involve erection loss, rapid ejaculation, uncertainty about anatomy, accidental painful contact, or difficulty coordinating sexual activity.
- Can be intensified by pornography-based expectations, inexperience, environmental pressure, and excessive self-monitoring.
- May reflect situational activation of the [[交感神经]] system and reduced relaxation, rather than a persistent physiological disorder.
- Does not by itself establish physical weakness, lack of masculinity, or lasting sexual dysfunction.
- A constructive response involves pausing, communicating, returning to affection or [[前戏]], and continuing only when both partners are comfortable.
- Comfort, mutual [[同意]], adequate [[润滑]], and [[语言确认]] are more important than maintaining a fixed performance script.
- Frantic masturbation and self-criticism are presented as unhelpful responses to an initial disappointing experience.

## Applications

- Normalizing common difficulties during a first partnered sexual encounter.
- Distinguishing situational sexual difficulties from persistent conditions such as [[场景性勃起困难]].
- Supporting communication about pain, arousal, pacing, condom use, and whether to continue.
- Guiding couples toward relaxation, affection, and gradual exploration instead of performance monitoring.
- Encouraging accurate anatomical understanding, including the distinction between [[外阴 ≠ 阴道]], and the locations of the [[阴道口]], [[阴蒂]], [[阴蒂包皮]], [[尿道口]], and [[阴道]].
- Reducing unrealistic expectations concerning [[高潮、喷水、G点]], penetration, and ejaculation.
- Promoting safer sexual practices involving [[安全套]], [[油性润滑]], and communication about [[怀孕或传染]].
- Providing context for learning about [[手指进入]], [[口交]], and other activities at a mutually comfortable pace.
