# 概率

## Definition

概率 is a field for representing and evaluating uncertainty. It provides a formal basis for reasoning about possible outcomes, assessing risk, and making decisions under incomplete information. In practical reasoning, it connects quantitative analysis with objective facts, incentives, and the evaluation of success and failure.

## Key Characteristics

- Formalizes uncertainty using quantitative models.
- Supports comparisons between possible outcomes and their likelihoods.
- Helps distinguish objective evidence from subjective judgment.
- Provides a framework for analyzing risk, incentives, success, and failure.
- Complements disciplines such as [[数学]], accounting, economics, and psychology.
- Can improve perspective and reduce cognitive bias, including [[损失厌恶]] and [[确认偏误]].

## Applications

- Evaluating risks and expected outcomes in personal, business, and public decisions.
- Interpreting evidence and distinguishing statistical patterns from anecdotal impressions.
- Assessing the likelihood of success or failure for projects, strategies, and interventions.
- Supporting forecasting, financial analysis, insurance, scientific research, and policy decisions.
- Combining formal reasoning with [[客观事实]] to improve judgment under uncertainty.
