# 保守

## Definition

保守 is a disciplined stance of caution in personal decisions, financial activity, and information processing. It emphasizes risk control, independent thinking, and emotional stability to reduce susceptibility to manipulation, overconfidence, and impulsive action. It does not imply passivity; rather, it combines restraint with continued execution and steady progress.

## Key Characteristics

- Favors caution and downside-risk control in decisions and financial activity.
- Encourages independent thinking instead of relying uncritically on external claims.
- Supports emotional stability and resistance to manipulation.
- Limits overconfidence, impulsive behavior, and excessive exposure to risk.
- Coexists with persistent action, project advancement, and results-oriented execution.
- Treats restraint as a practical discipline rather than avoidance or inactivity.

## Applications

- Making personal decisions under uncertainty by assessing risks before committing.
- Managing investments and financial activity with controlled exposure and realistic expectations.
- Processing information cautiously by checking assumptions, incentives, and evidence.
- Maintaining emotional stability when facing persuasion, hype, or social pressure.
- Advancing projects through consistent execution while avoiding unnecessary risk.
- Preventing confidence, enthusiasm, or opportunism from overriding sound judgment.
