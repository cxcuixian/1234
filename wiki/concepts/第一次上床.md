# 第一次上床

## Definition

第一次上床 refers to a person’s first sexual encounter. It is understood as a situation in which uncertainty, mistakes, and performance concerns are normal. The concept emphasizes preparation, communication, consent, relaxation, lubrication, condom use, and gradual foreplay over physical strength or performance-based expectations.

## Key Characteristics

- Challenges pornography-based expectations about immediate erection, prolonged penetration, and guaranteed orgasm.
- Recognizes that first-time men may experience [[表现焦虑]] or [[场景性勃起困难]].
- Frames sexual activity as collaborative exploration rather than an examination or performance test.
- Emphasizes consent, communication, sleep, relaxation, lubrication, condoms, and gradual [[前戏]].
- Treats bodily responses as variable and affected by psychological and situational factors, including [[交感神经]] activation.
- Encourages realistic expectations about [[阴蒂]], [[外阴]], [[处女膜]], and [[高潮]].

## Applications

- Preparing individuals for a first sexual encounter with realistic expectations.
- Supporting communication about [[同意]], boundaries, contraception, and comfort.
- Reducing performance pressure and addressing situational erectile difficulty.
- Encouraging safer-sex practices, including the use of [[安全套]] and [[润滑]].
- Helping partners approach the encounter as a gradual, cooperative learning experience.
