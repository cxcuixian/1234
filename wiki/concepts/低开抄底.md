# Low-Open Bottom Fishing (低开抄底)

## Definition

低开抄底 is a trading approach in which investors buy a stock after it opens lower, anticipating that the price will rebound. In the source, this behavior is associated with [[华电辽能]] and with the expectation that many people will attempt to buy the stock after a lower opening. The concept describes recurring market behavior rather than a validated investment thesis.

## Key Characteristics

- Involves buying after a stock opens below its previous closing price or expected level.
- Depends on the expectation of a short-term price rebound.
- Can reflect crowd behavior and speculative demand.
- May expose investors to continued declines if the anticipated rebound does not occur.
- Is discussed in connection with broader stock-market risks.

## Applications

低开抄底 may be used by traders seeking short-term opportunities following a weak opening. It can also describe observed investor behavior when analyzing speculative demand, crowd reactions, and risk around [[华电辽能]]. The approach should not be treated as evidence that a rebound is likely or that the underlying investment is fundamentally sound.
