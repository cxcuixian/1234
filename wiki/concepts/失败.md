# 失败

## Definition

失败 is an unsuccessful outcome or a condition in which an intended objective is not achieved. In the source, it is examined in contrast with [[成功]], with emphasis on the diversity of its causes and on learning from recurring harmful behaviors and irreversible major mistakes.

## Key Characteristics

- Failure can result from diverse causes rather than from a single universal mechanism.
- Repeatedly destructive behaviors can increase the likelihood of recurring failure.
- Irreversible major mistakes may produce especially serious consequences.
- Avoiding failure can help identify a personally suitable path toward success.
- The study of failure is connected to learning, incentives, competition, and human biases.
- Comparing unsuccessful and successful outcomes can reveal factors that influence performance.

## Applications

- **Learning and improvement:** Analyze unsuccessful outcomes to identify correctable behaviors, assumptions, and decision-making errors.
- **Risk management:** Avoid recurring destructive patterns and prevent irreversible major mistakes.
- **Personal development:** Use experiences of failure to discover strategies and paths that better fit an individual’s abilities and circumstances.
- **Organizational decision-making:** Examine incentives, competition, and bias when evaluating why projects or initiatives fail.
- **Comparative analysis:** Study failure alongside [[成功]] to distinguish conditions associated with poor and successful outcomes.
