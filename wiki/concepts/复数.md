# 复数

## Definition

A complex number is a mathematical number consisting of a real component and an imaginary component. It extends the real number system and provides solutions to equations that may have no real-number solutions, particularly certain quadratic equations.

## Key Characteristics

- Has the form \(a + bi\), where \(a\) and \(b\) are real numbers and \(i^2 = -1\).
- Extends the real numbers to support solutions for a broader class of algebraic equations.
- Connects algebraic expressions with geometric representations on the complex plane.
- Appears in a progression involving [[二次方程]], [[求根公式]], and completing the square.
- Demonstrates how abstract mathematical concepts can become meaningful through concrete problems and models.

## Applications

Complex numbers are used to solve quadratic equations and to represent transformations geometrically. They also support practical mathematical modeling in areas such as antenna design, headlight design, and trajectory analysis. In this context, complex numbers illustrate how formal mathematics can connect to engineering and other real-world applications.
