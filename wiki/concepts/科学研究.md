# Scientific Research

## Definition

Scientific research is a systematic activity for investigating meaningful questions, interpreting evidence, reassessing knowledge within a discipline, and producing original ideas. It may be transformed by [[Artificial Intelligence]], but effective use requires understanding the underlying reasons and mechanisms rather than blindly generating outputs.

## Key Characteristics

- Focuses on identifying meaningful and consequential problems.
- Relies on data interpretation, disciplined analysis, and deep exploration.
- Produces original ideas, explanations, methods, or evidence.
- Can be reshaped by technological changes such as [[Artificial Intelligence]].
- Requires understanding mechanisms rather than merely operating tools.
- May involve broad reassessment of assumptions and practices across a discipline.
- Treats periods of technological change as possible openings for new scientific opportunities.
- Is connected to [[Numerical Analysis]], [[Control Theory]], [[Control Engineering]], and [[Feedback Learning]].

## Applications

Scientific research is applied to:

- Formulating important questions before selecting analytical or computational tools.
- Interpreting experimental, observational, and simulated data.
- Reassessing theories, methods, and assumptions across an entire discipline.
- Conducting deep investigations into complex natural, technical, or social phenomena.
- Using artificial intelligence to support discovery while maintaining human understanding and critical judgment.
- Developing original hypotheses, models, methods, and explanations.
- Studying relationships among [[Loss Function]], [[Negative Feedback]], [[Steady State]], [[Neurons]], and [[Control and Communication in the Animal and the Machine]].
- Investigating scientific and technological opportunities associated with [[2026-08-24]].
