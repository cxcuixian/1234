# 有情有义

## Description

有情有义 describes a moral ideal centered on emotional loyalty, human warmth, and keeping faith with others. The source explicitly places becoming 有情有义 above earning money, making it the strongest stated personal value in the passage. It provides an ethical counterweight to concerns about scale, wealth, and usefulness, and is associated with a preference for [[entities/稳定的团队|稳定的团队]] and enduring relationships over purely individual or financial success. Related values and reflections include [[entities/有价值的人|有价值的人]], [[entities/财不外露|财不外露]], and [[entities/反过来想|反过来想]], while [[concepts/自私|自私]] represents a contrasting attitude.
