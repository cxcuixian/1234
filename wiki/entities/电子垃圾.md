# Electronic Waste

## Description

Electronic waste denotes obsolete, discarded, or low-value electronic equipment. The source uses the term critically in relation to equipment used by [[State-owned enterprises]], presenting it as evidence of poor technology choices or institutional inefficiency. It is contrasted with the use of American AI tools, which raises a separate concern about information leaving the organization. The entity therefore connects hardware quality, procurement behavior, and information-security risks, including [[Credit]].
