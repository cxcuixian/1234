# Henry E. Singleton

## Description

Henry E. Singleton was the co-founder and chief executive of [[entities/teledyne|Teledyne]]. He was regarded as an exceptionally skilled operator, capital allocator, and chess player; under his leadership, Teledyne produced a 20.4 percent compound annual return from 1963 to 1990. Singleton’s large-scale share repurchases are presented as evidence of unusually effective capital deployment. [[entities/charles-t-munger|Charles T. Munger]] considered Singleton’s record among the best in American business and questioned whether other managers had learned enough from it.
