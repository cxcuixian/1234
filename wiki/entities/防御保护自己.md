# Defensive Self-Protection

## Description

Defensive Self-Protection describes a defensive orientation toward difficult or adverse situations. In the stock-market context, it means protecting oneself when other market participants have more tools and may repeatedly force uncomfortable positions. The phrase emphasizes survival and risk control rather than relying solely on prediction or aggressive action. It can also be understood as a broader personal principle for responding to pressure and uncertainty.
