# 孙宇晨

## Description

孙宇晨 is discussed as an example of [[concepts/机会主义者|opportunistic behavior]], strong [[concepts/营销|marketing]] ability, and problematic conduct. The source advises readers not to become emotionally entangled with or overly concerned about such a person, suggesting that opportunism can eventually undermine the opportunist. A later passage refers to him by the nickname [[entities/孙宇晨|孙割]] and emphasizes his ability to capture online attention. The discussion connects his example with [[concepts/独立思考|independent thinking]], [[concepts/保守|conservatism]], stable temperament, integrity, and avoiding attempts to deceive others.

A later source mentions 孙宇晨 through a highly derogatory and hyperbolic remark, claiming that merely looking at him on the street would cause someone to lose 20 yuan. This passage provides no biographical or professional information and does not establish a relationship between him and the source's later discussion of taxation, stocks, or restaurant economics.
