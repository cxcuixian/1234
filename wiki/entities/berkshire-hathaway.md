# Berkshire Hathaway

## Description

Berkshire Hathaway is the principal business institution through which [[entities/charles-t-munger|Charles T. Munger]] and [[entities/warren-buffett|Warren Buffett]]'s investment philosophy is illustrated. The source emphasizes Berkshire's extraordinary long-term growth, decentralized operating structure, strong reputation, insurance float, conservative culture, and focus on acquiring durable businesses. Its model favors low overhead, long holding periods, limited turnover, rational capital allocation, and managers trusted to operate independently. Berkshire also serves as an investment case study supporting broader ideas about [[concepts/incentives|incentives]], trust, [[concepts/competitive-advantage|competitive advantage]], and disciplined ownership. Because of its size, its future percentage growth is expected to be lower, although its underlying businesses and financial strength remain substantial.
