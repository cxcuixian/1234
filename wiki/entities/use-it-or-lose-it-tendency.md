# Use-It-or-Lose-It Tendency

## Description

The Use-It-or-Lose-It Tendency describes the decline of skills and knowledge when they are not regularly practiced. [[Charles T. Munger]] argues that disuse can erase even abilities once mastered, while maintaining fluency slows deterioration and makes later recovery easier. He recommends regular practice, mental checklists, and simulator-like routines for rarely used but important skills, connecting personal learning discipline with maintenance systems used in aviation and other high-stakes fields. The tendency relates to [[Multiple Mental Models]], [[Circle of Competence]], [[Margin of Safety]], and [[Worldly Wisdom]].
