# Depth

## Description

Depth is a quality associated with greater value, fulfillment, and meaningful engagement in life. The source suggests that happiness depends more on depth than on speed, favoring sustained understanding over rapid movement. Lifelong learning provides a practical way to develop depth over time, while compounding reinforces its benefits through consistent effort. Depth therefore serves as a counterweight to impatience and superficial optimization.
