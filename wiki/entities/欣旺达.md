# 欣旺达

## Description

欣旺达 is presented as a lower-cost, second-ranked alternative to [[宁德时代]] when a market leader’s profits become excessive relative to the market average. The source immediately qualifies this possibility by noting that 欣旺达 has operational weaknesses, including incidents of spontaneous combustion.
