# Overthinking Versus Underacting

## Description

Overthinking versus underacting describes a behavioral imbalance in which extensive deliberation is not converted into learning, experimentation, or practical action. The pattern is especially relevant to beginning AI study, where continued speculation can prevent a person from starting to learn. It contrasts with steady progress and the principle of acting on correct ideas. The concept also explains why people may possess [[Advanced Tools|advanced tools]] or useful advice yet fail to improve their outcomes.
