# Metoclopramide

## Description

Metoclopramide is an antiemetic medication whose anti-dopaminergic activity can produce [[concepts/extrapyramidal-symptoms|extrapyramidal symptoms]] and other medication-induced movement disorders. Its adverse effects demonstrate that [[concepts/extrapyramidal-symptoms|extrapyramidal symptoms]] are not limited to psychiatric treatments such as [[concepts/typical-antipsychotic|typical antipsychotics]] or [[concepts/atypical-antipsychotic|atypical antipsychotics]]. Reported manifestations may include [[concepts/dystonia|dystonia]], [[concepts/akathisia|akathisia]], [[concepts/parkinsonism|parkinsonism]], [[entities/rigidity|rigidity]], [[entities/bradykinesia|bradykinesia]], and [[entities/tremor|tremor]]. The source cites a case report specifically addressing metoclopramide and extrapyramidal symptoms, making it relevant to [[concepts/drug-induced-extrapyramidal-toxicity|drug-induced extrapyramidal toxicity]].
