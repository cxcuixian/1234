# Procyclidine

## Description

Procyclidine is an [[concepts/anticholinergic-medications|anticholinergic medication]] used to reverse certain [[concepts/extrapyramidal-symptoms|extrapyramidal symptoms]], particularly [[concepts/dystonia|acute dystonia]] and [[concepts/parkinsonism|pseudoparkinsonism]]. It is grouped with [[entities/benztropine|benztropine]], [[entities/diphenhydramine|diphenhydramine]], and [[entities/trihexyphenidyl|trihexyphenidyl]] as an example of anticholinergic treatment. Its effects may address symptoms such as [[entities/rigidity|rigidity]], [[entities/bradykinesia|bradykinesia]], and [[entities/tremor|tremor]] associated with drug-induced parkinsonism. Anticholinergic medications are generally not helpful for [[concepts/akathisia|akathisia]], so the usefulness of procyclidine depends on the specific extrapyramidal syndrome.
