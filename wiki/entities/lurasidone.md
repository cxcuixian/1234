# Lurasidone

## Description

Lurasidone is a second-generation [[concepts/atypical-antipsychotic|atypical antipsychotic]] associated in the source with a comparatively higher likelihood of causing [[concepts/akathisia|akathisia]]. It is discussed alongside [[entities/aripiprazole|Aripiprazole]] in the context of treatment-emergent restlessness. When akathisia develops, switching to an antipsychotic with a lower akathisia risk may improve symptoms. The source does not provide a broader pharmacological or therapeutic profile of Lurasidone.
