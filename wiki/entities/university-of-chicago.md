# University of Chicago

## Description

The [[entities/university-of-chicago|University of Chicago]] is presented as an example of both the strengths and weaknesses of academic economics. [[entities/charles-t-munger|Charles T. Munger]] criticizes its historical emphasis on rational-agent models and efficient-market theory, while praising its willingness to appoint [[entities/richard-thaler|Richard Thaler]], whose work incorporates psychological irrationality into economics. The source also notes that the university once abolished its psychology department, an unusual institutional attempt to correct perceived deficiencies. Overall, the University of Chicago symbolizes the possibility of academic self-correction through criticism and intellectual openness.
