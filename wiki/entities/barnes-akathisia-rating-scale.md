# Barnes Akathisia Rating Scale

## Description

The Barnes Akathisia Rating Scale (BARS) is a clinical instrument used to assess the severity of [[concepts/akathisia|akathisia]] and related movement symptoms. It is one of four frequently used scales for evaluating [[concepts/extrapyramidal-symptoms|extrapyramidal symptoms]], alongside the [[entities/simpson-angus-scale|Simpson-Angus Scale]], [[entities/abnormal-involuntary-movement-scale|Abnormal Involuntary Movement Scale]], and [[entities/extrapyramidal-symptom-rating-scale|Extrapyramidal Symptom Rating Scale]]. The scale supports clinical judgment rather than functioning as a weighted diagnostic tool. Its results can help clinicians balance medication benefits against adverse-effect distress and decide whether to maintain, reduce, or discontinue a causative medication.
