# Fluoxetine

## Description

Fluoxetine is an antidepressant medication associated in the source with the induction of [[Extrapyramidal symptoms]]. It appears alongside [[Duloxetine]], [[Sertraline]], [[Escitalopram]], and [[Bupropion]] as a specific antidepressant supporting the broader observation that antidepressant treatment can produce extrapyramidal symptoms. The source frames these symptoms primarily as medication-induced manifestations in psychiatric practice while noting that non-medication causes, including [[Meningitis]], may also occur. It does not provide a specific mechanism, frequency, or preferred intervention for fluoxetine-related symptoms.
