# Mr. Market

## Description

Mr. Market is [[entities/benjamin-graham|Benjamin Graham’s]] personification of the stock market as an emotionally unstable business partner. He alternates between offering securities at prices far below their perceived value and offering to buy them at excessive prices. Investors are not required to follow his moods; they may buy, sell, or do nothing according to independent judgment. In [[sources/poor-charlies-almanack_38aa4e|Poor Charlie’s Almanack]], [[entities/charles-t-munger|Charles T. Munger]] connects the analogy to rational valuation, emotional discipline, and the limitations of efficient-market theory.
