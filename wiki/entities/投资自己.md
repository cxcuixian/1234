# Invest in Yourself

## Description

Invest in Yourself means directing time, money, and effort toward improving one’s own capabilities. The source recommends this approach in the context of Hong Kong’s high costs, limited development of new industries, and the need to increase [[entities/生产力|productivity]]. It is also discussed alongside historical reform and the claim that AI cannot yet completely replace people. The idea is presented as a practical response to structural uncertainty and technological change, related to [[concepts/合理化|rationalization]].
