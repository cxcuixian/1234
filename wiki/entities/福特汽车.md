# Ford Motor Company

## Description

Ford Motor Company is the automobile company used in the source’s historical investment example. The source characterizes the automobile business as capital-intensive and unprofitable, with frequent labor strikes and an inadequate competitive [[economic moat]]. The example is analytical rather than biographical: it illustrates why a familiar business may nevertheless be unattractive as an investment. The judgment is associated with [[Warren Buffett]] and related investment analysis.
