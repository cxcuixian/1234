# John Kenneth Galbraith

## Description

John Kenneth Galbraith was an economist whose analysis of the [[concepts/bezzle|bezzle]] became an important foundation for [[concepts/febezzlement|Febezzlement]], a concept developed by [[entities/charles-t-munger|Charles T. Munger]]. Galbraith defined the bezzle as undisclosed embezzlement that temporarily increases spending: the thief gains purchasing power while the victim continues to act as though the assets remain intact. Munger extended this logic to institutional investment fees and other wealth transfers that remain psychologically hidden, particularly during rising markets. His work connects accounting, incentives, wealth effects, and macroeconomic behavior with [[entities/behavioral-economics|Behavioral Economics]] and [[concepts/incentive-caused-bias|Incentive-Caused Bias]].
