# Jared Diamond

## Description

Jared Diamond is a prominent multidisciplinary scholar who applies evolutionary biology and other scientific models to geography, ecology, and the development or collapse of societies. His work demonstrates how concepts can be synthesized across academic boundaries, providing a counterexample to narrow and fragmented approaches to soft-science education. His books, including *Guns, Germs, and Steel* and *Collapse*, are included among [[entities/charles-t-munger|Charles T. Munger]]’s recommended reading. Diamond’s interdisciplinary approach is connected to [[concepts/multiple-mental-models|Multiple Mental Models]] and [[concepts/worldly-wisdom|Worldly Wisdom]].
