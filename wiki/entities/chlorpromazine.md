# Chlorpromazine

## Description

Chlorpromazine is a conventional, or [[concepts/typical-antipsychotic|typical antipsychotic]], medication associated with a comparatively higher risk of [[concepts/extrapyramidal-symptoms|extrapyramidal symptoms]] than many [[concepts/atypical-antipsychotic|atypical antipsychotics]]. Its movement-related adverse effects are linked primarily to [[concepts/dopamine-d2-receptor-antagonism|dopamine D2 receptor antagonism]] and disruption of [[concepts/dopaminergic-neurotransmission|dopaminergic neurotransmission]] in the [[concepts/nigrostriatal-pathway|nigrostriatal pathway]]. The source mentions chlorpromazine alongside [[entities/haloperidol|Haloperidol]] as an example of a conventional antipsychotic, rather than treating it as the article’s primary subject. Its associated adverse effects may include [[concepts/dystonia|dystonia]], [[concepts/akathisia|akathisia]], [[concepts/parkinsonism|parkinsonism]], and [[concepts/tardive-dyskinesia|tardive dyskinesia]], which may be evaluated with [[concepts/rating-scales-for-extrapyramidal-symptoms|rating scales for extrapyramidal symptoms]].
