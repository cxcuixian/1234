# Benjamin Franklin

## Description

Benjamin Franklin is presented in [[entities/poor-charlies-almanack|Poor Charlie's Almanack]] as [[entities/charles-t-munger|Charles T. Munger]]'s most important historical model and personal hero. A largely self-taught polymath, Franklin is associated with curiosity, hard work, preparation, patience, discipline, objectivity, public service, and financial independence. Munger repeatedly compares his own habits and values with Franklin's, particularly the commitment to [[concepts/lifelong-learning|lifelong learning]], [[concepts/worldly-wisdom|worldly wisdom]], and using wealth to serve society. Franklin's [[entities/poor-richards-almanack|Poor Richard's Almanack]] provided the literary and structural model for [[entities/poor-charlies-almanack|Poor Charlie's Almanack]], and his life is used as evidence that ethical conduct, practical wisdom, and broad intellectual interests can reinforce one another over long periods.
