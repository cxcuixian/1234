# CATIE trial

## Description

The CATIE trial, or Clinical Antipsychotic Trials for Intervention Effectiveness, was a large clinical trial evaluating the effectiveness and tolerability of antipsychotic drugs. It included 1,460 randomized subjects, of whom 213 discontinued participation. Fifty-eight discontinuations, representing 27.2% of those who dropped out, were attributed to [[Extrapyramidal symptoms|extrapyramidal symptoms]] (EPS). The findings connected adverse movement effects with treatment persistence and the practical evaluation of [[Antipsychotic|antipsychotic]] effectiveness, including comparisons involving [[Typical antipsychotic|typical antipsychotics]] and [[Atypical antipsychotic|atypical antipsychotics]].
