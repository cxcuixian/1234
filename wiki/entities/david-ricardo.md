# David Ricardo

## Description

David Ricardo was an English economist whose principle of [[entities/comparative-advantage|comparative advantage]] is presented as a foundational model for understanding trade and specialization. His model shows that countries can benefit from trade even when one country can produce every good more cheaply, because relative production costs differ. [[entities/charles-t-munger|Charles T. Munger]] extends the model beyond international trade to managerial delegation and organizational design. Ricardo’s model is paired with [[entities/adam-smith|Adam Smith]]’s pin factory to illustrate how specialization can arise through deliberate planning or decentralized exchange.
