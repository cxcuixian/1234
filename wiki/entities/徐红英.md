# Xu Hongying

## Description

Xu Hongying is described as a person who acquired wealth beyond her capabilities or financial understanding. The source states that she later became a defaulter, presenting her experience as an example of the difficulty of converting luck-based wealth into durable financial competence. Her story is associated with warnings about extravagance, poor self-management, and the challenge of preserving wealth. It supports the broader concept that [[concepts/财富守住比赚钱更难|preserving wealth can be harder than earning it]].
