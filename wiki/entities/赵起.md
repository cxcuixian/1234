# Zhao Qi

## Description

Zhao Qi is presented negatively as an example of attempting technical work without understanding its underlying principles. The source characterizes this behavior as forcing progress and becoming trapped in the problem rather than developing genuine competence. The recommended alternative is to fully understand one direction before starting another, reflecting a preference for principle-based learning, focused execution, and results over superficial activity. Zhao Qi is discussed in relation to [[entities/abaqus|ABAQUS]], [[entities/孙宇晨|孙宇晨]], and [[entities/chen-junjie|陈俊洁]].
