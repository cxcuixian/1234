# 人工智能认知放大

## Description

人工智能认知放大 describes the source’s claim that AI and other advanced tools magnify the assumptions, knowledge, and limitations already present in the user. Rather than guaranteeing better reasoning, these tools may give old beliefs a polished or authoritative appearance. The source therefore treats [[concepts/提问质量|提问质量]] and self-awareness as prerequisites for useful AI interaction. This phenomenon is connected to [[entities/先进工具|先进工具]] and to the recommendation to consider which questions are reasonable from AI’s perspective.
