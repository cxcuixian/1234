# 资本主义

## Description

资本主义 is discussed as a social and economic system shaped by [[concepts/生产方式|生产方式]], [[concepts/劳动|劳动]], [[concepts/分工|分工]], consumption, and [[concepts/市场|市场]]. The source places it within a historical progression from hunting and pastoral life through agriculture and commerce, connecting its development with [[concepts/社会形态|社会形态]] and the organization of modern enterprises. It also relates capitalism to self-interest, greed, monopoly, government, public works, [[concepts/资源稀缺|资源稀缺]], and the scale of economic organization. These relationships are presented as part of an effort to understand how material production shapes social institutions, values, and forms of collective life.
