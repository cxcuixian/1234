# Pari-Mutuel System

## Description

The pari-mutuel system is a wagering market in which participants bet against one another, with payout odds changing according to the distribution of bets. [[Charles T. Munger]] uses it as an analogy for the stock market, where prices reflect the judgments and actions of many participants. The analogy suggests that markets may be broadly efficient while still permitting occasional mispricing, especially when [[Behavioral Economics]] and other psychological influences affect market participants. It also supports Munger’s preference for acting rarely, waiting for unusually favorable odds, and investing heavily when a genuine [[Margin of Safety]] and [[Circle of Competence]] are present.
