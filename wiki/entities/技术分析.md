# Technical Analysis

## Description

Technical analysis is treated skeptically as a method for interpreting market movements. The source argues that it can become useless or misleading during market declines, especially for stocks that fall in both rising and falling market conditions. This is a broad critique rather than an explanation of particular indicators or chart patterns. The source instead favors directly assessing current conditions, business quality, management credibility, and downside risk, consistent with the [[entities/current-priority-principle|Current Priority Principle]]. Its limitations are illustrated through comparisons involving [[entities/中国巨石|China Jushi]], [[entities/huaneng-liaodian|Huaneng Liaodian]], [[entities/散户|Retail Investors]], and [[垃圾股|Junk Stocks]].
