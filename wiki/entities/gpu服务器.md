# GPU服务器

## Description

GPU服务器 refers to servers equipped with graphics processing units for intensive computational workloads. In the source, they are presented as part of the infrastructure required by data centers in the artificial-intelligence economy. Their viability depends not only on computing demand, but also on optical modules, liquid cooling, electricity generation, and grid regulation. This infrastructure chain highlights the physical costs that institutions and speculative investors may overlook when assessing high-technology expansion.
