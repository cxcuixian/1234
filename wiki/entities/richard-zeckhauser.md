# Richard Zeckhauser

## Description

Richard Zeckhauser is presented as a Harvard professor and exceptional bridge player whose decision-evaluation method strongly influenced [[Charles T. Munger|Charles T. Munger’s]] approach to investing. Zeckhauser emphasizes playing the hand correctly rather than judging oneself solely by the outcome, connecting this attitude to disciplined preparation, probability assessment, and acceptance that some results are outside one’s control. The source uses bridge as a practical model for forward and backward reasoning in investment and life decisions. His ideas are associated with [[Analytic Rigor|analytic rigor]], [[Inversion|inversion]], [[Circle of Competence|circle of competence]], and [[Multiple Mental Models|multiple mental models]].
