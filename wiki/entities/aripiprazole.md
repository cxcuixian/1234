# Aripiprazole

## Description

Aripiprazole is an [[Atypical antipsychotic|atypical antipsychotic]] and a partial agonist at [[dopamine D <sub>2</sub> receptors|dopamine D2 receptors]]. The source identifies it as a treatment option for [[Extrapyramidal symptoms|extrapyramidal symptoms]] caused by antipsychotic medication, particularly when reducing the causative medication dose or switching medications is appropriate. Compared with [[Typical antipsychotic|typical antipsychotics]], which strongly antagonize dopamine D2 receptors, aripiprazole has a distinct pharmacological profile. However, it has a comparatively elevated risk of [[Akathisia|akathisia]]; if akathisia develops, switching to an antipsychotic with a lower risk may be preferable.
