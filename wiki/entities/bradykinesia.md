# Bradykinesia

## Description

Bradykinesia is slowness of movement and a characteristic parkinsonian symptom within [[concepts/extrapyramidal-syndromes|extrapyramidal syndromes]]. It commonly appears alongside [[entities/rigidity|Rigidity]] and [[entities/tremor|Tremor]] in descriptions of [[concepts/parkinsonism|Parkinsonism]] and [[concepts/parkinsonism|Pseudoparkinsonism]]. In [[concepts/parkinsons-disease|Parkinson's disease]], bradykinesia is associated with the loss of dopaminergic neurons in the substantia nigra, disruption of the [[concepts/nigrostriatal-pathway|Nigrostriatal pathway]], and dysregulation of [[concepts/extrapyramidal-system|Extrapyramidal system]] motor control. Medication-induced bradykinesia may form part of [[concepts/extrapyramidal-symptoms|Extrapyramidal symptoms]] and can be managed by modifying the causative medication when clinically appropriate.
