# 香港

## Description

香港 is a place and economy shaped by trade, migration, high prices, limited industrial renewal, and financial crime. Its economic position is discussed in relation to [[concepts/转口贸易|转口贸易]], China–United States trade, the Middle Eastern war, immigration, and money laundering. The source suggests that money laundering contributes to a high economic floor while insufficient development of new industries limits further growth.
