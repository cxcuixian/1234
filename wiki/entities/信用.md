# 信用

## Description

信用 refers to trustworthiness, credibility, and the expectation that individuals or organizations will honor their commitments. In the source, it is used normatively to criticize companies that allegedly lack credibility and therefore have no goodwill. The concept is connected to [[entities/国企|国企]], [[entities/内幕交易|内幕交易]], and [[entities/电子垃圾|电子垃圾]] through questions of corporate reputation, ethical conduct, and institutional reliability. 信用 is presented as a foundation of business legitimacy and public trust.
