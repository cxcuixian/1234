# Munger

## Description

Munger is presented as a major intellectual influence on the source’s views about simplicity, happiness, and practical judgment. The notes attribute to him the idea that many things can support a happy life when a person is capable of carrying them out. He is also associated with the principle that simple methods are often the most difficult to execute properly. His ideas are connected to avoiding excessive effort, rejecting wasteful models, and focusing on practical implementation rather than slogans. In the source, Munger serves as a reference point for disciplined thinking and learning from outstanding people.
