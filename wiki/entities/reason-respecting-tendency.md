# Reason-Respecting Tendency

## Description

Reason-Respecting Tendency is the human inclination to comply more readily when an instruction or request is accompanied by a reason. [[entities/charles-t-munger|Charles T. Munger]] connects this tendency to the practical communication rule that people should be told who will do what, where, when, and why. The tendency is useful when the stated reasons are accurate and clarifying, but it can also be exploited through meaningless or false explanations. In this sense, it functions both as a learning principle and as a vulnerability in persuasion, alongside [[concepts/reciprocation-tendency|Reciprocation Tendency]], [[concepts/incentive-caused-bias|Incentive-Caused Bias]], and [[entities/robert-b-cialdini|Robert B. Cialdini]]’s work on influence.
