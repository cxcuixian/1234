# Robert B. Cialdini

## Description

Robert B. Cialdini is a psychologist whose research made social influence and persuasion more accessible to a broad audience, particularly through his book *Influence*. [[Charles T. Munger]] credits Cialdini’s work with helping him understand how people can be manipulated through psychological tendencies, including [[Reciprocation Tendency]] and [[Social-Proof Tendency]]. The source highlights Cialdini’s reciprocity experiment, in which reducing a large request to a smaller one substantially increased compliance. His work also illustrates that knowledge of persuasion can be applied ethically or exploited by salespeople.
