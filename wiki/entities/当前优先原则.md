# 当前优先原则

## Description

当前优先原则 is the practice of using present conditions as the primary basis for judgment rather than relying heavily on the past or the future. In the investment context, it favors observing the lived performance of individual stocks, such as [[entities/中国巨石|中国巨石]] and [[entities/华能辽电|华能辽电]], instead of trusting broad indices or historical reputations. The principle emphasizes direct observation of current reality over forecasts, abstract market narratives, and [[entities/技术分析|技术分析]]. It also supports responding to actual downside conditions through [[entities/卖出与风险退出|卖出与风险退出]] and is related to the divergence between index performance and individual stocks described by [[concepts/指数与个股背离|指数与个股背离]].
