# Authority-Misinfluence Tendency

## Description

Authority-Misinfluence Tendency is the tendency to follow leaders, experts, or other authority figures even when their instructions or judgments are mistaken. [[Charles T. Munger]] connects this tendency to human dominance hierarchies and describes how it can produce tragic errors in aviation, medicine, business, and social experiments. The experiments of [[Stanley Milgram]] demonstrate that ordinary people may perform harmful actions when directed by an apparent authority. Munger recommends training, independent judgment, and conscious awareness of authority-induced error as safeguards, supported by [[Checklist]], [[Multiple Mental Models]], and [[Circle of Competence]].
