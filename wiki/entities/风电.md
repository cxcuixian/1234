# Wind Power

## Description

Wind power is discussed as an energy source whose output is unstable and intermittent. In contrast, [[entities/核电|Nuclear power]] is associated with more stable operation, highlighting the need for [[entities/储能|energy storage]] and [[entities/电网调节|grid regulation]]. Wind power is therefore treated as part of an integrated energy system rather than as a standalone solution. Its reliability characteristics are especially relevant to the electricity demands of [[entities/数据中心|data centers]] and other high-tech infrastructure.
