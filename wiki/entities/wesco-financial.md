# Wesco Financial

## Description

Wesco Financial was a holding company chaired by [[entities/charles-t-munger|Charles T. Munger]] and associated with [[entities/berkshire-hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]]. It served as a vehicle through which Munger discussed and applied concentrated, low-turnover investing, with particular attention to business quality, accounting, insurance, and corporate governance. Munger frequently contrasted Wesco’s substantial cash position and conservative culture with more complex institutional investment practices. Its annual meetings also provided an important forum for Munger’s unscripted commentary on investing, human behavior, and [[concepts/worldly-wisdom|worldly wisdom]].

Wesco Financial was structured similarly to [[entities/berkshire-hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]], with Berkshire Hathaway owning 80.1 percent of the company.
