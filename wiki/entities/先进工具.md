# 先进工具

## Description

先进工具 refers to powerful technologies, particularly AI-related tools, that extend a user’s ability to ask questions and process information. Like [[entities/bo-shi-dun-dong-li|波士顿动力]] and other advanced technologies, these tools can expand practical capabilities without automatically improving judgment or strategic perspective. They may amplify existing cognition and give established beliefs an appearance of sophistication, a risk also associated with [[entities/ren-gong-zhi-neng-ren-zhi-fang-da|人工智能认知放大]] and [[entities/xiang-de-tai-duo-zuo-de-tai-shao|想得太多做得太少]]. A recommended corrective is to examine which questions are reasonable from the tool’s own operating perspective, rather than using it merely to confirm prior assumptions.
