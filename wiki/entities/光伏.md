# Photovoltaic Power

## Description

Photovoltaic power, also known as 光伏, is a source of electricity generated from sunlight. Its generation stops at night, making it less continuously available than generation from sources such as [[Nuclear power|核电]]. This limitation distinguishes photovoltaic power from more stable generation and creates a need for complementary [[Energy storage|储能]] and [[Grid regulation|电网调节]] when it serves high-demand infrastructure such as [[Data center|数据中心]]. It is part of the broader comparison of energy technologies relevant to [[Power plant|电厂]] and high-tech investment decisions.
