# 奥氮平

## Description

奥氮平 is an antipsychotic medication used in the treatment of schizophrenia and for sustained pharmacological management. The source discusses it as an example of why chemical and medical knowledge is useful when evaluating psychiatric medication. Associated adverse effects and monitoring concerns include agranulocytosis, prolactin changes, increased appetite, slowed metabolism, and metabolic syndrome.
