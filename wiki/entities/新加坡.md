# Singapore

## Description

Singapore is presented as a national case study of institutional effectiveness and long-term development. The source associates it with rational leadership, optimal resource allocation, high efficiency, bilingual education, preservation of native-language culture, a professional civil service, and port infrastructure. Its development is described as combining international integration with cultural continuity rather than relying on a single policy. Singapore is directly linked to [[李光耀|Lee Kuan Yew]] and to the concepts of [[长期主义|long-termism]], [[资源最优配置|optimal resource allocation]], [[教育|education]], and [[正确的激励方式|appropriate incentive design]].
