# Haloperidol

## Description

Haloperidol is a typical antipsychotic medication and a dopamine D2 receptor antagonist. It is identified as one of the drugs most commonly associated with [[concepts/extrapyramidal-symptoms|extrapyramidal symptoms]], reflecting the relationship between conventional antipsychotic treatment and disruption of [[concepts/dopaminergic-neurotransmission|dopaminergic neurotransmission]]. The source contrasts [[concepts/typical-antipsychotic|typical antipsychotics]] such as haloperidol with [[concepts/atypical-antipsychotic|atypical antipsychotics]], which are generally associated with fewer extrapyramidal side effects. Haloperidol is also discussed in relation to [[concepts/dopamine-d2-receptor-antagonism|dopamine D2 receptor antagonism]], the [[concepts/nigrostriatal-pathway|nigrostriatal pathway]], and [[concepts/movement-disorder|movement disorders]].
