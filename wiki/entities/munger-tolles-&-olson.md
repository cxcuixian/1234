# Munger Tolles & Olson

## Description

Munger Tolles & Olson is a Los Angeles law firm founded in 1962 by [[entities/charles-t-munger|Charles T. Munger]] and several colleagues. The firm evolved through several names, and [[entities/charles-t-munger|Charles T. Munger]] left its active partnership in 1965 while retaining a lasting influence on its culture and principles. Those principles included choosing clients as one would choose friends, declining to pursue every last dollar, and maintaining a significant pro bono commitment to impoverished and disadvantaged communities. The firm represents an important transition between [[entities/charles-t-munger|Charles T. Munger]]’s legal career and his later focus on investment, [[concepts/financial-independence|Financial Independence]], and [[concepts/worldly-wisdom|Worldly Wisdom]].
