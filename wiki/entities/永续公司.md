# Perpetual Company

## Description

A perpetual company is a business capable of maintaining its economic value and operations over a very long period. The concept emphasizes carefully studying and acquiring genuinely sustainable companies, placing durability and business quality at the center of a long-term investment approach. It contrasts with short-term trading and is related to [[entities/investment-in-oneself|Investment in Oneself]] as part of a broader philosophy of long-term value creation.
