# Valuable People

## Description

**Valuable People** refers to individuals whose abilities, character, or contributions make them meaningfully useful or important. The associated note expresses confidence that such people will eventually be recognized, even if their value is not immediately visible. This idea supports a long-term view of personal development and suggests that genuine value can outlast temporary obscurity. Recognizing valuable people may contribute to building [[entities/stable-teams|stable teams]], while contrasting with the frustration described by [[concepts/given-an-opportunity-but-unable-to-use-it|given an opportunity but unable to use it]].
