# Meningitis

## Description

Meningitis is identified as a non-medication-related cause that can produce [[concepts/extrapyramidal-symptoms|extrapyramidal symptoms]]. Alongside [[entities/brain-damage|brain damage]], it represents an alternative explanation for extrapyramidal presentations that might otherwise be attributed to medication effects. In psychiatric usage, the term generally refers to medication-induced symptoms, making meningitis an important differential context rather than the usual implied cause.
