# Cicero

## Description

Cicero, also known as Marcus Tullius Cicero, was a Roman statesman, orator, philosopher, and writer whose ideas strongly influenced [[entities/charles-t-munger|Charles T. Munger]]'s reflections on aging, civic duty, philosophy, and continued learning. Munger discusses Cicero's discourse on old age and [[entities/benjamin-franklin|Benjamin Franklin]]'s translation of it as an example of how later life can remain productive, honorable, and intellectually rich. Cicero's political thought is presented as a combination of limited democracy, oligarchy, temporary executive authority, and institutional checks and balances grounded in skepticism about human nature. His emphasis on philosophy, self-improvement, public service, and a well-spent life also illustrates how the preserved work of the dead can continue educating and influencing later generations.
