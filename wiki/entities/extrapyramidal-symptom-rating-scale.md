# Extrapyramidal Symptom Rating Scale

## Description

The Extrapyramidal Symptom Rating Scale is a clinical rating instrument used to evaluate the severity of [[concepts/extrapyramidal-symptoms|extrapyramidal symptoms]] and other [[concepts/movement-disorder|movement disorders]]. It is listed alongside the [[entities/simpson-angus-scale|Simpson-Angus Scale]], [[entities/barnes-akathisia-rating-scale|Barnes Akathisia Rating Scale]], and [[entities/abnormal-involuntary-movement-scale|Abnormal Involuntary Movement Scale]] as a frequently used assessment tool. These instruments are not weighted for diagnostic purposes, but they help clinicians quantify adverse-effect burden and may inform decisions about maintaining, reducing, or discontinuing a medication thought to be responsible. The scale may therefore support assessment of symptoms such as [[entities/rigidity|rigidity]], [[entities/bradykinesia|bradykinesia]], and [[entities/tremor|tremor]] during medication treatment, including treatment involving [[entities/haloperidol|Haloperidol]] or other antipsychotic medicines.
