# Financial discretion

## Description

Financial discretion is the norm of concealing or not publicly displaying personal wealth. In the source note, it appears alongside the claim that moral character is more important than making money, suggesting that wealth should not become a public identity or a source of unnecessary exposure. The principle concerns restraint, privacy, and the presentation of success, rather than financial management alone. It contrasts with conspicuous status-seeking and complements the emphasis on character and durable relationships.
