# 宁德时代

## Description

宁德时代 (CATL) is presented as an example of a company whose competitive moat may be weaker than commonly assumed. The source characterizes its market environment as a gradual and potentially dangerous deterioration, while explicitly questioning the strength of its [[concepts/护城河|competitive moat]]. Its high profitability may encourage customers to shift toward a cheaper second-ranked competitor such as [[entities/欣旺达|欣旺达]]. The company is therefore relevant to [[concepts/景气行业|industry cycles]], changing investor expectations, and the [[concepts/温水煮青蛙|boiling-frog effect]].
