# 散户

## Description

散户 refers to individual retail investors and is the primary behavioral subject of the source’s investment commentary. They are portrayed as prone to hesitation, fear, weak holding discipline, and treating unfavorable signals as reasons to exit, in contrast with disciplined investors who understand what they own and act decisively. The source suggests that散户 may achieve temporary gains in [[垃圾股|垃圾股]], but may eventually give those gains back because they do not understand the underlying investment. The commentary discusses their relationship to [[entities/中国巨石|中国巨石]], [[entities/华能辽电|华能辽电]], [[entities/星宇股份|星宇股份]], and [[entities/卖出与风险退出|卖出与风险退出]], with [[concepts/持有纪律|持有纪律]] presented as a contrasting principle.

Retail investors are distinguished from large institutional or controlling market participants and are described as repeatedly being exploited by the market-maker associated with [[entities/华能辽电|华能辽电]], illustrating their vulnerability to market-power imbalances and behavioral exploitation. The source also portrays them as particularly vulnerable when many participants seek to buy dips in low-opening-price situations.
