# 巴菲特

## Description

巴菲特 is presented as an investment thinker whose principles are widely recognized but poorly followed by ordinary investors. His example illustrates the difference between intellectually accepting a correct idea and acting on it consistently. He is associated with buying understandable, high-quality businesses, including [[entities/kai-shi-ke|开市客]], while avoiding decisions driven by excessive speculation or luck. His significance in the source is methodological rather than biographical: good judgment requires independent understanding, disciplined behavior, and [[concepts/du-li-si-kao|独立思考]]. His ideas are also connected to [[concepts/you-shi-lei-ji|优势累积]], [[concepts/bi-fu-de-li-liang|复利的力量]], and [[concepts/bi-sha-neng-li|避蠢能力]].
