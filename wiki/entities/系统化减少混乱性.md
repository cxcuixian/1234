# Systematic Reduction of Chaos

## Description

Systematic Reduction of Chaos is a practical principle for organizing behavior and reducing uncertainty. It is grounded in the claimed human preference for regular, orderly conditions, while emphasizing that a correct method must be maintained consistently. The principle applies beyond investing as a general discipline for decision-making and daily conduct. It is connected with learning through action, evaluating the present situation, and acting instead of deliberating indefinitely. It is closely related to [[entities/想得太多做得太少|Overthinking and Underacting]].
