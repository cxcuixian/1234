# 承认自己无知

## Description

承认自己无知 is presented as a mark of intelligence and intellectual honesty. It contrasts with [[entities/知错不改|知错不改]], in which a person recognizes an error but refuses to change. Acknowledging the limits of one’s knowledge enables faster correction, learning, and self-correction. It therefore complements [[concepts/理性|理性]] and [[concepts/终身学习|终身学习]].
