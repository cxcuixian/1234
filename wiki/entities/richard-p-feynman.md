# Richard P. Feynman

## Description

Richard P. Feynman is presented as a model of scientific reasoning, practical investigation, and causal explanation. In [[sources/poor-charlies-almanack_38aa4e|Poor Charlie's Almanack]], [[Charles T. Munger]] uses Feynman's analysis of the [[Challenger Space Shuttle]] disaster to illustrate how empirical testing and attention to physical mechanisms can identify the failure of cold-affected rubber O-rings. Feynman's example supports the importance of [[Analytic Rigor]], [[Multiple Mental Models]], fundamental knowledge, and a well-defined [[Circle of Competence]]. He is also associated with the principle that one must not fool oneself, because self-deception is a central obstacle to sound reasoning.
