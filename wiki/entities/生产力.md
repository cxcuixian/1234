# 生产力

## Description

生产力 refers to the capacity to produce useful output efficiently through human skills, tools, organization, and technology. The source argues that investing in oneself can significantly increase productivity, while also noting limitations in current AI-generated code and the continuing importance of human labor. It places productivity at the intersection of personal development, industrial modernization, and AI adoption. The source presents productivity growth as a more important objective than merely reducing costs. It is closely related to [[entities/投资自己|投资自己]] and the concept of [[concepts/合理化|合理化]].
