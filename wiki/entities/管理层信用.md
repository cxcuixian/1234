# Management Credibility

## Description

Management Credibility refers to the perceived trustworthiness, integrity, and reliability of a company’s management. When management lacks credibility, [[Retail Investors]] should consider selling without hesitation because governance problems may spread throughout the organization. It functions as a decisive investment filter rather than merely a secondary qualitative consideration. This entity connects corporate behavior with the practical risk-control rule of exiting a position quickly, particularly in relation to [[Starplus]] and [[Selling and Risk Exit]].
