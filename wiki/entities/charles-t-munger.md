# Charles T. Munger

## Description

Charles T. Munger is the central subject, narrator, and intellectual architect of [[sources/poor-charlies-almanack_38aa4e|Poor Charlie's Almanack]]. Born in Omaha in 1924, he studied mathematics and physics, served in the Army Air Corps, graduated magna cum laude from [[entities/harvard-law-school|Harvard Law School]], practiced law, developed real estate projects, managed an investment partnership, and later became the business partner of [[entities/warren-buffett|Warren Buffett]] at [[entities/berkshire-hathaway|Berkshire Hathaway]]. He is portrayed as intensely curious, disciplined, rational, contrarian, humorous, and committed to lifelong learning. His approach combines [[concepts/multiple-mental-models|Multiple Mental Models]], concentrated investing, psychological self-awareness, and strong standards of honesty and reliability. His family memories, professional recollections, speeches, and annual-meeting statements illustrate how these principles shaped both his business decisions and personal conduct.
