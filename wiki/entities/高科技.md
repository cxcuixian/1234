# 高科技

## Description

高科技 refers broadly to advanced technologies and technically sophisticated industries. The source argues that high technology has no inherent boundary, while also emphasizing the need for safety boundaries and reasonable valuation. This creates tension between rapid technological possibility and the practical limits required for safety, investment, and risk management.
