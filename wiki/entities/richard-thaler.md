# Richard Thaler

## Description

Richard Thaler is an economist known for challenging the assumption that human behavior is consistently rational in economic models. His invitation to the [[entities/university-of-chicago|University of Chicago]] is presented as evidence that economics was beginning to incorporate psychological insights through [[entities/behavioral-economics|Behavioral Economics]]. Thaler’s work aligns with [[entities/charles-t-munger|Charles T. Munger’s]] argument that psychological tendencies should be integrated into microeconomics. His example illustrates how academic disciplines can correct themselves through interdisciplinary thinking.
