# Selling and Risk Exit

## Description

Selling and Risk Exit is a practical response to unreliable management, uncontrolled price behavior, and the deterioration of a junk stock. It emphasizes decisive action without hesitation, comparing an uncontrolled investment to a car without brakes from which one should jump before greater harm occurs. The method prioritizes the preservation of capital and life over hope, loyalty, or the desire to recover losses. It is closely associated with [[entities/管理层信用|Management Credibility]], [[垃圾股|Junk Stocks]], and [[concepts/持有纪律|Holding Discipline]], and is illustrated through situations involving [[entities/星宇股份|Xingyu Co., Ltd.]], [[entities/中国巨石|China Jushi]], and [[entities/华能辽电|Huaneng Liaodian]].
