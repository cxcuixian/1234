# Teledyne

## Description

Teledyne is presented in [[entities/poor-charlies-almanack|Poor Charlie's Almanack]] as an example of exceptional operating performance and disciplined [[concepts/capital-allocation|capital allocation]]. Its co-founder and chief executive, [[entities/henry-e-singleton|Henry E. Singleton]], achieved a 20.4 percent compound annual return to shareholders from 1963 to 1990 while repurchasing a large majority of the company's shares. [[entities/charles-t-munger|Charles T. Munger]] uses Teledyne and Singleton to illustrate how technical ability, disciplined capital deployment, and long-term focus can outperform conventional corporate formulas. The example supports the broader idea that a small number of outstanding decisions can dominate long-term results and relates to [[concepts/focus-investing|Focus Investing]], [[concepts/compound-interest|Compound Interest]], and [[concepts/multiple-mental-models|Multiple Mental Models]].
