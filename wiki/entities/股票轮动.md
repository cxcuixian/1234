# 股票轮动

## Description

股票轮动 refers to the movement of capital and investor attention among different stocks or sectors. The source places it within a broader framework involving industry observation, market timing, and changing investment logic, with connections to [[entities/市场定价权|市场定价权]], [[entities/绿电|绿电]], and [[entities/英伟达|英伟达]]. It is contrasted with indiscriminate bottom-fishing and with the belief that the exact stock matters less than entering a profitable opportunity at the right moment. The concept is also associated with dominant companies, [[entities/资本主义|资本主义]], banking stocks, and the difficulty of avoiding losses during sustained market declines.
