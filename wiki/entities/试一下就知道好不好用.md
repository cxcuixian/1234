# Try It to Know Whether It Works Well

## Description

Try It to Know Whether It Works Well expresses a hands-on method for determining whether something is useful or effective. It rejects speculation in favor of direct experimentation and personal experience. The resulting experience is presented as the most genuine evidence available to the evaluator. This method applies broadly to products, tools, processes, and decisions whose qualities can be revealed through actual use. It is closely related to [[concepts/理性|rational evaluation]].
