# Inflation

## Description

Inflation is a sustained increase in the general price level that reduces the purchasing power of money. It makes money less valuable and affects the real value of future cash. The source connects inflation with changes in interest rates, reputation, threats, and practical thinking, placing it within an economic framework relevant to saving and investment.
