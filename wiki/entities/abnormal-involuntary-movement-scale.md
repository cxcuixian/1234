# Abnormal Involuntary Movement Scale

## Description

The Abnormal Involuntary Movement Scale, commonly abbreviated [[entities/abnormal-involuntary-movement-scale|AIMS]], is a standardized rating scale for assessing the severity of involuntary movements associated with [[concepts/extrapyramidal-symptoms|extrapyramidal symptoms]]. It is among the frequently used scales for measuring movement-disorder severity and is not weighted for diagnostic purposes. The scale is particularly relevant to assessing movements associated with [[concepts/tardive-dyskinesia|tardive dyskinesia]], although its use is not limited to that condition. Findings from the scale may inform decisions about continuing, reducing, or stopping a causative medication, including [[entities/haloperidol|Haloperidol]], [[entities/fluphenazine|Fluphenazine]], or [[entities/metoclopramide|Metoclopramide]].
