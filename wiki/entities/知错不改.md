# 知错不改

## Description

知错不改 describes recognizing an error while refusing to change one’s behavior, judgment, or conclusion. It is presented as an especially serious form of foolishness because awareness has little value without corrective action. The idea contrasts with [[entities/承认自己无知|承认自己无知]], which supports learning, adaptation, and intellectual improvement. It therefore refers to a failure of reflection to produce behavioral change and is relevant to [[concepts/理性|理性]] and [[concepts/终身学习|终身学习]].
