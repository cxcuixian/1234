# Tetrabenazine

## Description

Tetrabenazine is a medication used to treat persistent [[concepts/tardive-dyskinesia|tardive dyskinesia]] when other measures fail or are not feasible. It inhibits [[concepts/vesicular-monoamine-transporter-2|vesicular monoamine transporter 2]], thereby modulating monoamine storage and release. The source presents it alongside [[entities/deutetrabenazine|Deutetrabenazine]] as a pharmacological option for condition-specific treatment rather than as a general treatment for all [[concepts/extrapyramidal-symptoms|extrapyramidal symptoms]]. Its use is discussed in relation to movement disorders and the assessment of symptoms with [[entities/abnormal-involuntary-movement-scale|Abnormal Involuntary Movement Scale]].
