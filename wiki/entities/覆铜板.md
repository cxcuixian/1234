# Copper-clad laminate

## Description

Copper-clad laminate is a foundational material used in printed circuit board production. It is positioned after substrate materials and before [[entities/photosensitive-film|photosensitive film]], masks, developing chemicals, etching, and PCB fabrication, forming part of a manufacturing process chain in which conductive copper layers are patterned through imaging and chemical processing. Its importance is associated with [[concepts/manufacturing-cost-and-yield|manufacturing cost and yield]], [[concepts/routing-density|routing density]], and the comparison between subtractive and semi-additive processes. It is also relevant to [[concepts/pcb|PCB]], [[concepts/2-5d-packaging|2.5D packaging]], and [[concepts/supply-chain|the industrial chain]].
