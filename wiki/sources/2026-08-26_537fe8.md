## Core Content

This daily note presents a compact philosophy of character, wealth, teamwork, recognition, and reasoning. It places [[entities/有情有义|loyalty and affection]] above [[concepts/赚钱|making money]], while recommending discretion about personal wealth through [[entities/财不外露|financial discretion]]. The note argues that major accomplishments are difficult to achieve alone and gives priority to [[entities/稳定的团队|stable teams]] over individual self-interest. It also suggests that people with genuine value will eventually be recognized, even when recognition is delayed. The recommendation to use [[entities/反过来想|inversion thinking]] is associated with Charlie Munger and presented as a way to improve understanding and judgment. Overall, the source combines moral conduct, restraint, organizational stability, long-term evaluation, and intellectual discipline. It also expresses frustration that opportunities and advice are ineffective when people lack the ability or willingness to understand and use them.
