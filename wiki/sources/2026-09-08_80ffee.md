## Main Points

- Rational, long-term institutional design should align incentives, education, administration, and infrastructure with desired outcomes.
- Resources and responsibility should be allocated according to performance when this improves overall results.
- AI tools do not eliminate the need for mathematical foundations, hands-on programming, or deep understanding.
- Complete, controllable code is preferred over dependence on imperfect interfaces or informal vibe programming.
- Effective work proceeds through clear requirements, structured workflows, concrete action, and continuous optimization.
- Markets reward demonstrated performance and punish technology claims that lack meaningful results.
